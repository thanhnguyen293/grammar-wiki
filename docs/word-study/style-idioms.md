# Style and idioms

For the same idea, English has an **informal** way to say it (talking to friends, texting) and a **formal** way (job application letters, work emails, writing exams). This lesson is a reference table of common word pairs, tips for formal writing, and idioms people use often in daily life.

## Informal and formal

| Style | When to use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Informal | Talking or texting with friends and family | **Thanks** for your help. **See you** soon! |
| Formal | Letters, work emails, forms, essays | **Thank you** for your assistance. **I look forward to** meeting you. |

- **Hi** Nam, **can you** send me the file?<br>*Chào Nam, gửi mình cái file nhé?* (informal)
- **Dear** Mr Nam, **could you please** send me the file?<br>*Kính gửi anh Nam, anh vui lòng gửi giúp tôi tài liệu được không?* (formal)

### 1. Verbs: phrasal verbs are informal, single verbs are formal

| Informal | Formal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| get | receive | nhận | I **got** your email. → We have **received** your email. |
| ask for | request | yêu cầu, xin | I **asked for** a refund. → I **requested** a refund. |
| help | assist | giúp đỡ | Can I **help** you? → How may I **assist** you? |
| buy | purchase | mua | I **bought** a new laptop. → The company **purchased** new laptops. |
| need | require | cần | You **need** a passport. → A passport is **required**. |
| tell | inform | báo, thông báo | **Tell** me if you're late. → Please **inform** us if you will be late. |
| find out | discover | phát hiện, tìm ra | We **found out** the problem. → We **discovered** the problem. |
| put off | postpone | hoãn | They **put off** the meeting. → The meeting has been **postponed**. |
| go up | increase | tăng | Prices **went up** again. → Prices **increased** again. |
| give back | return | trả lại | I'll **give** it **back** tomorrow. → I will **return** it tomorrow. |
| look into | investigate | điều tra, xem xét | We'll **look into** it. → We will **investigate** the matter. |
| try | attempt | cố gắng, thử | I'll **try** to call him. → I will **attempt** to contact him. |

- **Informal:** I'll **find out** what happened and **tell** you.<br>*Mình sẽ tìm hiểu chuyện gì xảy ra rồi báo cậu.*
- **Formal:** I will **investigate** the matter and **inform** you.<br>*Tôi sẽ xem xét vấn đề và thông báo cho anh/chị.*
- **Informal:** We **put off** the party because the kids were sick.<br>*Bọn mình hoãn bữa tiệc vì bọn trẻ bị ốm.*
- **Formal:** The event has been **postponed** due to bad weather.<br>*Sự kiện đã được hoãn do thời tiết xấu.*

### 2. Linking words

| Informal | Formal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| but | however | nhưng, tuy nhiên | It's cheap, **but** it's slow. → It is cheap. **However**, it is slow. |
| so | therefore | vì vậy | I was ill, **so** I stayed home. → I was ill. **Therefore**, I was absent. |
| also, plus | in addition, furthermore | ngoài ra | **Plus**, it's near my house. → **In addition**, it is close to public transport. |
| because of | due to | do, vì | We were late **because of** the rain. → The flight was delayed **due to** fog. |
| anyway | nevertheless | dù sao | It was hard. I did it **anyway**. → It was difficult. **Nevertheless**, I completed it. |

- **Informal:** The hotel was nice, **but** the food wasn't great.<br>*Khách sạn đẹp, nhưng đồ ăn không ngon lắm.*
- **Formal:** The hotel was comfortable. **However**, the food was disappointing.<br>*Khách sạn tiện nghi. Tuy nhiên, đồ ăn gây thất vọng.*
- **Formal:** I have five years of experience. **In addition**, I speak fluent English.<br>*Tôi có năm năm kinh nghiệm. Ngoài ra, tôi nói tiếng Anh lưu loát.*

### 3. Other words

| Informal | Formal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| kids | children | trẻ em | The **kids** are asleep. → **Children** under 6 travel free. |
| about | approximately | khoảng | It's **about** 10 km. → The distance is **approximately** 10 km. |
| a lot of | a large number of / a great deal of | nhiều | **A lot of** people came. → **A large number of** people attended. |
| enough | sufficient | đủ | We don't have **enough** money. → We do not have **sufficient** funds. |
| job | position | công việc, vị trí | I want this **job**. → I am applying for the **position** of sales manager. |
| sorry | we apologise | xin lỗi | **Sorry** I'm late! → **We apologise** for the delay. |
| OK | acceptable | được, chấp nhận được | Is this **OK**? → Is this **acceptable**? |

- **Informal:** **Sorry** about the mess. The **kids** were playing here.<br>*Xin lỗi vì bừa bộn nhé. Bọn trẻ vừa chơi ở đây.*
- **Formal:** **We apologise** for any inconvenience.<br>*Chúng tôi xin lỗi vì mọi bất tiện.*
- **Formal:** The trip takes **approximately** three hours.<br>*Chuyến đi mất khoảng ba tiếng.*

## Tips for formal writing

| Tip | Informal | Formal |
|---|---|---|
| Don't use short forms | I'**m** sorry, I **can't** come. | I **am** sorry, but I **cannot** attend. |
| Use one verb instead of a phrasal verb | Please **fill in** this form. | Please **complete** this form. |
| Avoid slang and exclamations | I **wanna** know. It's **super** cheap! | I **would like to** know. It is **very** reasonably priced. |
| Make requests more polite | **Send** me the form. | **Could you please** send me the form? |
| Very polite requests | **Can you** call me back? | **I would be grateful if you could** call me back. |
| Opening a letter | **Hi** Lan, | **Dear** Ms Lan, |
| Closing a letter | **See you!** / **Bye!** | **Best regards,** / **Yours sincerely,** |

- **Informal:** Hi Minh, **I'm gonna** be late. **Can't** wait to see you!<br>*Chào Minh, mình đến muộn chút nhé. Nóng lòng gặp cậu quá!*
- **Formal:** Dear Mr Minh, **I am writing to inform you that** I will arrive later than planned. **I look forward to** meeting you.<br>*Kính gửi ông Minh, tôi viết thư này để báo rằng tôi sẽ đến muộn hơn dự kiến. Tôi rất mong được gặp ông.*
- **Formal:** **I would be grateful if you could** send me more information about the course.<br>*Tôi rất cảm ơn nếu anh/chị có thể gửi cho tôi thêm thông tin về khóa học.*

::: tip TIP
Don't **mix** the two styles in one piece of writing. An email that starts with *Dear Sir or Madam* must not have *gonna, wanna, thanks a lot*. And if you text a close friend *I am writing to inform you*, it sounds very funny.
:::

## Common idioms

An idiom is a phrase with a **non-literal meaning**: you can't guess it from the meaning of each word. Idioms belong to the **informal** style. Use them when speaking, **not** in formal writing.

| Idiom | Meaning | Example | Translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| a piece of cake | rất dễ, dễ như ăn kẹo | The test was **a piece of cake**. | Bài kiểm tra dễ như ăn kẹo. |
| break the ice | phá tan sự ngại ngùng | He told a joke to **break the ice**. | Anh ấy kể chuyện cười cho đỡ ngại. |
| under the weather | thấy mệt, không khỏe | I'm a bit **under the weather** today. | Hôm nay tôi thấy hơi mệt. |
| hit the books | học bài chăm chỉ | I have an exam tomorrow, so I need to **hit the books**. | Mai tôi thi nên phải học bài thôi. |
| call it a day | nghỉ, dừng làm việc | It's 7 p.m. Let's **call it a day**. | 7 giờ tối rồi. Hôm nay nghỉ thôi. |
| once in a blue moon | rất hiếm khi | I eat fast food **once in a blue moon**. | Lâu lắm tôi mới ăn đồ ăn nhanh một lần. |
| cost an arm and a leg | đắt cắt cổ | This phone **cost an arm and a leg**. | Cái điện thoại này đắt cắt cổ. |
| get cold feet | chùn bước, sợ vào phút chót | She **got cold feet** and didn't sing. | Cô ấy chùn bước nên không hát nữa. |
| on cloud nine | sướng như trên mây | He's been **on cloud nine** since he got the job. | Từ khi được nhận việc, anh ấy sướng như trên mây. |
| the last straw | giọt nước tràn ly | He was late again. That was **the last straw**. | Anh ta lại đến muộn. Đó là giọt nước tràn ly. |
| keep an eye on | trông chừng | Can you **keep an eye on** my bag? | Bạn trông giúp tôi cái túi nhé? |
| spill the beans | để lộ bí mật | Don't **spill the beans** about the party! | Đừng để lộ chuyện bữa tiệc nhé! |
| in hot water | gặp rắc rối | He's **in hot water** with his boss. | Anh ấy đang gặp rắc rối với sếp. |
| better late than never | muộn còn hơn không | You finally called! **Better late than never**. | Cuối cùng bạn cũng gọi! Muộn còn hơn không. |
| a blessing in disguise | trong cái rủi có cái may | Losing that job was **a blessing in disguise**. | Mất việc đó hóa ra lại là trong rủi có may. |
| when pigs fly | không bao giờ, còn lâu | He'll clean his room **when pigs fly**. | Còn lâu nó mới dọn phòng. |

::: tip TIP
Say an idiom **word for word**; don't change any words: *a piece of cake*, not *a cake piece*; *under **the** weather*, not *under weather*. Learn each idiom together with an example sentence so you remember how to use it too.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Dear Sir, I wanna apply for the job. | Dear Sir, I **would like to** apply for the position. | Formal letters don't use *wanna*. |
| Thanks a lot. Bye! (at the end of a job application letter) | **Thank you** for your time. **Yours faithfully,** | A formal letter needs a thank-you line and a standard closing. |
| I don't have sufficient money for a coffee, mate. | I don't have **enough** money for a coffee, mate. | In casual talk, *sufficient* sounds too stiff. |
| The exam was a cake piece. | The exam was **a piece of cake**. | Don't change the word order of an idiom. |
| I'm under weather today. | I'm **under the weather** today. | Keep every word of an idiom. |
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Informal: phrasal verbs, short forms, short words (*get, find out, but*). Formal: single verbs, no short forms (*receive, discover, however*).
- Formal requests: *Could you please…? / I would be grateful if you could…*
- Idioms have a non-literal meaning; keep them **word for word**, and use them only in speaking and informal writing.
:::
