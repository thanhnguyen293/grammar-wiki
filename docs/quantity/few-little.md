# few / a few · little / a little

All four words mean **not much / not many**. But with **a**, the meaning is **positive** (a small amount, but **enough**); without **a**, the meaning is **negative** (so little that there is **almost none**). Choose *few* or *little* depending on whether the noun is countable or not.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Word | Goes with | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| a few | plural countable | some, **enough** (positive) | I have **a few** friends in Hanoi. |
| few | plural countable | very few, **almost none** (negative) | **Few** people came to the meeting. |
| a little | uncountable | a small amount, **enough** (positive) | There's **a little** rice left. |
| little | uncountable | very little, **almost none** (negative) | There's **little** rice left. |
:::

::: warning IMPORTANT
**few / a few** + **countable** nouns (*friends, eggs, days*). **little / a little** + **uncountable** nouns (*money, time, water*).

With **a** = the glass **still has some** water you can drink. Without **a** = the glass is **almost empty**.
:::

## How to use

### 1. a few: some (positive)

- I have **a few** days off next week, so let's meet.<br>*Tuần sau tôi được nghỉ vài ngày, nên mình gặp nhau đi.*
- **A few** friends came to help me move house.<br>*Vài người bạn đến giúp tôi chuyển nhà.*
- Wait **a few** minutes, please.<br>*Làm ơn đợi vài phút.*
- She speaks **a few** words of Japanese.<br>*Cô ấy nói được vài từ tiếng Nhật.*

### 2. few: very few, almost none (negative)

- **Few** people know his real name.<br>*Rất ít người biết tên thật của anh ấy.*
- The village is very quiet. There are **few** shops there.<br>*Ngôi làng rất yên tĩnh. Ở đó hầu như không có cửa hàng nào.*
- He has **few** friends, so he often feels lonely.<br>*Anh ấy có rất ít bạn, nên anh ấy hay thấy cô đơn.*
- **Few** students passed the test. It was too hard.<br>*Rất ít học sinh qua bài kiểm tra. Nó khó quá.*

### 3. a little: a small amount (positive)

- Can I have **a little** sugar in my coffee?<br>*Cho tôi một chút đường vào cà phê được không?*
- I have **a little** money left. Let's get a drink.<br>*Tôi còn một ít tiền. Mình đi uống gì đi.*
- She speaks **a little** English.<br>*Cô ấy nói được một chút tiếng Anh.*
- We need **a little** more time to finish.<br>*Chúng tôi cần thêm một chút thời gian để làm xong.*

### 4. little: very little, almost none (negative)

- There's **little** hope now.<br>*Bây giờ hầu như không còn hy vọng.*
- I have **little** time for hobbies these days.<br>*Dạo này tôi gần như không có thời gian cho sở thích.*
- He eats very **little** food when he is stressed.<br>*Anh ấy ăn rất ít khi bị căng thẳng.*
- There was **little** water in the river last summer.<br>*Mùa hè năm ngoái, sông gần như cạn nước.*

## Comparing each pair

In the same situation, having **a** or not gives the sentence a completely different feeling.

| Positive (with a) | Negative (without a) |
|---|---|
| I have **a few** friends here, so I'm happy. | I have **few** friends here, so I feel lonely. |
| There are **a few** eggs. We can make an omelette. | There are **few** eggs. We can't make an omelette. |
| She has **a little** money, so she can buy lunch. | She has **little** money, so she can't buy lunch. |
| We have **a little** time. Let's have a coffee. | We have **little** time. We must hurry. |

- I have **a few** friends here, so I'm happy.<br>*Tôi có vài người bạn ở đây, nên tôi vui.*
- I have **few** friends here, so I feel lonely.<br>*Tôi có rất ít bạn ở đây, nên tôi thấy cô đơn.*
- She has **a little** money, so she can buy lunch.<br>*Cô ấy có chút tiền, nên có thể mua bữa trưa.*
- She has **little** money, so she can't buy lunch.<br>*Cô ấy gần như không có tiền, nên không mua được bữa trưa.*

## quite a few, very few, very little

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| quite a few | **quite a lot** | **Quite a few** people came to the wedding. |
| very few | very few (countable) | **Very few** students were late. |
| very little | very little (uncountable) | I have **very little** free time. |
| only a few | just a few | There are **only a few** tickets left. |
| only a little | just a small amount | There's **only a little** soup left. |

- **Quite a few** of my classmates live abroad now.<br>*Khá nhiều bạn học cũ của tôi giờ sống ở nước ngoài.*
- **Very few** shops are open on the first day of Tet.<br>*Rất ít cửa hàng mở cửa vào mùng Một Tết.*
- My father drinks **very little** alcohol.<br>*Bố tôi uống rất ít rượu.*
- Hurry! There are **only a few** seats left.<br>*Nhanh lên! Chỉ còn vài ghế thôi.*

::: tip TIP
*quite a few* sounds like "a small number", but it actually means **quite a lot**.

In speaking, people often use **not many / not much** instead of *few / little* to sound softer: *I don't have **many** friends here.* = *I have **few** friends here.*

*a little* can also come before an adjective, meaning **slightly**: *I'm **a little** tired.* (Tôi hơi mệt.)
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Can I have a few water? | Can I have **a little** water? | *water* is uncountable, so use *a little*. |
| I have little friends. | I have **few** friends. | *friends* is countable, so use *few*. |
| I'm sad because I have a few friends. | I'm sad because I have **few** friends. | For a sad, negative meaning, drop *a*. |
| Quite few people came, so it was fun. | **Quite a few** people came, so it was fun. | "Quite a lot" must be *quite a few*. |
| There are a little apples left. | There are **a few** apples left. | *apples* is countable, so use *a few*. |
:::

## In context

> **Mai:** How's your new neighbourhood? \
> **Đức:** It's nice, but it's very quiet. There are **few** restaurants and **very few** shops. \
> **Mai:** Do you know anyone there? \
> **Đức:** I know **a few** neighbours. They're friendly, and **quite a few** of them have kids like me. \
> **Mai:** That's good. Do you have time to visit me this weekend? \
> **Đức:** I have **little** free time on Saturday, but I have **a little** time on Sunday afternoon.

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **few / a few** + countable; **little / a little** + uncountable.
- With **a**: a small amount but **enough**, positive. Without **a**: so little there is **almost none**, negative.
- **quite a few** = quite a lot; **very few / very little** = very small amount.
:::
