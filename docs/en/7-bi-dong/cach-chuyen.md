# Forming the passive

A passive sentence puts **the person or thing affected** in the subject position, like "được / bị" in Vietnamese: *Ngôi nhà **được xây** năm 1990* (the house was built in 1990), *Xe tôi **bị lấy trộm*** (my bike was stolen). We use it when we care more about **what happened** than about **who did it**.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Active | S + V + O | My mother **cleans** the house. |
| Passive | S + be + V3 (+ by + doer) | The house **is cleaned** (by my mother). |
| Negative | S + be + not + V3 | The house **isn't cleaned** every day. |
| Question | Be + S + V3? | **Is** the house **cleaned** every day? |
:::

*V3* is the past participle: *clean → cleaned, make → made, write → written*. For irregular verbs, you have to learn the third column by heart.

- English **is spoken** in many countries.<br>*Tiếng Anh được nói ở nhiều nước.*
- My bike **was stolen** last night.<br>*Xe đạp của tôi bị lấy trộm tối qua.*
- These cakes **are made** by my grandma.<br>*Những chiếc bánh này do bà tôi làm.*

## Three steps to change

::: warning IMPORTANT
**Step 1:** Take the **object** (the person/thing affected) and make it the new subject.<br>
**Step 2:** Write **be + V3**. *be* follows the **tense of the original sentence** and the **new subject**.<br>
**Step 3:** Move the old subject to the end as **by + doer**, or leave it out if it isn't needed.
:::

### Sentence 1: My mother cooks dinner.

- **Step 1:** the object *dinner* goes first → **Dinner** …
- **Step 2:** the original is in the present simple, *dinner* is singular → **is** + *cooked* → Dinner **is cooked** …
- **Step 3:** *my mother* → **by my mother**.
- **Result:** Dinner **is cooked** by my mother.<br>*Bữa tối do mẹ tôi nấu.*

### Sentence 2: Tom broke the windows.

- **Step 1:** the object *the windows* goes first → **The windows** …
- **Step 2:** the original is in the past simple, *the windows* is plural → **were** + *broken* → The windows **were broken** …
- **Step 3:** *Tom* → **by Tom**.
- **Result:** The windows **were broken** by Tom.<br>*Những ô cửa sổ bị Tom làm vỡ.*

### Sentence 3: They sell fresh fruit at this market.

- **Step 1:** the object *fresh fruit* goes first → **Fresh fruit** …
- **Step 2:** present simple, *fruit* is uncountable (treated as singular) → **is** + *sold* → Fresh fruit **is sold** …
- **Step 3:** *they* means people in general, so we don't need it → **drop by them**.
- **Result:** Fresh fruit **is sold** at this market.<br>*Trái cây tươi được bán ở chợ này.*

### Sentence 4: Somebody stole my phone yesterday.

- **Step 1:** the object *my phone* goes first → **My phone** …
- **Step 2:** past simple, *my phone* is singular → **was** + *stolen* → My phone **was stolen** …
- **Step 3:** *somebody* means we don't know who → **drop by somebody**. The time word stays at the end.
- **Result:** My phone **was stolen** yesterday.<br>*Điện thoại của tôi bị lấy trộm hôm qua.*

### Sentence 5: The company will send you an email.

- **Step 1:** the object *an email* goes first → **An email** …
- **Step 2:** future simple → **will be** + *sent* → An email **will be sent** …
- **Step 3:** the sender is the company, everyone knows that → drop *by the company*; keep *to you*.
- **Result:** An email **will be sent** to you.<br>*Một email sẽ được gửi cho bạn.*

### Changing pronouns when the object becomes the subject

If the object is a pronoun, change it to the subject form.

| Object (active) | Subject (passive) | Example |
|---|---|---|
| me | I | They invited **me**. → **I** was invited. |
| you | you | We will call **you**. → **You** will be called. |
| him / her | he / she | The police stopped **him**. → **He** was stopped by the police. |
| us | we | Our teacher helps **us**. → **We** are helped by our teacher. |
| them | they | People love **them**. → **They** are loved. |

## How to use

### 1. We don't know who did it, or it isn't important

- My car **was stolen** last week.<br>*Xe hơi của tôi bị lấy trộm tuần trước.* (we don't know who took it)
- This bridge **was built** in 1995.<br>*Cây cầu này được xây năm 1995.*
- The rooms **are cleaned** every morning.<br>*Các phòng được dọn mỗi sáng.*
- Rice **is grown** in many parts of Vietnam.<br>*Lúa được trồng ở nhiều vùng của Việt Nam.*

### 2. We want to focus on the person or thing affected

- **My grandfather was born** in 1950.<br>*Ông tôi sinh năm 1950.*
- **The new hospital will be opened** next month.<br>*Bệnh viện mới sẽ được khai trương vào tháng sau.*
- **I was bitten** by a dog when I was a child.<br>*Hồi nhỏ tôi bị chó cắn.*
- **Our flight was cancelled** because of the storm.<br>*Chuyến bay của chúng tôi bị hủy vì bão.*

### 3. Notices, signs, news and instructions

Formal writing and instructions use the passive a lot.

- Breakfast **is served** from 6 to 9 a.m.<br>*Bữa sáng được phục vụ từ 6 đến 9 giờ sáng.*
- Smoking **is not allowed** in this building.<br>*Không được hút thuốc trong tòa nhà này.*
- Two people **were hurt** in the accident.<br>*Hai người bị thương trong vụ tai nạn.*
- The rice **is washed** and then **cooked** for 20 minutes.<br>*Gạo được vo sạch rồi nấu trong 20 phút.*

## When to drop "by + doer"

Most passive sentences **don't have** *by*. Keep *by* only when the doer is information you **need to give**.

| Situation | Drop or keep | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The doer is unknown: *someone, somebody* | Drop | Someone opened the door. → The door **was opened**. |
| The doer is general: *people, they, we, you* | Drop | People speak French here. → French **is spoken** here. |
| Everyone knows who it is | Drop | The police arrested the thief. → The thief **was arrested**. |
| The doer is important, new information | Keep | My uncle painted this picture. → This picture **was painted by my uncle**. |
| The doer is famous, worth mentioning | Keep | Nguyễn Du wrote *The Tale of Kiều*. → *The Tale of Kiều* **was written by Nguyễn Du**. |

- The letters **are delivered** every morning.<br>*Thư được phát mỗi sáng.* (drop *by the postman*, everyone knows)
- This dress **was made by my mother**.<br>*Chiếc váy này do mẹ tôi may.* (keep it, because that's what you want to show off)
- The window **was broken by the wind**, not by the kids.<br>*Cửa sổ bị gió làm vỡ, không phải bọn trẻ.* (keep it, because it's the main information)

## Verbs with no passive

Only verbs **with an object** can become passive. Verbs with no object (intransitive verbs) such as *happen, arrive, die, sleep, go, come, fall, rain, cry, laugh, appear, disappear* **have no passive**.

| Verb | Meaning | Correct example |
|---|---|---|
| happen | take place | The accident **happened** at 8 p.m. |
| arrive | reach a place | The train **arrived** late. |
| die | stop living | His dog **died** last year. |
| fall | drop down | The old man **fell** off his bike. |
| disappear | go out of sight | My keys **disappeared**. |

- The accident **happened** last night. (don't write ~~was happened~~)<br>*Vụ tai nạn xảy ra tối qua.*
- She **arrived** at the airport at 10. (don't write ~~was arrived~~)<br>*Cô ấy đến sân bay lúc 10 giờ.*
- My grandfather **died** in 2010. (don't write ~~was died~~)<br>*Ông tôi mất năm 2010.*

::: tip TIP
Quick check: ask "**what?**" or "**who?**" after the verb. *She cleaned **the room*** → there is an object, so it can change. *She arrived* → no object, so it can't.

Some state verbs like *have* (own), *fit* (be the right size), *suit* (look good on) also don't use the passive: *I **have** a car* (don't write ~~A car is had by me~~).
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The accident was happened at night. | The accident **happened** at night. | *happen* has no object, so it has no passive. |
| The house built in 1990. | The house **was built** in 1990. | The passive must have **be**. |
| This song was wrote by my brother. | This song was **written** by my brother. | Use **V3**, not V2. |
| The cakes is made by my mum. | The cakes **are** made by my mum. | *be* agrees with the new subject (plural). |
| French is spoken here by people. | French **is spoken** here. | *by people* adds nothing; drop it. |
:::

## In context

> Vietnamese coffee **is grown** mainly in the Central Highlands. \
> The coffee cherries **are picked** from October to December. \
> Then they **are dried** in the sun for two or three weeks. \
> After that, the beans **are roasted** and **sold** to shops and cafés. \
> Last year, my family visited a coffee farm there. \
> We **were shown** how the beans **are roasted**, and we **were given** some fresh coffee to take home.

Năm ngoái, gia đình tôi đã đến thăm một trang trại cà phê ở đó.<br>
Chúng tôi được chỉ cho xem cách rang hạt, và được tặng một ít cà phê tươi mang về nhà.

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Passive: **S + be + V3 (+ by + doer)**.
- Three steps: the object becomes the subject → **be** (in the original tense, agreeing with the new subject) + **V3** → the old subject becomes **by…** or is dropped.
- Drop *by* when the doer is unknown, general, or obvious.
- Verbs with no object (*happen, arrive, die…*) **have no passive**.
:::
