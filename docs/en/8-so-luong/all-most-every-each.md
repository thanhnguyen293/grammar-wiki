# all / most / every / each / both…

These words tell you whether you mean **all, most, each one, both, one of two** or **none**. The hardest part is knowing whether they take a **singular or plural** noun, and which verb form to use.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Word | Meaning | Goes with | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| all | the whole group | plural / uncountable nouns | **All** children **love** sweets. |
| most | the biggest part | plural / uncountable nouns | **Most** people **like** music. |
| every | all, each | **singular** nouns | **Every** student **has** a book. |
| each | each one separately | **singular** nouns | **Each** room **has** a TV. |
| both | the two together | plural nouns | **Both** my sisters **are** teachers. |
| either | one of the two | **singular** nouns | You can come on **either** day. |
| neither | not one of the two | **singular** nouns | **Neither** answer **is** correct. |
| no | not any | singular / plural / uncountable nouns | There **are no** buses today. |
| none of | not one person or thing | the / my / them… + noun | **None of** my friends **smoke**. |
:::

### Add "of the…" for a specific group

Most of the words above can be used as **word + of + the / my / these / them**. Then we are talking about a group we **already know**.

| Without of (in general) | With of the… (a specific group) |
|---|---|
| **Most** students like holidays. | **Most of the** students in my class live nearby. |
| **Each** room has a fan. | **Each of the** rooms on this floor has a fan. |
| **Both** cars are new. | **Both of** my cars are new. |
| **All** shops close at 10. | **All of the** shops in this street close at 10. |

::: warning IMPORTANT
After **of** you must have **the / my / your / these / them…**. Never put a noun straight after *of*.

~~Most of students~~ → **Most students** (in general) or **Most of the students** (a specific group).
:::

## How to use

### 1. all: the whole group

*all* + a plural or uncountable noun. You can say *all the…* or *all of the…*; they mean the same. With pronouns you must use *of*: *all of us, all of them*.

- **All** my friends **are** married now.<br>*Tất cả bạn bè tôi giờ đã lập gia đình.*
- **All the** food **was** delicious.<br>*Tất cả đồ ăn đều ngon.*
- **All of us** were tired after the trip.<br>*Tất cả chúng tôi đều mệt sau chuyến đi.*
- I've worked here **all** my life.<br>*Tôi làm ở đây cả đời rồi.*

### 2. most / most of the: the biggest part, almost all

*most* + noun: talking **in general**. *most of the / my…* + noun: talking about a **specific group**.

- **Most** Vietnamese people **eat** rice every day.<br>*Hầu hết người Việt ăn cơm hằng ngày.*
- **Most of the** students in my class **are** from Hanoi.<br>*Phần lớn học sinh lớp tôi đến từ Hà Nội.*
- I spent **most of my** money on books.<br>*Tôi tiêu phần lớn tiền vào sách.*
- **Most of them** didn't know the answer.<br>*Phần lớn bọn họ không biết câu trả lời.*

### 3. every: all, each (looking at the whole group)

*every* + a **singular** noun + a **singular** verb, even though it means "all". Don't use *every of*.

- **Every** child **needs** love.<br>*Đứa trẻ nào cũng cần tình yêu thương.*
- I go jogging **every** morning.<br>*Sáng nào tôi cũng chạy bộ.*
- **Every** room in the hotel **has** a balcony.<br>*Phòng nào trong khách sạn cũng có ban công.*
- She calls her mother **every** day.<br>*Ngày nào cô ấy cũng gọi cho mẹ.*

### 4. each: each (looking at things one by one)

*each* + a **singular** noun, or *each of the / them* + a **plural** noun. The verb is always **singular**.

- **Each** student **gets** a free book.<br>*Mỗi học sinh được tặng một cuốn sách.*
- **Each of the** rooms **has** its own bathroom.<br>*Mỗi phòng đều có phòng tắm riêng.*
- The teacher spoke to **each of us**.<br>*Cô giáo nói chuyện với từng người chúng tôi.*
- These apples cost 10,000 dong **each**.<br>*Những quả táo này giá 10.000 đồng mỗi quả.*

| Word | What's different | Example |
|---|---|---|
| every | Looks at **the whole group**, usually a big group, close to *all* | **Every** student in the school wears a uniform. |
| each | Looks at **each one separately**, a small group, even just **two** | She has a ring on **each** hand. |
| each of | Can take *of*; *every* can't | **Each of** the children got a gift. |

### 5. both: the two together

*both* + a **plural** noun + a **plural** verb. There is also *both… and…*.

- **Both** my parents **are** doctors.<br>*Cả bố và mẹ tôi đều là bác sĩ.*
- **Both of** the shirts **look** nice on you.<br>*Cả hai chiếc áo đều hợp với bạn.*
- **Both of them** live in Saigon.<br>*Cả hai người đều sống ở Sài Gòn.*
- She speaks **both** English **and** French.<br>*Cô ấy nói được cả tiếng Anh lẫn tiếng Pháp.*

### 6. either / neither: one of the two / not one of the two

*either* = this one or that one, both are fine. *neither* = not one of the two. Both take a **singular** noun. After *either of / neither of*, use a plural noun.

- "Tea or coffee?" – "**Either** is fine."<br>*"Trà hay cà phê?" – "Cái nào cũng được."*
- You can park on **either** side of the road.<br>*Bạn có thể đỗ xe ở bên nào của đường cũng được.*
- **Neither** of my brothers **is** married.<br>*Cả hai anh trai tôi đều chưa lập gia đình.*
- I tried two jackets, but **neither** fitted me.<br>*Tôi thử hai cái áo khoác, nhưng không cái nào vừa.*

### 7. no / none of: not any, not one person or thing

*no* + noun (the verb stays **positive**). *none of* + *the / my / them…*. After *none of* + a plural noun, the verb can be singular or plural; singular is more formal.

- There **are no** eggs in the fridge.<br>*Trong tủ lạnh không có quả trứng nào.*
- I have **no** time today.<br>*Hôm nay tôi không có thời gian.*
- **None of** my friends **smoke**.<br>*Không ai trong số bạn tôi hút thuốc.*
- **None of** the food **was** left.<br>*Không còn chút đồ ăn nào.*

## Verb agreement

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| all / most + plural noun | plural | **All** the students **are** here. |
| all / most + uncountable noun | singular | **Most** of the money **was** spent. |
| every + singular noun | singular | **Every** worker **has** a uniform. |
| each / each of the + noun | singular | **Each of** the boys **has** a bike. |
| both / both of | plural | **Both** of them **work** at night. |
| either / neither + singular noun | singular | **Neither** answer **is** right. |
| none of + plural noun | singular or plural | **None of** the guests **has / have** arrived. |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Most of students like music. | **Most students** like music. / **Most of the** students like music. | After *of* you need *the / my / them…*. |
| Every students are here. | **Every student is** here. | *every* + singular noun + singular verb. |
| Each of student has a book. | **Each of the students** has a book. | *each of* + *the* + plural noun. |
| Both of my sister is nurses. | Both of my **sisters are** nurses. | *both* + plural noun + plural verb. |
| Every of the rooms has a TV. | **Each of** the rooms has a TV. / **Every** room has a TV. | There is no *every of*. |
:::

## In context

> **Hùng:** How is the new team? \
> **Lan:** Very good. **All** of them **work** hard, and **most** of them speak good English. \
> **Hùng:** Do they have laptops? \
> **Lan:** Yes, **each** person **has** a laptop, and **every** desk **has** a phone. \
> **Hùng:** What about the two new designers? \
> **Lan:** **Both** of them **are** great. **Neither** of them **has** been late so far, and **none** of the clients **have** complained.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Hùng:** Nhóm mới thế nào?<br>
**Lan:** Rất tốt ạ. Tất cả đều làm việc chăm chỉ, và phần lớn nói tiếng Anh tốt.<br>
**Hùng:** Họ có máy tính xách tay chưa?<br>
**Lan:** Có ạ, mỗi người có một máy tính xách tay, và bàn nào cũng có điện thoại.<br>
**Hùng:** Còn hai bạn thiết kế mới thì sao?<br>
**Lan:** Cả hai đều rất giỏi. Đến giờ chưa bạn nào đi muộn, và không khách hàng nào phàn nàn cả.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **every, each, either, neither** + **singular** noun + **singular** verb.
- **all, most, both** + plural noun; **both** always takes a plural verb.
- After **of** you need *the / my / them…*: **most of the** students, not ~~most of students~~.
- **no** + noun; **none of** + *the / my / them…*. There is no *every of*.
:::
