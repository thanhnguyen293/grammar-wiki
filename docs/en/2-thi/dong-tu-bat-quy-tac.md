# Common irregular verbs

Irregular verbs don't add **-ed** in the past; they change in their own way. You need **V2** for the [past simple](./qua-khu-don) and **V3** for the [perfect tenses](./hien-tai-hoan-thanh) and the passive. This page lists about 80 of the most common ones, sorted into groups to make them easier to learn.

| Column | Name | Used for | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | base form | present, after *will, can, did, to* | I **go** to work by bus. |
| V2 | past form | past simple | I **went** to Hue last year. |
| V3 | past participle | after *have / has / had*, the passive | I have **gone** there twice. |

::: tip TIP
Don't learn them in a, b, c order. Learn them **in groups that change the same way**, as below: remember one word and you can guess the whole group. Learn 5–10 words a day, say all three forms out loud as one chain (*bring – brought – brought*), then make one sentence with V2 (*yesterday*) and one with V3 (*I have already…*).
:::

## Group A-A-A: all three forms are the same

The easiest group: nothing changes.

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| bet | bet | bet | đánh cược |
| cost | cost | cost | có giá, tốn |
| cut | cut | cut | cắt |
| hit | hit | hit | đánh, đâm vào |
| hurt | hurt | hurt | làm đau, bị đau |
| let | let | let | để cho, cho phép |
| put | put | put | đặt, để |
| quit | quit | quit | bỏ, nghỉ (việc) |
| read | read | read | đọc |
| set | set | set | đặt, cài (giờ) |
| shut | shut | shut | đóng |
| spread | spread | spread | trải ra, lan ra |
| upset | upset | upset | làm buồn lòng |

- I **cut** my finger while I was cooking yesterday.<br>*Hôm qua tôi bị đứt tay khi đang nấu ăn.*
- This bag **cost** 300,000 dong.<br>*Cái túi này giá 300.000 đồng.*
- Have you **read** this book?<br>*Bạn đọc cuốn sách này chưa?*
- My dad **quit** smoking last year.<br>*Bố tôi bỏ thuốc lá năm ngoái.*

::: tip TIP
**read** is spelled the same but **said differently**: V1 is /riːd/ (like "reed"), V2 and V3 are /red/ (like the color "red").
:::

## Group A-B-B: V2 and V3 are the same

The biggest group. You only need to remember **one past form** for both V2 and V3.

### Ending in -ought / -aught

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| bring | brought | brought | mang đến |
| buy | bought | bought | mua |
| fight | fought | fought | đánh nhau, chiến đấu |
| think | thought | thought | nghĩ |
| catch | caught | caught | bắt, đón (xe) |
| teach | taught | taught | dạy |

### Long vowel becomes short (ee, ea → e)

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| feel | felt | felt | cảm thấy |
| keep | kept | kept | giữ |
| sleep | slept | slept | ngủ |
| mean | meant | meant | có nghĩa là |
| leave | left | left | rời đi, để lại |
| meet | met | met | gặp |
| feed | fed | fed | cho ăn |
| lead | led | led | dẫn dắt |

### Ending -d becomes -t

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| build | built | built | xây |
| lend | lent | lent | cho mượn |
| send | sent | sent | gửi |
| spend | spent | spent | tiêu (tiền), dành (thời gian) |

### Ending -ay becomes -aid

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| pay | paid | paid | trả tiền |
| say | said | said | nói |

### Other verbs

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| find | found | found | tìm thấy |
| get | got | got / gotten | nhận được, trở nên |
| have | had | had | có |
| hear | heard | heard | nghe thấy |
| hold | held | held | cầm, tổ chức |
| lose | lost | lost | mất, thua |
| make | made | made | làm, chế tạo |
| sell | sold | sold | bán |
| tell | told | told | kể, bảo |
| sit | sat | sat | ngồi |
| stand | stood | stood | đứng |
| understand | understood | understood | hiểu |
| win | won | won | thắng |

- I **bought** a new phone last week.<br>*Tuần trước tôi mua điện thoại mới.*
- She **taught** English for ten years.<br>*Cô ấy dạy tiếng Anh mười năm.*
- We **have** already **paid** the bill.<br>*Chúng tôi trả tiền rồi.*
- My grandma **told** us a funny story last night.<br>*Tối qua bà kể cho chúng tôi nghe một câu chuyện vui.*

::: tip TIP
**gotten** is the V3 of *get* in American English; British English uses **got**. Both are correct.
:::

## Group A-B-C: three different forms

The hardest group, but the smaller groups still follow patterns.

### Vowels i – a – u

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| begin | began | begun | bắt đầu |
| drink | drank | drunk | uống |
| ring | rang | rung | reo, gọi điện |
| sing | sang | sung | hát |
| swim | swam | swum | bơi |

### V2 ends in -ew, V3 ends in -wn

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |

| grow | grew | grown | lớn lên, trồng |

| forget | forgot | forgotten | quên |
| speak | spoke | spoken | nói |

| wake | woke | woken | thức dậy, đánh thức |
| wear | wore | worn | mặc, đội, đeo |

### V3 adds -n / -en

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| bite | bit | bitten | cắn |
| drive | drove | driven | lái xe |
| eat | ate | eaten | ăn |
| fall | fell | fallen | ngã, rơi |
| forgive | forgave | forgiven | tha thứ |
| give | gave | given | cho, tặng |
| hide | hid | hidden | trốn, giấu |
| ride | rode | ridden | đi (xe đạp, xe máy), cưỡi |
| rise | rose | risen | mọc (mặt trời), tăng |
| see | saw | seen | nhìn thấy |
| show | showed | shown | cho xem, chỉ |
| take | took | taken | cầm, lấy, mang đi |
| write | wrote | written | viết |

### Special (learn these one by one)

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| be | was / were | been | thì, là, ở |
| do | did | done | làm |
| go | went | gone | đi |

- We **swam** in the sea yesterday.<br>*Hôm qua chúng tôi bơi ở biển.*
- Someone **has stolen** my bike!<br>*Ai đó lấy trộm xe đạp của tôi rồi!*
- I **wrote** a letter to my grandma.<br>*Tôi viết thư cho bà.*
- Have you ever **eaten** durian?<br>*Bạn đã bao giờ ăn sầu riêng chưa?*

## Group A-B-A: V1 and V3 are the same

| V1 | V2 | V3 | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| become | became | become | trở thành |
| come | came | come | đến |
| run | ran | run | chạy |

- She **became** a doctor in 2015.<br>*Cô ấy trở thành bác sĩ năm 2015.*
- My parents **have** just **come** back from Da Lat.<br>*Bố mẹ tôi vừa đi Đà Lạt về.*
- He **ran** to the bus stop.<br>*Anh ấy chạy ra bến xe buýt.*

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I buyed a new phone. | I **bought** a new phone. | *buy* is an irregular verb. |
| She has went home. | She has **gone** home. | After *has*, use V3, not V2. |
| I have ate lunch. | I have **eaten** lunch. | The V3 of *eat* is *eaten*. |
| We swum in the sea yesterday. | We **swam** in the sea yesterday. | The past simple uses V2 (*swam*), not V3. |
| He catched the ball. | He **caught** the ball. | *catch* belongs to the -aught group. |
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **V2** is for the past simple; **V3** goes after *have / has / had* and in the passive.
- Learn them in 4 groups: **A-A-A** (cut), **A-B-B** (buy – bought – bought), **A-B-C** (eat – ate – eaten), **A-B-A** (come – came – come).
- Say all three forms out loud as a chain and make a sentence with each form so you remember them longer.
:::
