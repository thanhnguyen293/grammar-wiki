# Adding -s/-es, -ed, -ing and pronunciation

English verbs change their endings all the time: **-s/-es** in the present simple (and for plural nouns), **-ed** in the past, **-ing** in the continuous tenses. This lesson puts the spelling and pronunciation of these endings in one place so you can look them up quickly.

## Adding -s / -es

Use it for verbs with **he / she / it** in the [present simple](./hien-tai-don), and for plural nouns.

| Rule | Words | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Most words: add **-s** | work → works<br>read → reads<br>book → books | She **reads** the news every morning.<br>*Sáng nào cô ấy cũng đọc tin tức.* |
| Ending in **-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -z**: add **-es** | miss → misses<br>wash → washes<br>watch → watches<br>fix → fixes<br>bus → buses | He **watches** TV after dinner.<br>*Ăn tối xong anh ấy xem tivi.* |
| Verbs ending in **-o**: add **-es** | go → goes<br>do → does | My son **goes** to school by bike.<br>*Con trai tôi đi học bằng xe đạp.* |
| **Consonant + y**: drop the y, add **-ies** | study → studies<br>cry → cries<br>city → cities<br>baby → babies | My sister **studies** English at night.<br>*Em gái tôi học tiếng Anh buổi tối.* |
| **Vowel + y**: just add **-s** | play → plays<br>buy → buys<br>say → says<br>day → days | He **plays** football on Sundays.<br>*Chủ nhật anh ấy đá bóng.* |
| Special | have → **has** | My grandma **has** a small garden.<br>*Bà tôi có một khu vườn nhỏ.* |

::: tip TIP
The vowels are **a, e, i, o, u**. All other letters are consonants. *play* has **a** (a vowel) before **y**, so just add -s: plays. *study* has **d** (a consonant) before **y**, so it changes to -ies: studies.
:::

- The baby **cries** at night.<br>*Em bé khóc đêm.*
- My mom **buys** fruit at the market.<br>*Mẹ tôi mua hoa quả ở chợ.*
- She **washes** her hair every day.<br>*Ngày nào cô ấy cũng gội đầu.*
- Our teacher **fixes** the computer when it breaks.<br>*Thầy giáo sửa máy tính mỗi khi máy hỏng.*

## How to say -s / -es

There are **3 ways to say it**, depending on the **last sound** of the base word.

| Last sound of the base word | Say the ending as | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| /p/ /t/ /k/ /f/ /θ/ | **/s/** | stops, hats, books, likes, laughs, months, shops |
| /s/ /z/ /ʃ/ /tʃ/ /dʒ/ | **/ɪz/** (an extra "iz" sound) | buses, misses, roses, washes, watches, changes, oranges |
| All other sounds (vowels, /b/ /d/ /g/ /l/ /m/ /n/ /v/ /r/…) | **/z/** | plays, goes, dogs, bags, calls, rooms, lives, cars |

- She **likes** (say /s/) cats.<br>*Cô ấy thích mèo.*
- My dad **plays** (say /z/) chess with me.<br>*Bố tôi chơi cờ với tôi.*
- He **watches** (say /ɪz/) cartoons with his kids.<br>*Anh ấy xem phim hoạt hình với các con.*
- We need two **oranges** (say /ɪz/) and three **eggs** (say /z/).<br>*Chúng ta cần hai quả cam và ba quả trứng.*

::: tip TIP
The five **/s/** sounds, /p/ /t/ /k/ /f/ /θ/, are all made without using your voice. Put your fingers on your throat: if you feel no buzz on the last sound, the ending is **/s/**; if you feel a buzz, it is **/z/**.
:::

## Adding -ed

Use it for regular verbs in the [past simple](./qua-khu-don) and in the V3 form (present perfect, passive).

| Rule | Words | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Most words: add **-ed** | work → worked<br>clean → cleaned<br>want → wanted | I **cleaned** my room yesterday.<br>*Hôm qua tôi dọn phòng.* |
| Ending in **-e**: just add **-d** | live → lived<br>like → liked<br>dance → danced | We **lived** in Hue for five years.<br>*Chúng tôi từng sống ở Huế năm năm.* |
| **Consonant + y**: drop the y, add **-ied** | study → studied<br>try → tried<br>cry → cried<br>carry → carried | She **studied** hard for the exam.<br>*Cô ấy học chăm để thi.* |
| **Vowel + y**: just add **-ed** | play → played<br>stay → stayed<br>enjoy → enjoyed | We **stayed** at a small hotel.<br>*Chúng tôi ở một khách sạn nhỏ.* |
| **Double the last consonant** (see below) | stop → stopped<br>plan → planned<br>prefer → preferred | The bus **stopped** suddenly.<br>*Xe buýt dừng đột ngột.* |

- I **tried** pho for the first time in Hanoi.<br>*Lần đầu tôi ăn phở là ở Hà Nội.*
- He **carried** my bag to the car.<br>*Anh ấy xách túi giúp tôi ra xe.*
- They **enjoyed** the party.<br>*Họ rất vui ở bữa tiệc.*

## How to say -ed

| Last sound of the base word | Say the ending as | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| /t/ /d/ | **/ɪd/** (an extra "id" sound) | wanted, needed, waited, visited, started, decided, ended |
| /p/ /k/ /f/ /s/ /ʃ/ /tʃ/ /θ/ | **/t/** | stopped, helped, looked, worked, laughed, missed, washed, watched |
| All other sounds (vowels, /b/ /g/ /l/ /m/ /n/ /v/ /z/ /dʒ/ /r/…) | **/d/** | played, cleaned, lived, called, opened, studied, changed, closed |

- I **wanted** (say /ɪd/) to go home.<br>*Tôi muốn về nhà.*
- She **looked** (say /t/) at me and **smiled** (say /d/).<br>*Cô ấy nhìn tôi và mỉm cười.*
- We **waited** (say /ɪd/) for an hour.<br>*Chúng tôi đợi một tiếng đồng hồ.*
- He **washed** (say /t/) the car and **cleaned** (say /d/) the house.<br>*Anh ấy rửa xe và dọn nhà.*

::: tip TIP
The seven **/t/** sounds, /p/ /k/ /f/ /s/ /ʃ/ /tʃ/ /θ/, are all made without using your voice (no buzz in your throat). After them, -ed sounds like **/t/**.

Only add the extra "id" sound when the base word ends in **/t/** or **/d/**. Don't say *looked* as two syllables ("look-id"); it is one syllable: /lʊkt/.
:::

## Listen to the sound, not the spelling

The pronunciation rules depend on the **last sound you say**, not the **last letter you write**. A final **e** is often silent, **gh** is sometimes said as /f/, and **x** is said as /ks/.

| Word | Last letter | Real last sound | Ending sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| laugh → laughs, laughed | gh | /f/ | /s/, /t/ |
| like → likes, liked | e | /k/ | /s/, /t/ |
| change → changes, changed | e | /dʒ/ | /ɪz/, /d/ |
| dance → dances, danced | e | /s/ | /ɪz/, /t/ |
| close → closes, closed | e | /z/ | /ɪz/, /d/ |
| fix → fixes, fixed | x | /ks/ | /ɪz/, /t/ |
| month → months | th | /θ/ | /s/ |

- She **laughed** (say /t/) at my joke.<br>*Cô ấy cười vì câu đùa của tôi.*
- The shop **closes** (say /ɪz/) at 9.<br>*Cửa hàng đóng cửa lúc 9 giờ.*
- We **danced** (say /t/) all night.<br>*Chúng tôi nhảy suốt đêm.*
- My plans **changed** (say /d/) at the last minute.<br>*Kế hoạch của tôi thay đổi vào phút chót.*

## Adding -ing

Use it for the continuous tenses ([present continuous](./hien-tai-tiep-dien), [past continuous](./qua-khu-tiep-dien)…).

| Rule | Words | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Most words: add **-ing** | work → working<br>read → reading<br>play → playing<br>study → studying | He **is studying** for a test.<br>*Anh ấy đang ôn thi.* |
| Ending in a **silent -e**: drop the e, add **-ing** | make → making<br>write → writing<br>come → coming<br>dance → dancing | She **is making** a cake.<br>*Cô ấy đang làm bánh.* |
| Ending in **-ee**: keep it, add **-ing** | see → seeing<br>agree → agreeing | I'**m seeing** the doctor this afternoon.<br>*Chiều nay tôi đi khám bác sĩ.* |
| Ending in **-ie**: change to **-ying** | lie → lying<br>die → dying<br>tie → tying | The cat **is lying** on the sofa.<br>*Con mèo đang nằm trên ghế sofa.* |
| **Double the last consonant** (see below) | run → running<br>swim → swimming<br>sit → sitting<br>begin → beginning | The kids **are swimming** in the pool.<br>*Bọn trẻ đang bơi trong bể.* |

::: tip TIP
With -ing, **keep the y**: study → stud**ying**, cry → cr**ying** (it doesn't change to i as it does with -ed).
:::

## When to double the last consonant

Double the last consonant before **-ed** or **-ing** if the word ends in **1 vowel + 1 consonant** and **the stress is on the last syllable**.

| Case | Double it? | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 syllable, ending in 1 vowel + 1 consonant | **Yes** | stop → stopped, plan → planned, run → running, sit → sitting, shop → shopping |
| 1 syllable, ending in 2 vowels + 1 consonant | No | rain → rained, cook → cooked, sleep → sleeping |
| 1 syllable, ending in 2 consonants | No | help → helped, work → working, want → wanted |
| Ending in **w, x, y** | No | snow → snowed, fix → fixed, play → playing |
| 2 syllables, stress on the **last syllable** | **Yes** | pre**fer** → preferred, ad**mit** → admitted, be**gin** → beginning, for**get** → forgetting |
| 2 syllables, stress on the **first syllable** | No | **vis**it → visited, **o**pen → opened, **lis**ten → listened, **hap**pen → happened |

- They **planned** a trip to Ha Long Bay.<br>*Họ lên kế hoạch đi vịnh Hạ Long.*
- She **preferred** the blue dress.<br>*Cô ấy thích cái váy màu xanh hơn.*
- He **admitted** his mistake.<br>*Anh ấy thừa nhận sai lầm của mình.*
- We **visited** our old teacher last week.<br>*Tuần trước chúng tôi đến thăm thầy giáo cũ.*
- What **happened**? Why **are** you **crying**?<br>*Có chuyện gì vậy? Sao bạn lại khóc?*

::: tip TIP
*travel* has two spellings: **travelled** (British English) and **traveled** (American English). Both are correct; just be consistent.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She studys English. | She **studies** English. | Consonant + y changes to -ies. |
| He watchs TV. | He **watches** TV. | Words ending in -ch add -es. |
| The bus stoped. | The bus **stopped**. | 1 vowel + 1 consonant: double the consonant. |
| We visitted Hue. | We **visited** Hue. | *visit* is stressed on the first syllable, so don't double. |
| She is writeing. | She is **writing**. | Drop the silent *e* before -ing. |
:::

## In context

> My sister Lan **lives** in Da Nang. She **teaches** English and **studies** French at night. Last Sunday she **visited** us. She **stopped** at the market, **picked** up some fruit and **arrived** at noon. We **cooked** lunch together and **talked** for hours. Now she **is planning** her next visit, and my kids **are counting** the days.

Read the endings out loud: lives /z/, teaches /ɪz/, studies /z/, visited /ɪd/, stopped /t/, picked /t/, arrived /d/, cooked /t/, talked /t/.

::: details Vietnamese translation
Chị gái tôi tên Lan, sống ở Đà Nẵng. Chị dạy tiếng Anh và học tiếng Pháp vào buổi tối. Chủ nhật vừa rồi chị đến thăm chúng tôi. Chị ghé chợ mua ít hoa quả rồi đến nơi lúc trưa. Chúng tôi cùng nấu bữa trưa và nói chuyện hàng giờ liền. Giờ chị đang lên kế hoạch cho lần đến chơi tới, còn bọn trẻ nhà tôi thì đang đếm từng ngày.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **-s/-es**: add -es after s, ss, sh, ch, x, z, o; consonant + y becomes -ies. Say it as **/s/**, **/z/** or **/ɪz/**.
- **-ed**: words ending in e just add -d; consonant + y becomes -ied. Say it as **/t/**, **/d/** or **/ɪd/** (only after /t/ /d/).
- Pronunciation depends on the **last sound you say**, not the last letter.
- Double the last consonant when the word ends in 1 vowel + 1 consonant and the stress is on the last syllable: stop → stopped, prefer → preferred, but visit → visited.
:::
