# Present simple

Use it to talk about **habits**, **facts** and **fixed timetables**. This is the tense you use most when you introduce yourself and talk about your daily life.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + V / V(s/es) | She **works** in a bank. |
| Negative | S + do/does + not + V | She **doesn't work** on Sundays. |
| Question | Do/Does + S + V? | **Does** she **work** here? |
:::

### Verb forms by subject

| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / You / We / They | I **work**. | I **don't work**. | **Do** you **work**? |
| He / She / It | He **works**. | He **doesn't work**. | **Does** he **work**? |
| Singular nouns (Tom, my mother) | Tom **plays** chess. | Tom **doesn't play** chess. | **Does** Tom **play** chess? |
| Plural nouns (my friends) | My friends **live** here. | My friends **don't live** here. | **Do** your friends **live** here? |

### The verb be

| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I **am** tired. | I **am not** tired. | **Am** I late? |
| He / She / It | She **is** a nurse. | She **isn't** a nurse. | **Is** she a nurse? |
| You / We / They | They **are** at home. | They **aren't** at home. | **Are** they at home? |

::: warning IMPORTANT
With **he / she / it** (and every singular noun), the verb adds **-s / -es**. But once you have **does / doesn't**, the verb goes back to the **base form**.

She **works** → She doesn't **work** → Does she **work**?
:::

## How to use

### 1. Habits and repeated actions

- I **drink** coffee every morning.<br>*Tôi uống cà phê mỗi sáng.*
- My dad **goes** to work by bus.<br>*Bố tôi đi làm bằng xe buýt.*
- We **don't eat** out very often.<br>*Chúng tôi không hay ăn ngoài.*
- How often **do** you **exercise**? – Three times a week.<br>*Bạn tập thể dục bao lâu một lần? – Ba lần một tuần.*

### 2. Facts and things that are always true

- Water **boils** at 100°C.<br>*Nước sôi ở 100 độ C.*
- The sun **rises** in the east.<br>*Mặt trời mọc ở hướng đông.*
- Cats **don't like** water.<br>*Mèo không thích nước.*
- My sister **speaks** three languages.<br>*Chị tôi nói được ba thứ tiếng.*

### 3. Fixed timetables and schedules

- The train **leaves** at 7:30 tomorrow morning.<br>*Tàu khởi hành lúc 7 giờ 30 sáng mai.*
- The shop **opens** at 9 and **closes** at 10 pm.<br>*Cửa hàng mở cửa lúc 9 giờ và đóng cửa lúc 10 giờ tối.*
- What time **does** the film **start**?<br>*Phim bắt đầu lúc mấy giờ?*

::: tip TIP
Even when the sentence is about tomorrow (*tomorrow*), if it is a **set timetable** for a train, bus, cinema or class, you still use the present simple.
:::

## Signal words

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| always | luôn luôn | She **always** gets up early. |
| usually | thường xuyên | I **usually** walk to school. |
| often | thường | They **often** play tennis. |
| sometimes | thỉnh thoảng | He **sometimes** cooks dinner. |
| rarely / seldom | hiếm khi | We **rarely** watch TV. |
| never | không bao giờ | My mother **never** drinks coffee. |
| every day / week / year | mỗi ngày / tuần / năm | I visit my grandparents **every week**. |
| once / twice a week | một / hai lần một tuần | She goes swimming **twice a week**. |
| on Mondays | vào các thứ Hai | We have English **on Mondays**. |

### Where adverbs of frequency go

| Position | Example |
|---|---|
| **Before** an ordinary verb | She **often** walks to school. |
| **After** the verb be | He is **always** late. |
| Between the auxiliary and the main verb | I don't **usually** eat breakfast. |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She work in a bank. | She **works** in a bank. | A third-person singular subject needs -s on the verb. |
| Does she works here? | Does she **work** here? | Once you have *does*, the verb goes back to the base form. |
| He don't like tea. | He **doesn't** like tea. | *he* goes with *does*, not *do*. |
| I am agree with you. | I **agree** with you. | Don't use *be* together with an ordinary verb. |
| My friends lives here. | My friends **live** here. | With a plural subject, don't add -s. |
:::

## In context

> **Lan:** What **do** you **do**, Minh? \
> **Minh:** I'**m** a nurse. I **work** at Bach Mai Hospital. \
> **Lan:** **Do** you **work** at night? \
> **Minh:** Sometimes. I usually **start** at 7 in the morning and **finish** at 5. But I **work** nights once a week. \
> **Lan:** That's hard! What **does** your wife **do**? \
> **Minh:** She **teaches** English. She **doesn't work** at weekends, so we **spend** Sundays together.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Lan:** Anh làm nghề gì vậy, Minh?<br>
**Minh:** Tôi là y tá. Tôi làm ở bệnh viện Bạch Mai.<br>
**Lan:** Anh có làm ca đêm không?<br>
**Minh:** Thỉnh thoảng. Tôi thường bắt đầu lúc 7 giờ sáng và xong lúc 5 giờ. Nhưng mỗi tuần tôi làm một ca đêm.<br>
**Lan:** Vất vả thật! Vợ anh làm nghề gì?<br>
**Minh:** Cô ấy dạy tiếng Anh. Cô ấy không làm vào cuối tuần, nên chúng tôi dành ngày Chủ nhật cho nhau.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Use it for **habits**, **facts** and **timetables**.
- **he / she / it** → the verb adds **-s / -es**; negatives and questions use **does** + the base form.
- Adverbs of frequency go **before an ordinary verb** and **after be**.
:::
