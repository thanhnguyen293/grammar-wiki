# Perfect continuous and other future tenses

This lesson brings together 5 less common tenses: the two **perfect continuous** tenses (which stress how long something lasts) and three **future** tenses besides *will* and *be going to*. Learn them after you are confident with the [present perfect](./hien-tai-hoan-thanh) and the [future simple](./tuong-lai-don).

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Tense | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present perfect continuous | S + have/has been + V-ing | I **have been waiting** for an hour. |
| Past perfect continuous | S + had been + V-ing | I **had been waiting** for an hour when she came. |
| Future continuous | S + will be + V-ing | I **will be working** at 9 tomorrow. |
| Future perfect | S + will have + V3 | I **will have finished** by Friday. |
| Future perfect continuous | S + will have been + V-ing | By June, I **will have been working** here for a year. |
:::

## How to use

### 1. Present perfect continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + have/has been + V-ing | She **has been studying** all day. |
| Negative | S + haven't/hasn't been + V-ing | She **hasn't been sleeping** well. |
| Question | Have/Has + S + been + V-ing? | How long **have** you **been learning** English? |

Use it for something that **started in the past and has gone on without a break until now** (it may still be going on), stressing **how long**. Also use it for something that **has just stopped** but left signs you can see.

- I'**ve been waiting** for you for an hour!<br>*Tôi đợi bạn cả tiếng đồng hồ rồi đấy!*
- She **has been working** here since 2019.<br>*Cô ấy làm ở đây từ năm 2019 đến giờ.*
- It'**s been raining** all morning.<br>*Trời mưa suốt cả buổi sáng.*
- You're all wet! **Have** you **been running**?<br>*Người bạn ướt hết rồi! Bạn vừa chạy bộ à?*

### 2. Past perfect continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + had been + V-ing | They **had been driving** for hours. |
| Negative | S + hadn't been + V-ing | They **hadn't been driving** long. |
| Question | Had + S + been + V-ing? | How long **had** they **been driving**? |

Use it for something that **went on without a break up to a moment in the past**, usually before another action.

- I **had been waiting** for 30 minutes when the bus finally came.<br>*Tôi đã đợi 30 phút thì xe buýt mới đến.*
- She was tired because she **had been working** all day.<br>*Cô ấy mệt vì đã làm việc cả ngày.*
- The road was wet. It **had been raining**.<br>*Đường ướt. Trước đó trời đã mưa.*
- How long **had** you **been living** in Hue before you moved here?<br>*Trước khi chuyển đến đây bạn đã sống ở Huế bao lâu?*

### 3. Future continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + will be + V-ing | We **will be flying** to Da Nang. |
| Negative | S + won't be + V-ing | We **won't be flying** tomorrow. |
| Question | Will + S + be + V-ing? | **Will** you **be flying** tomorrow? |

Use it for something that **will be in progress at a moment in the future**. It is also a polite way to ask about someone's plans.

- At 8 tomorrow morning, I'**ll be flying** to Da Nang.<br>*8 giờ sáng mai, tôi sẽ đang bay ra Đà Nẵng.*
- This time next week, we'**ll be lying** on the beach.<br>*Giờ này tuần sau, chúng tôi sẽ đang nằm dài trên bãi biển.*
- Don't call me at 3. I'**ll be having** a meeting.<br>*Đừng gọi tôi lúc 3 giờ nhé. Lúc đó tôi đang họp.*
- **Will** you **be using** the car tonight?<br>*Tối nay anh có dùng xe không?*

### 4. Future perfect

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + will have + V3 | I **will have finished** the report. |
| Negative | S + won't have + V3 | I **won't have finished** it by then. |
| Question | Will + S + have + V3? | **Will** you **have finished** by 5? |

Use it for something that **will be finished before a point in the future**. It often goes with **by + time** (before…, by…).

- By 6 pm, I **will have finished** this report.<br>*Trước 6 giờ tối, tôi sẽ làm xong bản báo cáo này.*
- By next year, they **will have built** the new bridge.<br>*Đến năm sau, họ sẽ xây xong cây cầu mới.*
- By the end of this month, I'**ll have saved** five million dong.<br>*Đến cuối tháng này, tôi sẽ để dành được năm triệu đồng.*
- Don't hurry. The film **won't have started** by the time we get there.<br>*Đừng vội. Lúc mình đến nơi thì phim vẫn chưa chiếu đâu.*

### 5. Future perfect continuous

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + will have been + V-ing | She **will have been teaching** for 20 years. |
| Negative | S + won't have been + V-ing | She **won't have been teaching** long. |
| Question | Will + S + have been + V-ing? | How long **will** she **have been teaching**? |

Use it to say **how long something will have lasted by a point in the future**. This tense is very rare in conversation; you only need to recognize it.

- By next June, I **will have been working** here for ten years.<br>*Đến tháng Sáu năm sau là tôi làm ở đây được mười năm.*
- By 5 pm, she **will have been studying** for six hours.<br>*Đến 5 giờ chiều là cô ấy đã học được sáu tiếng.*
- In October, we **will have been living** in this house for three years.<br>*Đến tháng Mười là chúng tôi sống ở ngôi nhà này được ba năm.*

## Perfect or perfect continuous?

The **perfect** (have + V3) stresses **the result, that it is finished, how much you did**. The **perfect continuous** (have been + V-ing) stresses **the process, how long it lasted**; the action may not be finished.

| Perfect: result | Perfect continuous: process |
|---|---|
| I'**ve painted** the room.<br>*Tôi sơn xong căn phòng rồi.* (finished, the room looks nice) | I'**ve been painting** the room.<br>*Tôi đang sơn căn phòng.* (painting all day, paint on my clothes, maybe not finished) |
| I'**ve read** three books this month.<br>*Tháng này tôi đọc được ba cuốn sách.* (how many) | I'**ve been reading** this book all morning.<br>*Tôi đọc cuốn sách này suốt buổi sáng.* (how long) |
| She **has written** five emails.<br>*Cô ấy viết được năm cái email rồi.* | She **has been writing** emails since 9 am.<br>*Cô ấy ngồi viết email từ 9 giờ sáng.* |
| He **had repaired** the car when I arrived.<br>*Khi tôi đến thì anh ấy đã sửa xong xe.* | He **had been repairing** the car, so his hands were dirty.<br>*Anh ấy sửa xe nãy giờ nên tay bẩn hết.* |

::: warning IMPORTANT
State verbs (know, like, want, have = own…) are **not** used in the continuous. Use the perfect instead.

I'**ve known** him for ten years. (don't write *I've been knowing*)
:::

## Signal words

| Tense | Common words / phrases | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present perfect continuous | for, since, all day, how long | **How long** have you been waiting? |
| Past perfect continuous | for + length of time, when + past simple, before | She'd been crying **when** I saw her. |
| Future continuous | at this time tomorrow, this time next week, at + time + tomorrow | **This time tomorrow** I'll be sitting on a plane. |
| Future perfect | by + future point in time, by the time, before | We'll have left **by the time** you arrive. |
| Future perfect continuous | by + future point in time + for + length of time | **By May**, I'll have been living here **for** a year. |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I have been wait for an hour. | I have been **waiting** for an hour. | After *been* you need V-ing. |
| I am waiting here since 8 o'clock. | I'**ve been waiting** here since 8 o'clock. | Something lasting from the past until now (*since*) doesn't take the present continuous. |
| I've been knowing her for years. | I'**ve known** her for years. | *know* is a state verb. |
| By Friday, I will finish the work. | By Friday, I **will have finished** the work. | *by* + a future point in time takes the future perfect. |
| This time tomorrow I will lie on the beach. | This time tomorrow I **will be lying** on the beach. | Something in progress at a moment in the future takes the future continuous. |
:::

## In context

> **Hanh:** You look tired. What **have** you **been doing**? \
> **Quang:** I'**ve been packing** all afternoon. We're moving to Can Tho tomorrow. \
> **Hanh:** Wow! **Will** you **be driving** there? \
> **Quang:** Yes. At this time tomorrow, we'**ll be driving** down the highway. \
> **Hanh:** Didn't you use to live there? \
> **Quang:** Yes. I **had been living** there for ten years before I moved to Saigon. And by next month, I'**ll have started** my new job there.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Hạnh:** Trông anh mệt thế. Anh làm gì nãy giờ vậy?<br>
**Quang:** Tôi dọn đồ cả buổi chiều. Mai chúng tôi chuyển xuống Cần Thơ.<br>
**Hạnh:** Ồ! Anh lái xe xuống đó à?<br>
**Quang:** Ừ. Giờ này ngày mai, chúng tôi sẽ đang chạy trên đường cao tốc.<br>
**Hạnh:** Hồi trước anh từng sống ở đó phải không?<br>
**Quang:** Đúng rồi. Tôi đã sống ở đó mười năm trước khi chuyển lên Sài Gòn. Và trước tháng sau là tôi đã bắt đầu công việc mới ở đó rồi.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **have/has been + V-ing**: lasting until now. **had been + V-ing**: lasting until a moment in the past.
- **will be + V-ing**: will be in progress at a moment in the future.
- **will have + V3**: will be finished before a future point in time (often with **by**).
- The perfect stresses the **result**; the perfect continuous stresses the **process, how long**.
:::
