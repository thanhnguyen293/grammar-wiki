# The 8 parts of speech

Every English word belongs to a **part of speech**: noun, verb, adjective… When you know a word's part of speech, you know **where it goes** in a sentence and whether it **changes form**. This is the foundation for every grammar lesson that follows.

## The 8 parts of speech

| Part of speech | What it does in a sentence | Example words | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noun | Names a person, thing, place or idea | mother, phone, Hanoi, love | My **mother** has a new **phone**. |
| Pronoun | Takes the place of a noun so you don't repeat it | I, you, he, she, it, they | Nam is tired, so **he** goes to bed early. |
| Verb | Shows an action or a state | eat, go, work, be, have | We **eat** dinner at 7. |
| Adjective | Describes a noun: what it is like | big, cheap, happy, old | This is a **cheap** hotel. |
| Adverb | Describes a verb or adjective: how, how much, how often | slowly, very, often, here | She speaks **slowly**. |
| Preposition | Shows place, time or direction | in, on, at, under, to | The keys are **on** the table. |
| Conjunction | Joins words, phrases or clauses | and, but, or, because, so | I like tea, **but** my wife likes coffee. |
| Interjection | Shows a feeling, often stands alone | oh, wow, ouch, hey | **Wow**, your house is beautiful! |

::: tip TIP
The articles **a / an / the** are often grouped with adjectives because they always come before a noun. They have their own lesson: [Articles: a / an / the](./mao-tu).
:::

## Each part of speech through examples

### 1. Nouns: people, things, places, ideas

- My **brother** is a **doctor**.<br>*Anh trai tôi là bác sĩ.*
- I left my **bag** in the **car**.<br>*Tôi để quên túi trong xe ô tô.*
- **Hanoi** is famous for its **food**.<br>*Hà Nội nổi tiếng về đồ ăn.*
- **Health** is more important than **money**.<br>*Sức khỏe quan trọng hơn tiền bạc.*

### 2. Pronouns: take the place of nouns

- Lan is my neighbor. **She** is very kind.<br>*Lan là hàng xóm của tôi. Cô ấy rất tốt bụng.*
- I bought a new shirt, but **it** is too small.<br>*Tôi mua một chiếc áo sơ mi mới, nhưng nó bị chật.*
- My parents live in Hue. I visit **them** every month.<br>*Bố mẹ tôi sống ở Huế. Tháng nào tôi cũng về thăm bố mẹ.*

### 3. Verbs: actions and states

- I **get up** at 6 and **go** to work at 7.<br>*Tôi dậy lúc 6 giờ và đi làm lúc 7 giờ.*
- She **is** a teacher.<br>*Cô ấy là giáo viên.*
- We **have** two cats.<br>*Chúng tôi có hai con mèo.*
- My son **loves** chocolate.<br>*Con trai tôi rất thích sô-cô-la.*

### 4. Adjectives: describe nouns

- She lives in a **small** house.<br>*Cô ấy sống trong một ngôi nhà nhỏ.*
- The soup is **hot**.<br>*Món súp còn nóng.*
- My boss is **young** but very **smart**.<br>*Sếp tôi còn trẻ nhưng rất thông minh.*

### 5. Adverbs: how, how much, how often

- Please drive **carefully**.<br>*Làm ơn lái xe cẩn thận.*
- I am **very** tired today.<br>*Hôm nay tôi rất mệt.*
- He **often** goes swimming.<br>*Anh ấy thường đi bơi.*
- Come **here**, please.<br>*Lại đây nào.*

### 6. Prepositions: place, time, direction

- The cat is **under** the bed.<br>*Con mèo ở dưới gầm giường.*
- I work **from** Monday **to** Friday.<br>*Tôi làm việc từ thứ Hai đến thứ Sáu.*
- We met **at** the coffee shop.<br>*Chúng tôi gặp nhau ở quán cà phê.*

### 7. Conjunctions: join ideas

- I want to go out, **but** it is raining.<br>*Tôi muốn ra ngoài, nhưng trời đang mưa.*
- Do you want tea **or** coffee?<br>*Bạn muốn uống trà hay cà phê?*
- She stayed home **because** she was sick.<br>*Cô ấy ở nhà vì bị ốm.*

### 8. Interjections: show feelings

- **Oh**, I forgot my keys!<br>*Ôi, tôi quên chìa khóa rồi!*
- **Ouch**! That hurts.<br>*Á! Đau quá.*
- **Hey**, wait for me!<br>*Này, đợi tôi với!*

## Recognizing parts of speech by their endings

Many words have an **ending** (suffix) that tells you their part of speech. When you meet a new word, look at the ending to guess where it goes in a sentence.

| Part of speech | Common endings | Example words | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noun | -tion / -sion | education, decision | **Education** is very important. |
| Noun | -ment | apartment, payment | Our **apartment** is on the fifth floor. |
| Noun | -ness | happiness, kindness | Thank you for your **kindness**. |
| Noun for a person | -er / -or / -ist | driver, actor, dentist | My uncle is a taxi **driver**. |
| Adjective | -ful / -less | useful, careless | This app is very **useful**. |
| Adjective | -ous | famous, dangerous | This road is **dangerous** at night. |
| Adjective | -al / -ive | national, expensive | This phone is too **expensive**. |
| Adjective | -able / -ible | comfortable, possible | The new sofa is very **comfortable**. |
| Adjective | -y | sunny, healthy | It's a **sunny** day. |
| Verb | -ize, -en, -ify | apologize, widen, simplify | I **apologize** for being late. |
| Adverb | -ly | quickly, slowly, carefully | He finished his work **quickly**. |

### One word, several parts of speech

Endings are only a hint. Some words keep the same form but change their part of speech depending on their **position** in the sentence:

- I **work** in a bank. (verb)<br>*Tôi làm việc ở ngân hàng.*
- I have a lot of **work** today. (noun)<br>*Hôm nay tôi có nhiều việc.*
- He has a **fast** car. (adjective)<br>*Anh ấy có một chiếc xe chạy nhanh.*
- He drives **fast**. (adverb)<br>*Anh ấy lái xe nhanh.*

## Basic word positions

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Noun phrase | (a / an / the) + adjective + noun | a **big** house |
| Adjective after be | S + be + adjective | The room is **clean**. |
| Adverb after the verb | S + V (+ object) + adverb | She drives the car **slowly**. |
| Adverb before an adjective | adverb + adjective | **very** tired |
:::

### 1. Article + adjective + noun

- I live in **a small house**.<br>*Tôi sống trong một ngôi nhà nhỏ.*
- She bought **a red dress**.<br>*Cô ấy mua một chiếc váy đỏ.*
- **The new teacher** is very nice.<br>*Cô giáo mới rất dễ mến.*
- We stayed at **an old hotel**.<br>*Chúng tôi ở một khách sạn cũ.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
Vietnamese says *ngôi nhà **nhỏ***, with the adjective **after** the noun. English is the opposite: the adjective comes **before** the noun.

nhà nhỏ → a **small** house (not *a house small*)
:::

### 2. Adjectives after be

- The coffee **is hot**.<br>*Cà phê nóng.*
- My children **are happy**.<br>*Các con tôi vui lắm.*
- This bag **is cheap**.<br>*Cái túi này rẻ.*

### 3. Adverbs after the verb (or after the object)

- She sings **beautifully**.<br>*Cô ấy hát hay.*
- He speaks English **well**.<br>*Anh ấy nói tiếng Anh giỏi.*
- Please close the door **quietly**.<br>*Làm ơn đóng cửa nhẹ nhàng.*
- They walked **slowly** to the park.<br>*Họ đi bộ chầm chậm ra công viên.*

### 4. Adverbs before an adjective or another adverb

- I'm **very** hungry.<br>*Tôi đói lắm.*
- The test was **really** easy.<br>*Bài kiểm tra thật sự dễ.*
- She speaks **too** fast.<br>*Cô ấy nói nhanh quá.*

## Breaking down a sentence

**Wow! The little boy cooks very well, and he often makes dinner for us.**<br>*Chà! Cậu bé ấy nấu ăn rất giỏi, và cậu thường nấu bữa tối cho chúng tôi.*

| Word | Part of speech | Role in the sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Wow | Interjection | Shows surprise |
| The | Article (adjective group) | Shows which boy |
| little | Adjective | Describes *boy*: small |
| boy | Noun | The person doing the action |
| cooks | Verb | The action: cooking |
| very | Adverb | Modifies *well*: very |
| well | Adverb | Modifies *cooks*: how he cooks |
| and | Conjunction | Joins the two clauses |
| he | Pronoun | Takes the place of *the little boy* |
| often | Adverb | How often: usually |
| makes | Verb | The action: make, cook |
| dinner | Noun | The thing that is cooked |
| for | Preposition | For whom |
| us | Pronoun | Takes the place of *the speaker and others* |

A second, shorter sentence:

**My new neighbor sings beautifully in the evening.**<br>*Người hàng xóm mới của tôi hát rất hay vào buổi tối.*

| Word | Part of speech | Other words of the same type |
|---|---|---|
| My | Possessive adjective (pronoun group) | your, his, her |
| new | Adjective | old, nice, quiet |
| neighbor | Noun | friend, teacher, boss |
| sings | Verb | cooks, dances, works |
| beautifully | Adverb | loudly, badly, well |
| in | Preposition | on, at, after |
| the | Article | a, an |
| evening | Noun | morning, afternoon |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She has a car red. | She has a **red car**. | The adjective comes before the noun. |
| He speaks English very good. | He speaks English very **well**. | To describe how he speaks (a verb), use an adverb. |
| She drives careful. | She drives **carefully**. | To describe the verb *drives*, you need an adverb ending in -ly. |
| He is very kindness. | He is very **kind**. | After *be* + *very* you need an adjective; *kindness* is a noun. |
| I very like coffee. | I **really like** coffee. | *very* does not go before a verb. |
:::

## In context

The words in bold are **adjectives** and **adverbs**, the two parts of speech learners mix up most often.

> **Hoa:** Your new apartment is really **beautiful**, Nam! \
> **Nam:** Thanks. It's **small**, but it's very **quiet**. \
> **Hoa:** And this soup is **delicious**. You cook so **well**! \
> **Nam:** I just cook **slowly** and **carefully**. That's my secret. \
> **Hoa:** Can you teach me? My food always tastes **terrible**. \
> **Nam:** Sure. Come **here** on Sunday and we can cook together.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Hoa:** Căn hộ mới của anh đẹp quá, Nam ơi!<br>
**Nam:** Cảm ơn em. Nó nhỏ nhưng rất yên tĩnh.<br>
**Hoa:** Món súp này cũng ngon nữa. Anh nấu ăn giỏi thật!<br>
**Nam:** Anh chỉ nấu từ từ và cẩn thận thôi. Bí quyết của anh đấy.<br>
**Hoa:** Anh dạy em được không? Đồ em nấu lúc nào cũng dở tệ.<br>
**Nam:** Được chứ. Chủ nhật em qua đây rồi mình cùng nấu.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- The 8 parts of speech: **noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection**.
- Adjectives go **before a noun** or **after be**: *a **cheap** hotel*, *the hotel is **cheap***.
- Adverbs go **after a verb** or **before an adjective**: *speak **slowly***, ***very** tired*.
- Look at **word endings** (-tion, -ful, -ly…) to guess the part of speech, but the position in the sentence decides.
:::
