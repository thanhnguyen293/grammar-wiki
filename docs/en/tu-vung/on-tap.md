# Review quiz

Review the words from the **Today's words** lists of earlier days. Each question shows the Vietnamese meaning, the part of speech and a translated example; type the English word and press Enter.

::: tip TIP
The underscores show how many letters the word has. If you're stuck, click **Show first letter**. British and American spellings (*colour* / *color*) both count as correct. If you typed a synonym and it was marked wrong, click **My word is also right**.
:::
