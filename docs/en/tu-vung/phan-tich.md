# Learn from an article

Paste a paragraph, news article, email or document in English. The wiki finds the words and phrases in it, reduces them to their dictionary form (*went* → *go*, *children* → *child*), and keeps phrases such as *give up*, *look forward to* and *turn the light off* together as one row instead of splitting them into single words.

::: tip HOW TO USE
- Mark each word **Know** or **Don't know**. "Know" also counts as "learned" on the topic pages.
- Click **+ Today** (or the button that adds every "Don't know" word) to put words into [Today's words](./hom-nay).
- For words that are not in the wiki's dictionary, the add form looks up the IPA, part of speech and a suggested Vietnamese meaning; check them and save to [My words](./cua-toi).
:::
