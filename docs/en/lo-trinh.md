# Study path

You don't need to study chapter 1 to chapter 9 in order. Each lesson has a label:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <span class="lv v1">Round 1</span> | Study first, roughly the first 3 months. Enough to build simple, correct sentences. |
| <span class="lv v2">Round 2</span> | After round 1: clauses, conditionals, reported speech and the remaining tenses. |
| <span class="lv tc">Reference</span> | No need to memorise. Open it when you meet it. |

::: tip TIP
Study one lesson a day for 30–45 minutes: read the theory, copy 3 examples, write 3 sentences about yourself, then do the matching exercises in the book. Move on when you get at least 80% right.
:::
