# Negatives and questions

How to say "**not**" and how to **ask questions** in English. The secret is the **auxiliary verb** (be, do, does, did, can, have, will…): for a negative, add **not** after it; for a question, move it **to the front**.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Negative | S + auxiliary + **not** + V | She **doesn't like** fish.<br>*Cô ấy không thích cá.* |
| Yes / No question | **Auxiliary** + S + V? | **Does** she **like** fish?<br>*Cô ấy có thích cá không?* |
| Wh- question | **Wh-** + auxiliary + S + V? | What **does** she **like**?<br>*Cô ấy thích gì?* |
| Subject question | **Who / What** + V? (no do) | **Who likes** fish?<br>*Ai thích cá?* |
:::

### The auxiliary for each type of sentence

| Sentence type | Positive | Negative | Question + short answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| be (present) | She **is** a teacher.<br>*Cô ấy là giáo viên.* | She **isn't** a teacher. | **Is** she a teacher? – Yes, she **is**. |
| be (past) | They **were** at home.<br>*Họ đã ở nhà.* | They **weren't** at home. | **Were** they at home? – No, they **weren't**. |
| Present simple | He **plays** football.<br>*Anh ấy chơi bóng đá.* | He **doesn't play** football. | **Does** he **play** football? – Yes, he **does**. |
| Past simple | You **called** me.<br>*Bạn đã gọi cho tôi.* | You **didn't call** me. | **Did** you **call** me? – Yes, I **did**. |
| Modal verb | I **can** swim.<br>*Tôi biết bơi.* | I **can't** swim. | **Can** you swim? – Yes, I **can**. |
| Present perfect | She **has finished**.<br>*Cô ấy đã làm xong.* | She **hasn't finished**. | **Has** she **finished**? – No, she **hasn't**. |
| Future simple | It **will rain**.<br>*Trời sẽ mưa.* | It **won't rain**. | **Will** it **rain**? – Yes, it **will**. |

## How to use

### 1. The verb be: add not after be, move be to the front

- I'**m not** hungry.<br>*Tôi không đói.*
- He **isn't** at work today.<br>*Hôm nay anh ấy không đi làm.*
- **Are** you ready? – Yes, I **am**.<br>*Bạn sẵn sàng chưa? – Rồi.*
- **Was** the film good? – No, it **wasn't**.<br>*Phim có hay không? – Không hay.*

### 2. Present simple: borrow do / does

- I **don't eat** meat.<br>*Tôi không ăn thịt.*
- My son **doesn't like** milk.<br>*Con trai tôi không thích sữa.*
- **Do** you **live** near here? – Yes, I **do**.<br>*Bạn sống gần đây à? – Đúng vậy.*
- **Does** this bus **go** to the station? – No, it **doesn't**.<br>*Xe buýt này có đến nhà ga không? – Không.*

### 3. Past simple: borrow did

- We **didn't go** out last night.<br>*Tối qua chúng tôi không ra ngoài.*
- She **didn't buy** anything.<br>*Cô ấy không mua gì cả.*
- **Did** you **see** the news? – Yes, I **did**.<br>*Bạn xem tin tức chưa? – Xem rồi.*
- **Did** they **win**? – No, they **didn't**.<br>*Họ có thắng không? – Không.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
Once you have **do / does / did**, the main verb goes back to its **base form** (no *-s*, no *-ed*, no past form).

She **went** home. → She didn't **go** home. → Did she **go** home?
:::

### 4. Modal verbs (can, should, must, may…): add not, move to the front

Don't borrow *do*. A modal verb is always followed by the base verb, without *to*.

- I **can't** drive.<br>*Tôi không biết lái xe.*
- You **shouldn't** eat so much sugar.<br>*Bạn không nên ăn nhiều đường như vậy.*
- **Can** you help me? – Sure, I **can**.<br>*Bạn giúp tôi được không? – Được chứ.*
- **Should** I call her? – Yes, you **should**.<br>*Tôi có nên gọi cho cô ấy không? – Có, bạn nên gọi.*

### 5. have (present perfect) and will

- I **haven't seen** that film.<br>*Tôi chưa xem bộ phim đó.*
- **Have** you ever **been** to Hue? – No, I **haven't**.<br>*Bạn đã bao giờ đến Huế chưa? – Chưa.*
- She **won't** be home late.<br>*Cô ấy sẽ không về nhà muộn.*
- **Will** you **come** to my party? – Yes, I **will**.<br>*Bạn sẽ đến dự tiệc của tôi chứ? – Có, tôi sẽ đến.*

::: tip TIP
When **have** means "to own / to have" (a main verb), negatives and questions use **do / does / did**: I **don't have** a car. *(Tôi không có xe hơi.)* – **Do** you **have** a pen? *(Bạn có bút không?)*
:::

## Wh- questions

Structure: **Question word + auxiliary + S + V?** Just put the question word in front of a Yes / No question.

Do you live in Hanoi? → **Where** do you live? *(Bạn sống ở đâu?)*

| Question word | Asks about | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | things | **What** do you do?<br>*Bạn làm nghề gì?* | I'm a nurse.<br>*Tôi là y tá.* |
| Where | places | **Where** do you live?<br>*Bạn sống ở đâu?* | In Hanoi.<br>*Ở Hà Nội.* |
| When | time | **When** is your birthday?<br>*Sinh nhật bạn là khi nào?* | It's in May.<br>*Vào tháng Năm.* |
| What time | clock time | **What time** does the shop open?<br>*Cửa hàng mấy giờ mở cửa?* | At 8 a.m.<br>*Lúc 8 giờ sáng.* |
| Who | people | **Who** did you meet?<br>*Bạn đã gặp ai?* | My old teacher.<br>*Cô giáo cũ của tôi.* |
| Whose | owner | **Whose** bag is this?<br>*Cái túi này của ai?* | It's Lan's.<br>*Của Lan.* |
| Which | which one (from a choice) | **Which** colour do you want, red or blue?<br>*Bạn muốn màu nào, đỏ hay xanh?* | Blue, please.<br>*Màu xanh.* |
| Why | reasons | **Why** are you late?<br>*Sao bạn đến muộn?* | Because I missed the bus.<br>*Vì tôi lỡ xe buýt.* |
| How | the way, the means | **How** do you go to work?<br>*Bạn đi làm bằng gì?* | By motorbike.<br>*Bằng xe máy.* |
| How much | how much (price, uncountable) | **How much** is this shirt?<br>*Cái áo này bao nhiêu tiền?* | 200,000 dong.<br>*200.000 đồng.* |
| How many | how many (countable) | **How many** children do you have?<br>*Bạn có mấy con?* | Two.<br>*Hai đứa.* |
| How old | age | **How old** is your son?<br>*Con trai bạn mấy tuổi?* | He's seven.<br>*Cháu bảy tuổi.* |
| How long | length of time | **How long** does it take to get there?<br>*Đến đó mất bao lâu?* | About 20 minutes.<br>*Khoảng 20 phút.* |
| How often | how many times | **How often** do you exercise?<br>*Bạn tập thể dục bao lâu một lần?* | Twice a week.<br>*Hai lần một tuần.* |

## Subject questions (no do)

When **Who / What / Which** is itself the **subject** (the person / thing doing the action), the question keeps statement word order and does **not** use *do / does / did*. The verb takes its normal form.

Compare each pair:

- **Who called** you? – **Minh** called me.<br>*Ai đã gọi cho bạn? – Minh gọi cho tôi.* (*Who* is the caller = subject)
- **Who did** you **call**? – I called **Minh**.<br>*Bạn đã gọi cho ai? – Tôi gọi cho Minh.* (*Who* is the person called = object)
- **What happened**? – I fell off my bike.<br>*Chuyện gì đã xảy ra vậy? – Tôi bị ngã xe đạp.*
- **Who lives** in that house? – An old man.<br>*Ai sống trong ngôi nhà đó? – Một ông cụ.*
- **Which bus goes** to the airport? – Number 86.<br>*Xe buýt nào ra sân bay? – Xe số 86.*
- **How many people came** to the party? – About twenty.<br>*Có bao nhiêu người đến dự tiệc? – Khoảng hai mươi người.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
Asking **who did it** → no *do*: **Who broke** the glass? *(Ai làm vỡ cái cốc?)*<br>
Asking **who / what it was done to** → with *do*: **What did** you **break**? *(Bạn đã làm vỡ cái gì?)*
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She don't like coffee. | She **doesn't** like coffee. | *she* goes with *does*. |
| Did you went to school? | Did you **go** to school? | After *did*, the verb goes back to its base form. |
| Where you live? | Where **do** you live? | A Wh- question still needs an auxiliary. |
| Who did call you? | Who **called** you? | When *Who* is the subject, don't use *did*. |
| Can you to help me? | Can you **help** me? | No *to* after *can*. |
:::

## In context

> **Mr. Hung:** Hello! **Are** you the new neighbour? \
> **Anna:** Yes, I **am**. I moved in last week. \
> **Mr. Hung:** Welcome! **Where do** you **come** from? \
> **Anna:** From Australia. I **don't speak** much Vietnamese yet. **Who lives** in the house opposite? \
> **Mr. Hung:** My sister. **Have** you **met** her? \
> **Anna:** No, I **haven't**. **Can** you **introduce** us? \
> **Mr. Hung:** Of course. **Will** you **be** free this evening?

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Negative: auxiliary + **not**; question: move the auxiliary **to the front**.
- A normal verb with no auxiliary borrows **do / does / did**, and the main verb goes back to its **base form**.
- Wh- questions: **question word + auxiliary + S + V?**
- When you ask about the **subject** (Who called?), do **not** use *do*.
:::
