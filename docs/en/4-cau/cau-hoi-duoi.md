# Question tags

A question tag is a short question added to the **end of a sentence**, like "…, **phải không**?" or "…, **nhỉ**?" in Vietnamese. People use it to **check** something they think is true, or to **invite** the other person to agree and keep the conversation going.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive sentence → negative tag | S + V …, auxiliary + **n't** + pronoun? | You **are** tired, **aren't you**?<br>*Bạn mệt rồi, phải không?* |
| Negative sentence → positive tag | S + auxiliary + **not** + V …, auxiliary + pronoun? | She **doesn't** eat meat, **does she**?<br>*Cô ấy không ăn thịt, phải không?* |
:::

::: warning IMPORTANT
- A **positive** sentence → a **negative** tag; a **negative** sentence → a **positive** tag.
- The tag reuses the **auxiliary** of the main sentence (be, do / does / did, have, will, can…).
- The tag always uses a **pronoun** (he, she, it, they…), never the name or noun again.
:::

### Choosing the auxiliary for the tag

| Main sentence | Tag uses | Example |
|---|---|---|
| be | am / is / are / was / were | It's cold today, **isn't it**?<br>*Hôm nay trời lạnh nhỉ?* |
| Present simple | do / does | You like coffee, **don't you**?<br>*Bạn thích cà phê, phải không?* |
| Past simple | did | He went home early, **didn't he**?<br>*Anh ấy về sớm, phải không?* |
| Present perfect | have / has | You've been to Da Lat, **haven't you**?<br>*Bạn từng đến Đà Lạt rồi, phải không?* |
| will | will | They'll come, **won't they**?<br>*Họ sẽ đến, phải không?* |
| Modal verb | can / should / must… | She can swim, **can't she**?<br>*Cô ấy biết bơi, phải không?* |

## How to use

### 1. Positive sentence → negative tag

- You're a student, **aren't you**?<br>*Bạn là sinh viên, phải không?*
- Your brother works in a bank, **doesn't he**?<br>*Anh trai bạn làm ở ngân hàng, phải không?*
- It rained a lot yesterday, **didn't it**?<br>*Hôm qua mưa nhiều nhỉ?*
- We can park here, **can't we**?<br>*Mình đỗ xe ở đây được, phải không?*

### 2. Negative sentence → positive tag

- She isn't married, **is she**?<br>*Cô ấy chưa lập gia đình, phải không?*
- You don't eat beef, **do you**?<br>*Bạn không ăn thịt bò, phải không?*
- They didn't call, **did they**?<br>*Họ không gọi, phải không?*
- You haven't finished yet, **have you**?<br>*Bạn vẫn chưa xong, phải không?*

### 3. The tag uses a pronoun instead of the noun

- Your mother is a teacher, isn't **she**?<br>*Mẹ bạn là giáo viên, phải không?*
- The film was good, wasn't **it**?<br>*Bộ phim hay nhỉ?*
- Minh and Lan are coming, aren't **they**?<br>*Minh và Lan sẽ đến, phải không?*
- This is your car, isn't **it**?<br>*Đây là xe của bạn, phải không?* (*this / that* → **it**)
- These are your keys, aren't **they**?<br>*Đây là chìa khóa của bạn, phải không?* (*these / those* → **they**)

## Special cases

| Main sentence | Tag | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I am | **aren't I** | I'm late, **aren't I**?<br>*Tôi đến muộn rồi, phải không?* |
| Let's … | **shall we** | Let's go out for dinner, **shall we**?<br>*Mình đi ăn tối ở ngoài nhé?* |
| Commands | **will you** | Close the door, **will you**?<br>*Đóng cửa giúp tôi nhé?* |
| There is / There are | **is there / are there** | There's a bank near here, **isn't there**?<br>*Gần đây có ngân hàng, phải không?* |
| never, hardly, seldom, no… | **positive** tag | He never drinks beer, **does he**?<br>*Anh ấy không bao giờ uống bia, phải không?* |
| everyone, someone, nobody… | **they** | Everyone is here, **aren't they**?<br>*Mọi người đến đủ rồi, phải không?* |
| everything, nothing | **it** | Nothing is wrong, **is it**?<br>*Không có gì sai, phải không?* |
| have (= own, main verb) | **do / does / did** | You have a car, **don't you**?<br>*Bạn có xe hơi, phải không?* |

### I am → aren't I

There is no form *amn't*, so people use **aren't I**. The negative sentence *I'm not* still takes **am I**.

- I'm right, **aren't I**?<br>*Tôi nói đúng, phải không?*
- I'm your best friend, **aren't I**?<br>*Tớ là bạn thân nhất của cậu, phải không?*
- I'm not late, **am I**?<br>*Tôi không đến muộn đấy chứ?*

### Let's → shall we

- Let's take a break, **shall we**?<br>*Mình nghỉ một lát nhé?*
- Let's watch a film tonight, **shall we**?<br>*Tối nay mình xem phim nhé?*
- Let's start now, **shall we**?<br>*Mình bắt đầu luôn nhé?*

### Commands → will you

The tag **will you** (or *can you, would you*) makes a request sound softer and more polite.

- Open the window, **will you**?<br>*Mở cửa sổ giúp tôi nhé?*
- Don't tell anyone, **will you**?<br>*Đừng nói với ai nhé?*
- Pass me the salt, **would you**?<br>*Đưa tôi lọ muối được không?*

### There is / There are → there

- There's a problem, **isn't there**?<br>*Có vấn đề gì rồi, phải không?*
- There are some eggs in the fridge, **aren't there**?<br>*Trong tủ lạnh còn ít trứng, phải không?*
- There wasn't much traffic, **was there**?<br>*Đường không đông lắm, phải không?*

### never / hardly / seldom / no → positive tag

These words already have a **negative** meaning, so the sentence counts as negative and the tag must be **positive**.

- She never eats breakfast, **does she**?<br>*Cô ấy không bao giờ ăn sáng, phải không?*
- You hardly know him, **do you**?<br>*Bạn hầu như không quen anh ta, phải không?*
- He seldom calls you, **does he**?<br>*Anh ấy hiếm khi gọi cho bạn, phải không?*
- There's no milk left, **is there**?<br>*Hết sữa rồi, phải không?*

### everyone / someone / nobody → they

- Everyone loved the food, **didn't they**?<br>*Ai cũng thích món ăn, phải không?*
- Somebody called me, **didn't they**?<br>*Có ai gọi cho tôi, phải không?*
- Nobody came, **did they**?<br>*Không ai đến, phải không?* (*nobody* has a negative meaning → positive tag)

### everything / nothing → it

- Everything is ready, **isn't it**?<br>*Mọi thứ đã sẵn sàng, phải không?*
- Nothing happened, **did it**?<br>*Không có chuyện gì xảy ra, phải không?*
- Nothing is free, **is it**?<br>*Chẳng có gì miễn phí cả, phải không?*

## Intonation: falling or rising

The same sentence can mean different things depending on your voice.

| Intonation on the tag | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Falling** ↘ | You are **sure**; you just want the other person to agree, or to start a chat. | It's a lovely day, **isn't it** ↘?<br>*Hôm nay trời đẹp quá nhỉ.* |
| **Rising** ↗ | You are **not sure**; it's a real question and you wait for an answer. | You're from Hue, **aren't you** ↗?<br>*Bạn là người Huế, phải không?* |

- The food here is great, **isn't it** ↘?<br>*Đồ ăn ở đây ngon nhỉ.* (you know it's good and just want the other person to agree)
- You've locked the door, **haven't you** ↗?<br>*Anh khóa cửa rồi, phải không?* (you're not sure and need the other person to confirm)
- You're coming tomorrow, **aren't you** ↘?<br>*Mai bạn đến nhé.* (you're sure the other person is coming)
- You're coming tomorrow, **aren't you** ↗?<br>*Mai bạn có đến không?* (you really don't know)

::: tip TIP
When you answer, **Yes / No** follows the **facts**, not the tag. Vietnamese speakers often get this wrong.

You don't eat meat, do you? – **No**, I don't. *(Đúng vậy, tôi không ăn.)*<br>
You don't eat meat, do you? – **Yes**, I do. *(Không, tôi có ăn chứ.)*
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She is a doctor, isn't it? | She is a doctor, isn't **she**? | The pronoun in the tag must match the subject. |
| You like tea, isn't it? | You like tea, **don't you**? | If the main sentence uses a normal verb, the tag uses *do / does / did*. |
| He went home, wasn't he? | He went home, **didn't he**? | Past simple of a normal verb → *did*. |
| I'm late, am not I? | I'm late, **aren't I**? | *I am* has a special tag: *aren't I*. |
| He never smokes, doesn't he? | He never smokes, **does he**? | *never* is already negative, so the tag is positive. |
:::

## In context

> **Lan:** It's really hot today, **isn't it**? \
> **Hoa:** Yes, it is. Let's get some iced tea, **shall we**? \
> **Lan:** Good idea. You don't have to go back to work, **do you**? \
> **Hoa:** No, I don't. There's a new café near the park, **isn't there**? \
> **Lan:** Yes, but everyone goes there at lunchtime, **don't they**? It'll be crowded. \
> **Hoa:** Let's try it anyway. Hold my bag for a second, **will you**? I need to find my phone.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Lan:** Hôm nay nóng thật đấy nhỉ?<br>
**Hoa:** Ừ, nóng thật. Mình đi uống trà đá nhé?<br>
**Lan:** Ý hay đấy. Cậu không phải quay lại làm việc, phải không?<br>
**Hoa:** Ừ, không. Gần công viên có một quán cà phê mới mở, phải không?<br>
**Lan:** Đúng rồi, nhưng giờ trưa ai cũng đến đó mà, phải không? Chắc sẽ đông lắm.<br>
**Hoa:** Cứ thử xem. Cầm túi giúp mình một chút nhé? Mình phải tìm điện thoại.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Positive → **negative** tag; negative (including *never, hardly, nobody, nothing*) → **positive** tag.
- Tag = the **auxiliary** of the main sentence + a **pronoun**.
- Special cases: *I am* → **aren't I**, *Let's* → **shall we**, commands → **will you**, *everyone* → **they**, *nothing* → **it**.
- **Falling** voice = you're sure and want agreement; **rising** voice = a real question.
:::
