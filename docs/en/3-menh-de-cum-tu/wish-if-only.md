# Wish and if only

Use these to say you **wish** things were different from reality: you want the present to be different, you regret something in the past, or you want someone to change. The meaning is like "ước gì…" or "giá mà…" in Vietnamese.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Wish about the present | S + wish + S + **V2 / were / could V** | I **wish** I **had** a car.<br>*Ước gì tôi có xe hơi.* |
| Regret about the past | S + wish + S + **had + V3** | I **wish** I **had studied** harder.<br>*Giá mà hồi đó tôi học chăm hơn.* |
| Wanting someone / something to change | S + wish + S + **would + V** | I **wish** it **would stop** raining.<br>*Ước gì trời tạnh mưa.* |
| If only (stronger) | **If only** + S + V2 / had V3 / would V | **If only** I **knew** her number!<br>*Giá mà tôi biết số của cô ấy!* |
:::

::: warning IMPORTANT
A wish always goes **one tense back** from reality:

- Reality in the **present** → wish + **past simple** (V2 / were).
- Reality in the **past** → wish + **past perfect** (had V3).

I **don't have** a car. → I wish I **had** a car.<br>
I **didn't study** hard. → I wish I **had studied** harder.
:::

## How to use

In each example below, the **reality** is on the left and the **wish** is on the right of the arrow.

### 1. Wish about the present: wish + V2 / were / could

You are not happy with the situation **now** and want it to be different.

- I don't have a car. → I **wish** I **had** a car.<br>*Tôi không có xe hơi. → Ước gì tôi có xe hơi.*
- I live far from work. → I **wish** I **lived** closer to work.<br>*Tôi sống xa chỗ làm. → Ước gì tôi sống gần chỗ làm hơn.*
- I can't swim. → I **wish** I **could** swim.<br>*Tôi không biết bơi. → Ước gì tôi biết bơi.*
- It's so hot today. → I **wish** it **weren't** so hot.<br>*Hôm nay nóng quá. → Ước gì trời đừng nóng thế này.*
- My son doesn't eat vegetables. → I **wish** my son **ate** vegetables.<br>*Con trai tôi không ăn rau. → Ước gì con tôi chịu ăn rau.*

::: tip TIP
After *wish*, the verb **be** becomes **were** for every subject: I wish I **were** taller, I wish he **were** here. In everyday speech, native speakers also often use *was* (I wish I **was** taller), but in exams you should use **were**.
:::

### 2. Regret about the past: wish + had V3

It already happened, you can't change it, and you **regret** it.

- I didn't study hard. → I **wish** I **had studied** harder.<br>*Tôi đã không học chăm. → Giá mà tôi đã học chăm hơn.*
- I ate too much last night. → I **wish** I **hadn't eaten** so much.<br>*Tối qua tôi ăn nhiều quá. → Giá mà tôi đừng ăn nhiều như thế.*
- She didn't go to the party. → She **wishes** she **had gone** to the party.<br>*Cô ấy đã không đi dự tiệc. → Cô ấy tiếc vì đã không đi dự tiệc.*
- I didn't bring an umbrella. → I **wish** I **had brought** an umbrella.<br>*Tôi không mang ô. → Giá mà tôi mang theo ô.*

### 3. Wanting someone / something to change: wish + would

Use this when you are **annoyed** by what someone else does, or when you want a situation to change soon.

- My neighbour plays loud music every night. → I **wish** he **would stop** playing loud music.<br>*Hàng xóm tôi mở nhạc to mỗi đêm. → Ước gì anh ta thôi mở nhạc to.*
- It has been raining all day. → I **wish** it **would stop** raining.<br>*Trời mưa cả ngày rồi. → Ước gì trời tạnh mưa.*
- You always leave your clothes on the floor. → I **wish** you **wouldn't leave** your clothes on the floor.<br>*Con lúc nào cũng vứt quần áo dưới sàn. → Mẹ mong con đừng vứt quần áo dưới sàn nữa.*
- The bus is late again. → I **wish** the bus **would come**.<br>*Xe buýt lại trễ nữa rồi. → Ước gì xe buýt tới ngay.*

::: tip TIP
Don't say *I wish I would…* about yourself. To wish you could do something, use **could**: I wish I **could** come to your party. *(Ước gì tôi đến dự tiệc của bạn được.)*
:::

### 4. If only — stronger than wish

**If only** works exactly like *wish* (the same verb forms), but the feeling is stronger and it often ends with an exclamation mark.

- I don't have time. → **If only** I **had** more time!<br>*Tôi không có thời gian. → Giá mà tôi có thêm thời gian!*
- I said something stupid. → **If only** I **hadn't said** that!<br>*Tôi đã nói một câu ngớ ngẩn. → Giá mà tôi đừng nói câu đó!*
- She never calls me. → **If only** she **would call** me!<br>*Cô ấy chẳng bao giờ gọi cho tôi. → Giá mà cô ấy gọi cho tôi!*
- I'm not tall. → **If only** I **were** taller!<br>*Tôi không cao. → Giá mà tôi cao hơn!*

## Summary table

| Reality is in | After wish / if only | Example |
|---|---|---|
| The present | V2 / were / could + V | I'm short. → I wish I **were** taller.<br>*Tôi thấp. → Ước gì tôi cao hơn.* |
| The past | had + V3 | I missed the bus. → I wish I **hadn't missed** the bus.<br>*Tôi lỡ xe buýt. → Giá mà tôi không lỡ xe.* |
| Another person / a situation you want to change | would + V | He smokes. → I wish he **would stop** smoking.<br>*Anh ấy hút thuốc. → Ước gì anh ấy bỏ thuốc.* |

::: tip TIP
**wish** can also mean "to wish someone something" or "to want" (formal). In that case, you don't go back a tense:
- I **wish you** a happy birthday.<br>*Chúc bạn sinh nhật vui vẻ.*
- I **wish to speak** to the manager.<br>*Tôi muốn nói chuyện với quản lý.*
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I wish I have a car. | I wish I **had** a car. | A wish about the present must go back to the past. |
| I wish I can swim. | I wish I **could** swim. | *can* goes back to *could*. |
| I wish I didn't eat so much last night. | I wish I **hadn't eaten** so much last night. | For regret about the past, use *had V3*. |
| I wish I would be rich. | I wish I **were** rich. | Don't use *would* for a wish about yourself. |
| I wish it stops raining. | I wish it **would stop** raining. | To want a situation to change, use *would*. |
:::

## In context

> **Ha:** You look tired. What's wrong? \
> **Duc:** I stayed up late watching football. I **wish** I **had gone** to bed earlier. \
> **Ha:** And you have a big meeting today! \
> **Duc:** I know. I **wish** I **didn't have** to go. **If only** today **were** Saturday! \
> **Ha:** Well, I **wish** you **would stop** watching football so late. \
> **Duc:** You're right. **If only** I **could** sleep for one more hour!

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Hà:** Trông anh mệt thế. Sao vậy?<br>
**Đức:** Anh thức khuya xem bóng đá. Giá mà anh đi ngủ sớm hơn.<br>
**Hà:** Mà hôm nay anh có cuộc họp quan trọng đấy!<br>
**Đức:** Anh biết. Ước gì anh không phải đi. Giá mà hôm nay là thứ Bảy!<br>
**Hà:** Em thì mong anh đừng xem bóng đá muộn như thế nữa.<br>
**Đức:** Em nói đúng. Giá mà anh được ngủ thêm một tiếng nữa!
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Wish about the **present** → wish + **V2 / were / could**.
- Regret about the **past** → wish + **had V3**.
- Want **another person / a situation** to change → wish + **would V**.
- **If only** = wish but stronger, with exactly the same verb forms.
:::
