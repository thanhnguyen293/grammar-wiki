# Purpose

Use these structures to say **why you do something** (what you want to achieve). You meet them every day: "I study English **to** get a better job", "I get up early **so as not to** miss the bus".

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Most common | S + V + **to** + V | I went out **to buy** some milk.<br>*Tôi ra ngoài để mua sữa.* |
| More formal | S + V + **in order to / so as to** + V | She studies hard **in order to pass** the exam.<br>*Cô ấy học chăm để thi đỗ.* |
| Negative | S + V + **in order not to / so as not to** + V | He left early **so as not to miss** the bus.<br>*Anh ấy đi sớm để không lỡ xe buýt.* |
| With its own subject | S + V + **so that** + S + can / could / will / would + V | I speak slowly **so that** you **can** understand.<br>*Tôi nói chậm để bạn hiểu được.* |
:::

## How to use

### 1. to + V — the most common way

- I went to the shop **to buy** some bread.<br>*Tôi ra cửa hàng để mua bánh mì.*
- She called me **to say** sorry.<br>*Cô ấy gọi cho tôi để xin lỗi.*
- We're saving money **to buy** a new house.<br>*Chúng tôi đang để dành tiền để mua nhà mới.*
- Why are you learning English? – **To get** a better job.<br>*Sao bạn học tiếng Anh? – Để kiếm việc tốt hơn.*

### 2. in order to / so as to — more formal, stresses the purpose

The meaning is exactly the same as **to**, but it sounds clearer and more formal. You often see it in writing and work emails.

- He gets up at 5 a.m. **in order to have** time for exercise.<br>*Anh ấy dậy lúc 5 giờ sáng để có thời gian tập thể dục.*
- **In order to apply** for this job, you need to send your CV.<br>*Để ứng tuyển vị trí này, bạn cần gửi CV.*
- We turned off the lights **so as to save** electricity.<br>*Chúng tôi tắt đèn để tiết kiệm điện.*
- She took a taxi **so as to arrive** on time.<br>*Cô ấy đi taxi để đến đúng giờ.*

::: tip TIP
**In order to** can start a sentence (like the second example above). **So as to** usually only goes in the middle of a sentence.
:::

### 3. Negative: in order not to / so as not to

Put **not** right before **to**.

- I wrote it down **so as not to forget** it.<br>*Tôi ghi lại để khỏi quên.*
- He walked quietly **in order not to wake** the baby.<br>*Anh ấy đi rón rén để không làm em bé thức giấc.*
- We left home early **so as not to get** stuck in traffic.<br>*Chúng tôi đi sớm để không bị kẹt xe.*
- She whispered **so as not to disturb** the other people.<br>*Cô ấy nói thầm để không làm phiền người khác.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
When you want to say "in order **not** to…", don't use **not to** on its own. Use **so as not to** or **in order not to**.

✗ He left early **not to miss** the bus.<br>
✓ He left early **so as not to miss** the bus.
:::

### 4. so that + clause

After **so that** comes a full sentence: **subject + can / will** (present, future) or **could / would** (past).

Present or future sentence → **can / will**:

- Speak louder **so that** everyone **can** hear you.<br>*Nói to lên để mọi người nghe thấy bạn.*
- I'll give you my number **so that** you **can** call me.<br>*Tôi sẽ cho bạn số điện thoại để bạn gọi cho tôi.*
- She is saving money **so that** her son **can** go to university.<br>*Cô ấy đang để dành tiền để con trai được học đại học.*
- Take an umbrella **so that** you **won't** get wet.<br>*Mang ô theo để không bị ướt.*

Past sentence → **could / would**:

- I left the door open **so that** the cat **could** come in.<br>*Tôi để cửa mở để con mèo vào được.*
- He wrote the address down **so that** he **wouldn't** forget it.<br>*Anh ấy ghi địa chỉ lại để khỏi quên.*
- We whispered **so that** we **wouldn't** wake the children.<br>*Chúng tôi nói thầm để không làm bọn trẻ thức giấc.*

| Main sentence | After so that | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present / future | can / will (+ not) | I'm leaving now **so that** I **can** catch the train.<br>*Tôi đi bây giờ để kịp chuyến tàu.* |
| Past | could / would (+ not) | I left early **so that** I **could** catch the train.<br>*Tôi đã đi sớm để kịp chuyến tàu.* |

## Same subject or different subject

This is the quickest way to choose between **to V** and **so that**.

| Case | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Same** subject (the person who acts = the person who gets the result) | to / in order to / so as to + V | I study hard **to pass** the exam.<br>*Tôi học chăm để thi đỗ.* (I study, I pass) |
| **Different** subject (the person who acts ≠ the person who gets the result) | so that + new subject | The teacher speaks slowly **so that** we **can** understand.<br>*Cô giáo nói chậm để chúng tôi hiểu.* (she speaks, we understand) |

Compare each pair:

- My mom works hard **to buy** a house.<br>*Mẹ tôi làm việc vất vả để mua nhà.* (mom works, mom buys)
- My mom works hard **so that** we **can** go to a good school.<br>*Mẹ tôi làm việc vất vả để chúng tôi được học trường tốt.* (mom works, we go to school)
- I turned on the light **to read** my book.<br>*Tôi bật đèn để đọc sách.*
- I turned on the light **so that** my son **could** read his book.<br>*Tôi bật đèn để con trai tôi đọc sách.*

### Changing to V into so that

When the subject is the same, you can still use **so that**. Just repeat the subject and add *can / could*:

- I turned on the light **to see** better.<br>→ I turned on the light **so that** I **could** see better.<br>*Tôi bật đèn để nhìn rõ hơn.*
- She goes to bed early **so as not to be** tired in the morning.<br>→ She goes to bed early **so that** she **won't be** tired in the morning.<br>*Cô ấy đi ngủ sớm để buổi sáng không bị mệt.*
- We took a map **in order not to get** lost.<br>→ We took a map **so that** we **wouldn't get** lost.<br>*Chúng tôi mang theo bản đồ để không bị lạc.*

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I went to the shop for buying milk. | I went to the shop **to buy** milk. | To give the purpose of an action, use *to V*, not *for + V-ing*. |
| He left early not to miss the bus. | He left early **so as not to** miss the bus. | The negative of purpose is *so as not to / in order not to*. |
| I study hard so that pass the exam. | I study hard so that **I can** pass the exam. | *so that* must be followed by a subject and a verb. |
| She saves money so that she can to buy a car. | She saves money so that she can **buy** a car. | After *can*, use the base verb without *to*. |
| The teacher speaks slowly to we understand. | The teacher speaks slowly **so that we can** understand. | With a different subject, use *so that*. |
:::

## In context

> **Linh:** Why are you learning English, Tuan? \
> **Tuan:** **To get** a better job. My company needs staff who can talk to foreign customers. \
> **Linh:** How do you study? \
> **Tuan:** I get up at 5:30 **in order to study** for an hour before work. And I write new words in a notebook **so as not to forget** them. \
> **Linh:** That's great. I watch films with English subtitles **so that I can hear** real English. \
> **Tuan:** Good idea! Send me some film names **so that I can try** it too.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Linh:** Sao anh lại học tiếng Anh vậy, Tuấn?<br>
**Tuấn:** Để kiếm việc tốt hơn. Công ty anh cần nhân viên nói chuyện được với khách nước ngoài.<br>
**Linh:** Anh học thế nào?<br>
**Tuấn:** Anh dậy lúc 5 rưỡi để học một tiếng trước khi đi làm. Anh còn ghi từ mới vào sổ để không quên.<br>
**Linh:** Hay quá. Em thì xem phim có phụ đề tiếng Anh để nghe tiếng Anh thật.<br>
**Tuấn:** Ý hay đấy! Gửi anh vài tên phim để anh thử với.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **to / in order to / so as to + V** when the person who acts and the person who gets the result are the **same**.
- "In order not to…" → **so as not to / in order not to + V**; don't use *not to* on its own.
- **so that + S + can / will** (present) or **could / would** (past), especially when the **subject is different**.
:::
