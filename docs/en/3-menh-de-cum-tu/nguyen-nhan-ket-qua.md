# Cause and result

Use these structures to say **why** something happens and what its **result** is: "I stayed home because it was raining", "The tea was too hot, so I couldn't drink it". This lesson collects the most common structures and shows how to change one into another.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Reason + clause | because / since / as + S + V | I stayed home **because it was raining**.<br>*Tôi ở nhà vì trời mưa.* |
| Reason + noun | because of / due to + noun / V-ing | I stayed home **because of the rain**.<br>*Tôi ở nhà vì mưa.* |
| Result | S + V, **so** + S + V | It was raining, **so** I stayed home.<br>*Trời mưa nên tôi ở nhà.* |
| So … that | S + be / V + **so** + adjective / adverb + **that** + S + V | The soup was **so hot that** I couldn't eat it.<br>*Súp nóng đến nỗi tôi không ăn được.* |
| So … that (with a noun) | **such** + (a / an) + adjective + noun + **that** + S + V | It was **such a hot day that** we stayed inside.<br>*Hôm đó nóng đến nỗi chúng tôi ở trong nhà.* |
| Too … to (cannot) | S + be + **too** + adjective + (for sb) + **to** V | The box is **too heavy** for me **to carry**.<br>*Cái thùng quá nặng, tôi không bê nổi.* |
| Enough … to | adjective + **enough** + to V / **enough** + noun + to V | She is **old enough to drive**.<br>*Cô ấy đủ tuổi lái xe.* |
:::

::: warning IMPORTANT
Vietnamese says "**Vì** … **nên** …" (because … so …), but English uses only **one** of the two: either *because* or *so*.

✗ **Because** it rained, **so** we stayed home.<br>
✓ **Because** it rained, we stayed home. / It rained, **so** we stayed home.
:::

## How to use

### 1. because / since / as + clause — because

**because** is the most common and puts the focus on the reason. **since / as** are used when the reason is already clear or the listener already knows it; they usually start the sentence.

- I'm tired **because** I worked late last night.<br>*Tôi mệt vì tối qua làm muộn.*
- She didn't come to class **because** she was sick.<br>*Cô ấy không đến lớp vì bị ốm.*
- **Since** you're here, can you help me with this box?<br>*Vì bạn đang ở đây rồi, giúp tôi cái thùng này nhé?*
- **As** it was late, we took a taxi home.<br>*Vì đã muộn nên chúng tôi đi taxi về.*

::: tip TIP
**since** can also mean "from the time" (I've lived here **since** 2015). If a reason comes after *since*, it means "because".
:::

### 2. because of / due to + noun / V-ing — because of

After **because of / due to** comes a **noun** or **V-ing**, not a full sentence.

- The flight was delayed **because of** the bad weather.<br>*Chuyến bay bị hoãn vì thời tiết xấu.*
- We couldn't sleep **because of** the noise.<br>*Chúng tôi không ngủ được vì tiếng ồn.*
- The match was cancelled **due to** heavy rain.<br>*Trận đấu bị hủy do mưa to.*
- He lost his job **because of** being late so often.<br>*Anh ấy mất việc vì đi làm muộn quá nhiều.*

::: tip TIP
**due to** is more formal. You often hear it in announcements at airports, train stations, and companies.
:::

### 3. so — so, therefore

**so** starts the **result** clause, and there is usually a comma before it.

- It was raining, **so** we stayed at home.<br>*Trời mưa nên chúng tôi ở nhà.*
- I was hungry, **so** I made some noodles.<br>*Tôi đói nên nấu ít mì.*
- The shop was closed, **so** I went home.<br>*Cửa hàng đóng cửa nên tôi về nhà.*

### 4. so … that — so … that

**so** + adjective / adverb (**no** noun after it).

- The soup was **so hot that** I couldn't eat it.<br>*Súp nóng đến nỗi tôi không ăn được.*
- He spoke **so fast that** nobody understood him.<br>*Anh ấy nói nhanh đến nỗi không ai hiểu.*
- I was **so tired that** I fell asleep on the sofa.<br>*Tôi mệt đến nỗi ngủ quên trên ghế sofa.*
- There were **so many people that** we couldn't find a seat.<br>*Đông người đến nỗi chúng tôi không tìm được chỗ ngồi.* (so many / so much + noun)

### 5. such … that — so … that (with a noun)

**such** + (a / an) + adjective + **noun**. With uncountable or plural nouns, there is no *a / an*.

- It was **such a hot day that** we stayed inside.<br>*Hôm đó nóng đến nỗi chúng tôi ở trong nhà.*
- She is **such a kind person that** everyone likes her.<br>*Cô ấy tốt bụng đến nỗi ai cũng quý.*
- They are **such good friends that** they tell each other everything.<br>*Họ thân nhau đến nỗi chuyện gì cũng kể cho nhau.*
- We had **such bad weather that** we couldn't go out.<br>*Thời tiết xấu đến nỗi chúng tôi không ra ngoài được.*

| Word | Goes with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| so | adjective / adverb (no noun) | The house is **so beautiful**.<br>*Ngôi nhà đẹp quá.* |
| such | (a / an) + adjective + noun | It's **such a beautiful house**.<br>*Đó là một ngôi nhà đẹp quá.* |

### 6. too … to — too … to (cannot)

**too … to** already has a **negative** meaning (you can't do it), so don't add *not*.

- The tea is **too hot to drink**.<br>*Trà nóng quá, không uống được.*
- He is **too young to drive** a car.<br>*Cậu ấy còn quá nhỏ, chưa lái xe được.*
- The box is **too heavy for me to carry**.<br>*Cái thùng quá nặng, tôi không bê nổi.*
- It's **too late to call** her now.<br>*Giờ muộn quá rồi, không gọi cho cô ấy được.*

### 7. enough … to — enough … to

- **Adjective + enough**: She is **old enough to vote**.<br>*Cô ấy đủ tuổi đi bầu cử.*
- He isn't **tall enough to play** basketball.<br>*Anh ấy không đủ cao để chơi bóng rổ.*
- **enough + noun**: We have **enough money to buy** a car.<br>*Chúng tôi có đủ tiền mua xe hơi.*
- Do you have **enough time to help** me?<br>*Bạn có đủ thời gian giúp tôi không?*

::: tip TIP
An **adjective** comes **before** enough (*old enough*); a **noun** comes **after** enough (*enough money*).
:::

## Changing between structures

You can say the same idea in several ways. These are the pairs you often see in sentence-rewriting exercises.

**because ↔ because of**: turn the clause into a noun phrase or V-ing.

- I stayed home **because I was sick**.<br>→ I stayed home **because of my illness**.<br>*Tôi ở nhà vì bị ốm.*
- We arrived late **because the traffic was bad**.<br>→ We arrived late **because of the bad traffic**.<br>*Chúng tôi đến muộn vì đường tắc.*
- She failed **because she didn't study**.<br>→ She failed **because of not studying**.<br>*Cô ấy trượt vì không học bài.*

**because ↔ so**: swap the reason and the result.

- We stayed home **because** it was cold.<br>→ It was cold, **so** we stayed home.<br>*Trời lạnh nên chúng tôi ở nhà.*

**so … that ↔ such … that**: when you add a noun, change *so* to *such*.

- The film was **so interesting that** I watched it twice.<br>→ It was **such an interesting film that** I watched it twice.<br>*Phim hay đến nỗi tôi xem hai lần.*
- The questions were **so easy that** everyone finished early.<br>→ They were **such easy questions that** everyone finished early.<br>*Câu hỏi dễ đến nỗi ai cũng làm xong sớm.*

**so … that (cannot) ↔ too … to ↔ not … enough**: use these when the result is that you **can't do something**.

- He is **so young that** he can't drive.<br>→ He is **too young to drive**.<br>→ He is **not old enough to drive**.<br>*Cậu ấy còn quá nhỏ, chưa lái xe được.*
- The coffee is **so hot that** I can't drink it.<br>→ The coffee is **too hot** for me **to drink**.<br>→ The coffee **isn't cool enough** for me **to drink**.<br>*Cà phê nóng quá, tôi không uống được.*
- The bag is **so heavy that** she can't lift it.<br>→ The bag is **too heavy** for her **to lift**.<br>→ She **isn't strong enough to lift** the bag.<br>*Cái túi nặng quá, cô ấy không nhấc nổi.*

::: tip TIP
When you change to **too … to**, drop the object at the end: *so hot that I can't drink **it*** → *too hot to drink* (no *it*). With **not … enough**, use the **opposite** adjective: *young → not old enough*, *heavy → not light enough*.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Because it rained, so we stayed home. | Because it rained, we stayed home. | Use only one of the two: *because* or *so*. |
| We stayed home because of it rained. | We stayed home **because** it rained. | *because of* + noun; with a full sentence, use *because*. |
| It was so a nice day that we went out. | It was **such** a nice day that we went out. | With a noun (*day*), use *such*. |
| He is enough old to drive. | He is **old enough** to drive. | The adjective comes before *enough*. |
| The tea is too hot to drink it. | The tea is too hot **to drink**. | Don't repeat the object after *too … to*. |
:::

## In context

> **Mai:** Why were you late this morning? \
> **Khoa:** **Because of** the traffic. There were **so many cars that** my bus couldn't move. \
> **Mai:** Poor you! And why didn't you eat lunch? \
> **Khoa:** The canteen food was **too salty to eat**, **so** I just had a coffee. \
> **Mai:** Let's have dinner together tonight. It's **such a nice evening that** we should eat outside. \
> **Khoa:** Good idea, but I'm **too tired to go** far. Is the restaurant **close enough to walk** to?

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Mai:** Sao sáng nay anh đến muộn thế?<br>
**Khoa:** Vì tắc đường. Xe nhiều đến nỗi xe buýt của anh không nhích được.<br>
**Mai:** Tội anh quá! Thế sao anh không ăn trưa?<br>
**Khoa:** Đồ ăn ở căng tin mặn quá, không ăn nổi, nên anh chỉ uống cà phê.<br>
**Mai:** Tối nay mình ăn tối cùng nhau nhé. Buổi tối đẹp thế này nên ăn ở ngoài trời.<br>
**Khoa:** Ý hay đấy, nhưng anh mệt quá, không đi xa được. Nhà hàng có đủ gần để đi bộ không?
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **because / since / as** + clause; **because of / due to** + noun / V-ing.
- Don't use *because* and *so* together.
- **so** + adjective; **such** + (a / an) + adjective + noun; then **that** + clause.
- **too … to** = so … that you can't; **adjective + enough to** = enough … to.
:::
