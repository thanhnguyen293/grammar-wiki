# Reported questions, commands and suggestions

The last lesson showed how to report a **statement**. This lesson shows how to report **questions**, **requests**, **suggestions**, **advice**, and reporting verbs like *promise, refuse, apologise*. The rules for moving the tense back, changing the person and changing time words still apply.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Yes/No question | asked (+ person) + if / whether + S + V | "Are you tired?" → He asked me **if I was** tired. |
| Wh- question | asked (+ person) + question word + S + V | "Where do you live?" → She asked me **where I lived**. |
| Command, request | told / asked + person + (not) to V | "Close the door." → He told me **to close** the door. |
| Suggestion | suggested + V-ing / suggested (that) + S + (should) V | "Let's go out." → She suggested **going** out. |
| Advice | advised + person + (not) to V | "You should rest." → The doctor advised me **to rest**. |
:::

## How to use

### 1. Yes/No questions: use if or whether

A question you answer with *Yes/No* is reported with **if** or **whether**. After that, use **statement word order**: subject first, then verb. Drop *do / does / did* and drop the question mark.

- "**Are** you hungry?" he asked.<br>→ He asked **if I was** hungry.<br>*Anh ấy hỏi tôi có đói không.*
- "**Do** you **like** coffee?" she asked me.<br>→ She asked me **if I liked** coffee.<br>*Cô ấy hỏi tôi có thích cà phê không.*
- "**Have** you **finished** your homework?" Mum asked.<br>→ Mum asked **whether I had finished** my homework.<br>*Mẹ hỏi tôi đã làm xong bài tập chưa.*
- "**Can** you **drive**?" the manager asked Lan.<br>→ The manager asked Lan **if she could** drive.<br>*Người quản lý hỏi Lan có biết lái xe không.*

### 2. Wh- questions: keep the question word, use statement word order

Keep the question word (*what, where, when, why, how…*), then write **S + V**. Don't move the helping verb to the front.

- "Where **do you live**?" she asked.<br>→ She asked me where **I lived**.<br>*Cô ấy hỏi tôi sống ở đâu.*
- "What time **is it**?" he asked.<br>→ He asked what time **it was**.<br>*Anh ấy hỏi mấy giờ rồi.*
- "Why **are you** late?" the teacher asked.<br>→ The teacher asked me why **I was** late.<br>*Cô giáo hỏi tôi tại sao đến muộn.*
- "How much **did you pay** for it?" my wife asked.<br>→ My wife asked how much **I had paid** for it.<br>*Vợ tôi hỏi tôi đã trả bao nhiêu tiền cho nó.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
A reported question **is no longer a question**: the subject comes before the verb, there is no *do / does / did*, and there is no question mark.

"Where **does she** work?" → He asked where **she worked**. (don't write ~~where did she work~~)
:::

### 3. Commands and requests: told / asked + person + (not) to V

Use **told** for commands and **asked** for polite requests (*please, can you…*). A negative (*Don't…*) becomes **not to V**.

- "Sit down," the teacher said to us.<br>→ The teacher **told** us **to sit** down.<br>*Cô giáo bảo chúng tôi ngồi xuống.*
- "**Don't** be late," my boss said.<br>→ My boss **told** me **not to be** late.<br>*Sếp dặn tôi đừng đến muộn.*
- "Please turn off the light," Mum said.<br>→ Mum **asked** me **to turn** off the light.<br>*Mẹ nhờ tôi tắt đèn.*
- "Can you help me with my bags?" the old lady said.<br>→ The old lady **asked** me **to help** her with her bags.<br>*Bà cụ nhờ tôi xách giúp túi.*

### 4. Suggestions: suggested V-ing / suggested that… should

Sentences with *Let's…, Why don't we…, How about…, What about…* are usually reported with **suggest**.

- "**Let's** go to the cinema," Hoa said.<br>→ Hoa **suggested going** to the cinema.<br>*Hoa rủ đi xem phim.*
- "**Why don't we** eat out tonight?" my husband said.<br>→ My husband **suggested eating** out that night.<br>*Chồng tôi gợi ý tối đó đi ăn ngoài.*
- "**How about** taking a taxi?" Nam said.<br>→ Nam **suggested that we (should) take** a taxi.<br>*Nam gợi ý chúng tôi nên đi taxi.*
- "You **should** call the bank," my friend said.<br>→ My friend **suggested that I (should) call** the bank.<br>*Bạn tôi gợi ý tôi nên gọi cho ngân hàng.*

### 5. Advice: advised + person + (not) to V

Sentences with *You should…, You'd better…, If I were you, I'd…* are reported with **advise**.

- "You **should** sleep more," the doctor said.<br>→ The doctor **advised** me **to sleep** more.<br>*Bác sĩ khuyên tôi ngủ nhiều hơn.*
- "You **shouldn't** eat so much sugar," she said.<br>→ She **advised** me **not to eat** so much sugar.<br>*Cô ấy khuyên tôi không nên ăn nhiều đường như vậy.*
- "**If I were you, I'd** buy a new phone," Minh said.<br>→ Minh **advised** me **to buy** a new phone.<br>*Minh khuyên tôi mua điện thoại mới.*
- "You'**d better** take a coat," Dad said.<br>→ Dad **advised** me **to take** a coat.<br>*Bố khuyên tôi mang áo khoác.*

## Other reporting verbs

Instead of always using *said*, you can use a verb that shows what the speaker **meant**. Each verb has its own structure.

| Verb | Meaning | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| promise | say you will surely do it | promise (not) to V | "I'll pay you back tomorrow." → He **promised to pay** me back the next day. |
| refuse | say you won't do it | refuse to V | "I won't lend you my car." → She **refused to lend** me her car. |
| agree | say yes to doing it | agree to V | "OK, I'll help you." → He **agreed to help** me. |
| offer | say you will help | offer to V | "Shall I carry your bag?" → He **offered to carry** my bag. |
| remind | help someone remember | remind + person + to V | "Don't forget to lock the door." → She **reminded** me **to lock** the door. |
| warn | tell someone about a danger | warn + person + (not) to V | "Don't touch the pan. It's hot." → She **warned** me **not to touch** the pan. |
| invite | ask someone to come or join | invite + person + to V | "Would you like to come to my party?" → He **invited** me **to come** to his party. |
| apologise | say sorry | apologise for V-ing | "I'm sorry I'm late." → She **apologised for being** late. |
| thank | say thanks | thank + person + for V-ing | "Thanks for helping me." → He **thanked** me **for helping** him. |
| deny | say you didn't do it | deny V-ing | "I didn't take the money." → He **denied taking** the money. |
| admit | say you did it (something bad) | admit V-ing | "I broke the vase." → My son **admitted breaking** the vase. |
| accuse | say someone did something bad | accuse + person + of V-ing | "You stole my phone!" → She **accused** him **of stealing** her phone. |
| insist | say firmly and not change your mind | insist on V-ing | "I'll pay. I insist!" → He **insisted on paying**. |
| congratulate | praise someone for a success | congratulate + person + on V-ing | "Congratulations on passing the exam!" → They **congratulated** me **on passing** the exam. |

### Group 1: verb + to V

- "I'll call you every day," he said.<br>→ He **promised to call** me every day.<br>*Anh ấy hứa ngày nào cũng gọi cho tôi.*
- "No, I won't tell you my age," the woman said.<br>→ The woman **refused to tell** me her age.<br>*Người phụ nữ từ chối cho tôi biết tuổi.*
- "I can take you to the airport," my brother said.<br>→ My brother **offered to take** me to the airport.<br>*Anh trai tôi đề nghị chở tôi ra sân bay.*

### Group 2: verb + person + to V

- "Remember to buy some milk," my wife said.<br>→ My wife **reminded** me **to buy** some milk.<br>*Vợ tôi nhắc tôi mua sữa.*
- "Don't swim here. It's dangerous," the guard said.<br>→ The guard **warned** us **not to swim** there.<br>*Người bảo vệ cảnh báo chúng tôi không được bơi ở đó.*
- "Come and have dinner with us," Lan said.<br>→ Lan **invited** me **to have** dinner with them.<br>*Lan mời tôi ăn tối cùng họ.*

### Group 3: verb + (preposition) + V-ing

- "I'm sorry I broke your cup," the boy said.<br>→ The boy **apologised for breaking** my cup.<br>*Cậu bé xin lỗi vì đã làm vỡ cốc của tôi.*
- "I didn't eat the cake!" my son said.<br>→ My son **denied eating** the cake.<br>*Con trai tôi chối là không ăn bánh.*
- "You lied to me!" she said to him.<br>→ She **accused** him **of lying** to her.<br>*Cô ấy buộc tội anh ta đã nói dối cô.*
- "Yes, I forgot to send the email," Tuấn said.<br>→ Tuấn **admitted forgetting** to send the email.<br>*Tuấn thừa nhận đã quên gửi email.*

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| He asked me where did I live. | He asked me where **I lived**. | Reported questions use S + V word order; drop *did*. |
| She asked me was I OK. | She asked me **if I was** OK. | Yes/No questions need *if / whether*. |
| He told me don't go out. | He told me **not to go** out. | A negative command becomes *not to V*. |
| She suggested me to go home. | She suggested **that I (should) go** home. | *suggest* doesn't take *person + to V*. |
| He denied to take the money. | He denied **taking** the money. | *deny* takes V-ing. |
:::

## In context

> **Mai:** How was your job interview? \
> **Phong:** Not bad. First, they **asked** me **where I lived** and **how long I had worked** at my old company. \
> **Mai:** Did they ask about your English? \
> **Phong:** Yes. They **asked** me **if I could** speak English well. Then the manager **asked** me **why I wanted** the job. \
> **Mai:** And at the end? \
> **Phong:** She **told** me **to wait** for an email, and she **promised to call** me within a week.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Mai:** Buổi phỏng vấn của anh thế nào?<br>
**Phong:** Cũng ổn. Đầu tiên, họ hỏi anh sống ở đâu và đã làm ở công ty cũ bao lâu.<br>
**Mai:** Họ có hỏi về tiếng Anh của anh không?<br>
**Phong:** Có. Họ hỏi anh có nói tiếng Anh tốt không. Sau đó chị quản lý hỏi tại sao anh muốn làm công việc này.<br>
**Mai:** Còn lúc cuối thì sao?<br>
**Phong:** Chị ấy bảo anh chờ email, và hứa sẽ gọi cho anh trong vòng một tuần.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Yes/No questions: **asked if / whether + S + V**. Wh- questions: **asked + Wh- + S + V**, no inversion, no question mark.
- Commands, requests: **told / asked + person + (not) to V**.
- Suggestions: **suggested V-ing** or **suggested that + S + (should) V**. Advice: **advised + person + to V**.
- Learn each verb's structure: *promise / refuse / offer* **to V**; *deny / admit* **V-ing**; *apologise for*, *accuse sb of* **V-ing**.
:::
