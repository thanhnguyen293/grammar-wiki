# Workplace English for IT

*Giao tiếp công sở IT*

Phrases and dialogues for everyday work at a tech company: meetings, status updates, clarifying requirements, code review, chat and email. Each lesson is one situation, with example sentences, the mistakes Vietnamese speakers often make and a sample dialogue.

<WorkplaceIndex />

::: tip HOW TO STUDY
- Read each phrase out loud and swap the bold part for your own work (your project, your screen, your teammates).
- Pick 3 phrases you will use this week and write them in your notes.
- Read the sample dialogue out loud, playing both people.
:::

Technical words (API, deploy, merge…) are in the [IT for mobile developers](/vocabulary/it-mobile) topic. The grammar you will use most at work: the [present perfect](/tenses/present-perfect), the [future simple and be going to](/tenses/future-simple) and [modal verbs](/parts-of-speech/modal-verbs).
