# Review quiz

Test yourself on the words you have studied: the **Today's words** lists of earlier days, every word you marked as learned, the learned words of one topic, or the ones you learned most recently. Each question shows the Vietnamese meaning, the part of speech and a translated example; type the English word and press Enter.

::: tip TIP
The underscores show how many letters the word has. If you're stuck, click **Show first letter**. British and American spellings (*colour* / *color*) both count as correct. If you typed a synonym and it was marked wrong, click **My word is also right**.
:::
