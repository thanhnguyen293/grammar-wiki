# The causative

We use the causative when you **ask, pay, force** or **allow** someone else to do something. For example, you don't cut your own hair, you go to a salon and the hairdresser cuts it; or a mother makes her child clean the room. The five main forms: **have, get, make, let**, and **have / get + thing + V3**.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ask or pay someone to do it | have + person + V | I **had** the mechanic **check** my car. |
| Ask or persuade someone to do it | get + person + **to** V | I **got** my brother **to fix** my bike. |
| Have something done for you | have / get + thing + V3 | I **had** my hair **cut**. |
| Force someone to do it | make + person + V | My mum **made** me **clean** my room. |
| Allow someone to do it | let + person + V | My dad **lets** me **drive** his car. |
:::

## How to use

### 1. have + person + V: ask someone, give someone a job to do

Usually used when the person is doing **their job** (a worker, staff member, someone below you at work). The verb after the person is the **bare infinitive (no to)**.

- I'll **have** the receptionist **call** you a taxi.<br>*Tôi sẽ nhờ lễ tân gọi taxi cho anh.*
- The teacher **had** us **read** the story aloud.<br>*Cô giáo cho chúng tôi đọc to câu chuyện.*
- My boss **had** me **write** the report.<br>*Sếp giao cho tôi viết bản báo cáo.*
- We **had** a plumber **look** at the kitchen sink.<br>*Chúng tôi nhờ thợ sửa ống nước xem cái bồn rửa trong bếp.*

### 2. get + person + to V: persuade someone, manage to get someone to do it

Like *have*, but it often means you **had to talk to or persuade** the other person before they did it. There is **to** after the person.

- I **got** my brother **to help** me with my homework.<br>*Tôi nhờ được anh trai giúp làm bài tập.*
- She finally **got** her husband **to stop** smoking.<br>*Cuối cùng cô ấy cũng khiến được chồng bỏ thuốc lá.*
- Can you **get** Nam **to call** me back?<br>*Bạn bảo Nam gọi lại cho tôi được không?*
- I can't **get** my son **to eat** vegetables.<br>*Tôi không sao bắt được con trai ăn rau.*

### 3. have / get + thing + V3: have something done for you by someone else

Very common when talking about **services**: a haircut, a car repair, an eye test, laundry. You usually don't need to say who did it. *get* is a bit more informal than *have*.

- I **had** my hair **cut** yesterday.<br>*Hôm qua tôi đi cắt tóc.*
- She **gets** her nails **done** every month.<br>*Tháng nào cô ấy cũng đi làm móng.*
- We'**re having** our house **painted** next week.<br>*Tuần sau chúng tôi thuê người sơn nhà.*
- You should **get** your eyes **tested**.<br>*Bạn nên đi kiểm tra mắt.*

This structure is also used for **bad things** that happen to you:

- He **had** his wallet **stolen** on the bus.<br>*Anh ấy bị lấy cắp ví trên xe buýt.*
- She **had** her bike **damaged** in the storm.<br>*Xe đạp của cô ấy bị hỏng trong trận bão.*
- I **had** my phone **taken** at the airport.<br>*Tôi bị lấy mất điện thoại ở sân bay.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
*have* changes with the tense like a normal verb. The order is always **have + thing + V3**.

I **have** my car washed · I **had** my car washed · I **will have** my car washed · I'**m having** my car washed
:::

### 4. make + person + V: force someone to do something

The other person **doesn't want to** but **has to** do it. The verb after the person is the **bare infinitive (no to)**.

- My mum **made** me **clean** my room before dinner.<br>*Mẹ bắt tôi dọn phòng trước bữa tối.*
- The teacher **made** him **say** sorry.<br>*Cô giáo bắt cậu ấy xin lỗi.*
- Sad films always **make** me **cry**.<br>*Phim buồn lúc nào cũng làm tôi khóc.*
- Don't **make** me **wait** too long.<br>*Đừng bắt tôi đợi lâu quá.*

In the passive, *make* needs **to**: *He **was made to say** sorry.* (Cậu ấy bị bắt phải xin lỗi.)

### 5. let + person + V: allow someone to do something

The verb after the person is the **bare infinitive (no to)**. *let* doesn't change form: *let – let – let*.

- My parents **let** me **go** out on Saturday nights.<br>*Bố mẹ cho tôi đi chơi tối thứ Bảy.*
- **Let** me **help** you with that bag.<br>*Để tôi giúp bạn xách cái túi đó.*
- The boss didn't **let** us **leave** early.<br>*Sếp không cho chúng tôi về sớm.*
- She never **lets** her children **watch** TV after 9.<br>*Cô ấy không bao giờ cho các con xem TV sau 9 giờ.*

*let* has no passive form. Use *be allowed to*: *We **weren't allowed to** leave early.*

## Comparing the structures

| Structure | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| have + person + V | ask someone, give them a job | I **had** the waiter **bring** some water. |
| get + person + to V | persuade, manage to get someone to do it | I **got** the waiter **to bring** some water. |
| make + person + V | force | The manager **made** the waiter **clean** the tables again. |
| let + person + V | allow | The manager **let** the waiter **go** home early. |
| have / get + thing + V3 | something done for you by someone else | I **had** my shirt **ironed**. |

Note: only **get** has **to** after the person. *have, make, let* have **no** *to*.

## I cut my hair or I had my hair cut?

These two sentences are different in **who does it**.

| Sentence | Who does it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I **cut** my hair. | **I** do it **myself** | I cut my hair myself to save money. |
| I **had** my hair **cut**. | **Someone else** (a hairdresser) does it for me | I had my hair cut at a salon near my office. |
| I **repaired** my car. | **I** do it **myself** | My dad taught me, so I repaired my car myself. |
| I **had** my car **repaired**. | **A mechanic** does it for me | I had my car repaired at the garage. |

- I **washed** my car this morning.<br>*Sáng nay tôi tự rửa xe.*
- I **had** my car **washed** this morning.<br>*Sáng nay tôi đem xe đi rửa.*
- She **made** a beautiful dress.<br>*Cô ấy tự may một chiếc váy đẹp.*
- She **had** a beautiful dress **made**.<br>*Cô ấy đặt may một chiếc váy đẹp.*

::: tip TIP
Vietnamese speakers often say "Tôi đi cắt tóc" and translate it as *I cut my hair*. That sounds like you **cut it yourself with scissors**. If you went to a salon, say ***I had my hair cut*** or ***I got my hair cut***.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I cut my hair at the salon. | I **had my hair cut** at the salon. | When a hairdresser does it for you, use *have + thing + V3*. |
| My mum made me to clean the room. | My mum made me **clean** the room. | No *to* after *make + person*. |
| Let me to help you. | Let me **help** you. | No *to* after *let + person*. |
| I got my brother fix my bike. | I got my brother **to fix** my bike. | *get + person* needs *to*. |
| I had cut my hair yesterday. | I **had my hair cut** yesterday. | The thing must go **between** *have* and V3. |
:::

## In context

> **Hà:** You look different! **Did** you **have** your hair **cut**? \
> **Thảo:** Yes, I **got** it **cut** yesterday. I also **had** my nails **done**. \
> **Hà:** Lucky you! I spent the whole weekend at home. My mother-in-law **made** me **clean** the kitchen. \
> **Thảo:** Oh no! Couldn't you **get** your husband **to help**? \
> **Hà:** He was busy. He **was having** his motorbike **repaired**. \
> **Thảo:** Next weekend, come to the salon with me. I'll **have** them **give** you a discount!

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Hà:** Trông cậu khác quá! Cậu đi cắt tóc à?<br>
**Thảo:** Ừ, hôm qua tớ đi cắt. Tớ còn đi làm móng nữa.<br>
**Hà:** Sướng thế! Tớ thì ở nhà cả cuối tuần. Mẹ chồng bắt tớ dọn bếp.<br>
**Thảo:** Ôi không! Cậu không nhờ được chồng giúp à?<br>
**Hà:** Anh ấy bận. Anh ấy đang mang xe máy đi sửa.<br>
**Thảo:** Cuối tuần sau đi tiệm với tớ nhé. Tớ sẽ bảo họ giảm giá cho cậu!
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **have + person + V**, **make + person + V**, **let + person + V**: no *to*.
- **get + person + to V**: with *to*.
- **have / get + thing + V3**: something is done for you by someone else (a service), or something bad happens to you.
- *I cut my hair* = I did it myself; *I had my hair cut* = a hairdresser did it.
:::
