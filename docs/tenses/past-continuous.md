# Past continuous

Use it to talk about something that **was in progress at a moment in the past**. When telling stories, this tense often goes with the past simple: one action was in progress when another action happened.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + was/were + V-ing | I **was sleeping** at 11 pm. |
| Negative | S + was/were + not + V-ing | I **wasn't sleeping**. |
| Question | Was/Were + S + V-ing? | **Were** you **sleeping**? |
:::

### Forms by subject

| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / He / She / It | She **was reading**. | She **wasn't reading**. | **Was** she **reading**? |
| You / We / They | They **were playing**. | They **weren't playing**. | **Were** they **playing**? |
| Singular nouns (my dad) | My dad **was driving**. | My dad **wasn't driving**. | **Was** your dad **driving**? |
| Plural nouns (the kids) | The kids **were laughing**. | The kids **weren't laughing**. | **Were** the kids **laughing**? |

::: warning IMPORTANT
**I / he / she / it** go with **was**; **you / we / they** go with **were**. After that, always use **V-ing**.
:::

## How to use

### 1. Something in progress at a moment in the past

The sentence often has a specific time or *at this time yesterday / last week*.

- At 8 pm yesterday, I **was having** dinner with my family.<br>*Lúc 8 giờ tối qua, tôi đang ăn tối với gia đình.*
- What **were** you **doing** at this time last week?<br>*Giờ này tuần trước bạn đang làm gì?*
- At midnight, my neighbors **were** still **singing** karaoke.<br>*Nửa đêm rồi mà hàng xóm nhà tôi vẫn đang hát karaoke.*
- She **wasn't working** at 10 am. She **was sleeping**.<br>*Lúc 10 giờ sáng cô ấy không làm việc. Cô ấy đang ngủ.*

### 2. Something in progress when another action interrupted it (when)

The **longer** action takes the past continuous; the **short** action that interrupts takes the **past simple**.

- I **was taking** a shower **when** the phone **rang**.<br>*Tôi đang tắm thì điện thoại reo.*
- **When** I **got** home, my kids **were watching** cartoons.<br>*Khi tôi về đến nhà, bọn trẻ đang xem phim hoạt hình.*
- She **was walking** to work **when** it **started** to rain.<br>*Cô ấy đang đi bộ đi làm thì trời bắt đầu mưa.*
- We **were eating** dinner **when** the lights **went** out.<br>*Chúng tôi đang ăn tối thì mất điện.*

### 3. Two actions in progress at the same time (while)

Both actions last for a while and happen at the same time.

- **While** I **was cooking**, my husband **was cleaning** the house.<br>*Trong lúc tôi nấu ăn thì chồng tôi dọn nhà.*
- The kids **were playing** in the garden **while** we **were talking**.<br>*Bọn trẻ chơi ngoài vườn trong lúc chúng tôi nói chuyện.*
- **While** she **was studying**, her brother **was listening** to music.<br>*Trong lúc cô ấy học bài thì em trai cô ấy nghe nhạc.*

### 4. Setting the scene at the start of a story

Use it to paint the background; the main events then use the past simple.

- It **was raining** and the wind **was blowing** hard.<br>*Trời đang mưa và gió thổi mạnh.*
- The sun **was shining** and people **were walking** along the beach.<br>*Nắng đang chiếu và mọi người đang đi dạo dọc bãi biển.*
- Everyone **was waiting** for the bus. Suddenly, a man **shouted**.<br>*Mọi người đang chờ xe buýt. Bỗng nhiên, một người đàn ông hét lên.*

## When and while

| Word | Usually goes with | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **when** | **past simple** (short action that interrupts) | khi, lúc | I was reading **when** she **called**. |
| **while** | **past continuous** (longer action in progress) | trong lúc, trong khi | **While** I **was reading**, she called. |

These two sentences mean the same thing; only the linking word is different:

- I **was driving** home **when** I **saw** the accident.<br>*Tôi đang lái xe về nhà thì thấy vụ tai nạn.*
- **While** I **was driving** home, I **saw** the accident.<br>*Trong lúc lái xe về nhà, tôi thấy vụ tai nạn.*

::: tip TIP
If the **when / while** part comes first, put a **comma** between the two parts: *When the phone rang, I was cooking.* If it comes in the middle, you don't need a comma: *I was cooking when the phone rang.*
:::

## Compared with the past simple

| Past continuous: in the middle of doing | Past simple: did (and finished) |
|---|---|
| When she arrived, I **was cooking** dinner.<br>*Khi cô ấy đến, tôi đang nấu cơm.* (I had started before) | When she arrived, I **cooked** dinner.<br>*Khi cô ấy đến, tôi (mới) nấu cơm.* (she arrived, then I cooked) |
| I **was reading** a book last night.<br>*Tối qua tôi đọc sách.* (in progress, maybe not finished) | I **read** a book last night.<br>*Tối qua tôi đọc (hết) một cuốn sách.* |
| At 7, we **were having** breakfast.<br>*Lúc 7 giờ, chúng tôi đang ăn sáng.* | At 7, we **had** breakfast.<br>*Chúng tôi ăn sáng lúc 7 giờ.* (started eating at 7) |

## Signal words

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at + time + yesterday / last night | lúc … hôm qua / tối qua | **At 9 last night**, I **was studying**. |
| at this time yesterday / last week | giờ này hôm qua / tuần trước | **At this time yesterday**, we **were flying** to Hue. |
| when + past simple | khi | She **was sleeping** **when** I **called**. |
| while | trong khi | **While** he **was working**, I **was shopping**. |
| all day / all morning yesterday | cả ngày / cả buổi sáng hôm qua | It **was raining** **all day yesterday**. |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I was watch TV at 8 pm. | I was **watching** TV at 8 pm. | After *was/were* you need V-ing. |
| They was playing football. | They **were** playing football. | *they* goes with *were*. |
| I was walking home when I was seeing an accident. | I was walking home when I **saw** an accident. | The short action that interrupts takes the past simple. |
| While the phone rang, I was cooking. | **When** the phone rang, I was cooking. | A short action (one ring) goes with *when*. |
| I was knowing the answer. | I **knew** the answer. | *know* is a state verb, so no continuous. |
:::

## In context

> **Police officer:** What **were** you **doing** at 9 o'clock last night? \
> **Mr Binh:** I **was watching** a football match at home. \
> **Police officer:** And your wife? \
> **Mr Binh:** She **was reading** in the bedroom **while** I **was watching** TV. \
> **Police officer:** Did you hear anything strange? \
> **Mr Binh:** Yes. We **were getting** ready for bed **when** we **heard** a loud noise outside.

::: details Vietnamese translation

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Formula: **was/were + V-ing**.
- Use it for things **in progress** at a moment in the past, actions that were **interrupted**, and two actions **at the same time**.
- **when** + past simple (short action that interrupts); **while** + past continuous (longer action).
:::
