# Future simple and be going to

Both **will** and **be going to** talk about the future, but they are used in different situations. In short: **will** is for things you **decide right now** or **guess from your feelings**; **be going to** is for things you **planned before** or **predict from clear signs**.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive (will) | S + will + V | I **will help** you. |
| Negative (will) | S + will not (won't) + V | I **won't tell** anyone. |
| Question (will) | Will + S + V? | **Will** you **come** tomorrow? |
| Positive (going to) | S + am/is/are + going to + V | She **is going to buy** a car. |
| Negative (going to) | S + am/is/are + not + going to + V | She **isn't going to buy** a car. |
| Question (going to) | Am/Is/Are + S + going to + V? | **Is** she **going to buy** a car? |
:::

### Short forms

| Full form | Short form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I will / you will / she will | I'll / you'll / she'll | I'**ll** call you later. |
| will not | won't | It **won't** take long. |
| I am going to | I'm going to | I'**m going to** cook tonight. |
| he is going to | he's going to | He'**s going to** sell his motorbike. |

::: warning IMPORTANT
After **will** and after **going to**, always use the **base form** of the verb. No -s, -ed or -ing, and no *to* after *will*.

She **will go** (don't write *will goes*, *will to go*) · She is going to **go** (don't write *going to went*)
:::

## How to use

### 1. Deciding at the moment of speaking → will

You hadn't planned it before; you decide after hearing the situation.

- The phone is ringing. – I'**ll get** it.<br>*Điện thoại đang reo. – Để tôi nghe.*
- I'm cold. – I'**ll close** the window.<br>*Lạnh quá. – Để tôi đóng cửa sổ.*
- I'm tired. I think I'**ll go** to bed early.<br>*Tôi mệt rồi. Chắc tôi sẽ đi ngủ sớm.*
- What would you like? – I'**ll have** a coffee, please.<br>*Anh dùng gì ạ? – Cho tôi một ly cà phê.*

### 2. Plans and intentions made before → be going to

You thought about it and decided before you spoke.

- I'**m going to visit** my grandma this weekend.<br>*Cuối tuần này tôi sẽ về thăm bà.*
- We'**re going to paint** the kitchen next month.<br>*Tháng sau chúng tôi định sơn lại bếp.*
- He **isn't going to study** abroad. He wants to work.<br>*Anh ấy không định đi du học. Anh ấy muốn đi làm.*
- What **are** you **going to do** after graduation?<br>*Tốt nghiệp xong bạn định làm gì?*

### 3. Predictions from feelings, with no evidence → will

Often used with *I think, I'm sure, probably, maybe, I hope*.

- I think it **will be** a good year.<br>*Tôi nghĩ năm nay sẽ là một năm tốt.*
- Don't worry. You'**ll pass** the exam.<br>*Đừng lo. Bạn sẽ thi đỗ thôi.*
- She **will** probably **be** late again.<br>*Chắc cô ấy lại đến muộn nữa.*
- I'm sure the kids **will love** this present.<br>*Tôi chắc bọn trẻ sẽ thích món quà này.*

### 4. Predictions from signs you can see now → be going to

You see or hear something, so you are sure it is about to happen.

- Look at those dark clouds! It'**s going to rain**.<br>*Nhìn đám mây đen kìa! Trời sắp mưa rồi.*
- Be careful! You'**re going to fall**!<br>*Cẩn thận! Bạn sắp ngã đấy!*
- She's pregnant. She'**s going to have** a baby in May.<br>*Cô ấy đang mang thai. Tháng Năm cô ấy sẽ sinh em bé.*
- It's 8:55 and the meeting starts at 9. We'**re going to be** late.<br>*8 giờ 55 rồi mà 9 giờ họp. Chúng ta sẽ muộn mất.*

### 5. Promises, offers of help and requests → will

- I **won't forget** your birthday again. I promise.<br>*Anh hứa sẽ không quên sinh nhật em nữa.*
- That bag looks heavy. I'**ll carry** it for you.<br>*Cái túi trông nặng đấy. Để tôi xách giúp.*
- **Will** you **help** me with this box, please?<br>*Bạn giúp tôi bê cái thùng này được không?*
- I'**ll pay** you back tomorrow.<br>*Mai tôi sẽ trả lại tiền cho bạn.*

::: tip TIP
To **offer help** as a question, use **Shall I…?**: **Shall** I **open** the window? (*Tôi mở cửa sổ nhé?*)
:::

## will or be going to?

| Situation | will | be going to |
|---|---|---|
| Decisions | decided while speaking<br>We're out of milk? I'**ll buy** some.<br>*Hết sữa à? Để anh đi mua.* | decided before<br>I'**m going to buy** milk after work.<br>*Tan làm tôi định đi mua sữa.* |
| Predictions | from feelings<br>I think he'**ll win**.<br>*Tôi nghĩ anh ấy sẽ thắng.* | from clear signs<br>He's 10 points ahead. He'**s going to win**.<br>*Anh ấy dẫn 10 điểm. Anh ấy sắp thắng rồi.* |
| Promises, offers | use will<br>I'**ll help** you tomorrow.<br>*Mai tôi sẽ giúp bạn.* | not used |

Things with a set time or booked tickets can also use the [present continuous](./present-continuous): I'**m meeting** Lan at 7.

## Signal words

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| tomorrow | ngày mai | I'**ll call** you **tomorrow**. |
| tonight | tối nay | We'**re going to eat** out **tonight**. |
| next week / month / year | tuần / tháng / năm sau | She'**s going to start** a new job **next month**. |
| in + length of time | … nữa | The bus **will arrive** **in 5 minutes**. |
| soon | sắp, chẳng bao lâu nữa | I'**ll be** back **soon**. |
| I think / probably / maybe | tôi nghĩ / có lẽ | **Maybe** it **will be** sunny tomorrow. |

## No will after when / if / as soon as

In a clause starting with **when, if, as soon as, before, after, until**, use the **present simple**, even though you mean the future. *will* goes only in the other part of the sentence.

| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right |
|---|---|
| When I will get home, I'll call you. | **When** I **get** home, I'll call you. |
| If it will rain, we'll stay in. | **If** it **rains**, we'll stay in. |
| I'll tell her as soon as she will arrive. | I'll tell her **as soon as** she **arrives**. |
| Wait here until I will come back. | Wait here **until** I **come** back. |

- **When** I **get** home, I'll call you.<br>*Khi về đến nhà, tôi sẽ gọi cho bạn.*
- **If** it **rains** tomorrow, we'll stay at home.<br>*Nếu mai trời mưa, chúng ta sẽ ở nhà.*
- I'll tell her the news **as soon as** she **arrives**.<br>*Ngay khi cô ấy đến, tôi sẽ báo tin cho cô ấy.*
- Please turn off the lights **before** you **leave**.<br>*Trước khi đi, nhớ tắt đèn nhé.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
**when / if / as soon as / before / after / until** + **present simple**; only the other part uses **will**.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I will to call you. | I **will call** you. | No *to* after *will*. |
| She will goes to school. | She **will go** to school. | After *will* comes the base form. |
| She going to buy a car. | She **is going to** buy a car. | *am/is/are* is missing before *going to*. |
| When I will finish, I'll call you. | When I **finish**, I'll call you. | After *when*, use the present simple. |
| Look at the clouds! It will rain. | Look at the clouds! It'**s going to rain**. | There is a sign right in front of you, so use *be going to*. |
:::

## In context

> **Linh:** **Are** you **going to do** anything this summer? \
> **Hung:** Yes, I'**m going to take** my family to Nha Trang. I booked the hotel last week. \
> **Linh:** Nice! I think your kids **will love** it. \
> **Hung:** I hope so. When we **get** there, we'**ll go** to the beach first. \
> **Linh:** Can I borrow your big suitcase? I'**ll give** it back next week. \
> **Hung:** Sure. I'**ll bring** it tomorrow.

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **will + V**: deciding while you speak, guessing from feelings, promises, offers of help.
- **am/is/are going to + V**: plans made before, predictions from signs you can see.
- After **when / if / as soon as / until / before / after**, use the **present simple**, not *will*.
:::
