# Past simple

Use it to talk about things that **happened and finished** in the past, often at a clear time (yesterday, last year, two days ago). It is the main tense for telling stories and describing a day or a trip.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + V2 / V-ed | She **visited** her parents last week. |
| Negative | S + did not (didn't) + V | She **didn't visit** them yesterday. |
| Question | Did + S + V? | **Did** she **visit** them? |
:::

In the past simple, **every subject** (I, you, he, she, we, they) uses the same verb form. There is no extra -s for he/she/it.

### Regular and irregular verbs

| Type | How to make V2 | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Regular | add **-ed** | work → **worked**, play → **played**, live → **lived** |
| Irregular | learn each one by heart | go → **went**, eat → **ate**, buy → **bought** |

- I **worked** late last night.<br>*Tối qua tôi làm việc muộn.*
- We **played** badminton after school.<br>*Chúng tôi chơi cầu lông sau giờ học.*
- He **went** to Hue last month.<br>*Tháng trước anh ấy đi Huế.*
- My son **ate** all the cake!<br>*Con trai tôi ăn hết cả cái bánh!*

How to spell and say the -ed ending: see [Adding -s/-es, -ed, -ing and pronunciation](./endings-pronunciation). For a list of irregular verbs: see [Common irregular verbs](./irregular-verbs).

### The verb be: was / were

| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / He / She / It | I **was** tired. | I **wasn't** tired. | **Was** he at home? |
| You / We / They | They **were** late. | They **weren't** late. | **Were** you busy? |
| Singular nouns (the film) | The film **was** boring. | The film **wasn't** boring. | **Was** the film good? |
| Plural nouns (the shoes) | The shoes **were** cheap. | The shoes **weren't** cheap. | **Were** the shoes expensive? |

- I **was** at home all day yesterday.<br>*Hôm qua tôi ở nhà cả ngày.*
- The weather **wasn't** very good.<br>*Thời tiết không đẹp lắm.*
- **Were** you happy with the hotel?<br>*Bạn có hài lòng với khách sạn không?*

::: warning IMPORTANT
Once you have **did / didn't**, the main verb goes back to the **base form**. *did* already carries the past meaning.

She **went** → She didn't **go** → Did she **go**?
:::

## How to use

### 1. Finished actions at a definite time

- I **bought** this phone two months ago.<br>*Tôi mua cái điện thoại này hai tháng trước.*
- We **got** married in 2018.<br>*Chúng tôi cưới nhau năm 2018.*
- **Did** you **see** the football match last night?<br>*Tối qua bạn có xem trận bóng đá không?*
- My grandfather **didn't go** to university.<br>*Ông tôi không học đại học.*

### 2. A series of actions, one after another

Tell what happened step by step, one thing after the next, like telling a story.

- I **got** up, **had** a shower and **left** for work.<br>*Tôi dậy, tắm rồi đi làm.*
- She **opened** the door, **looked** around and **walked** in.<br>*Cô ấy mở cửa, nhìn quanh rồi bước vào.*
- We **arrived** at the hotel, **checked** in and **went** to the beach.<br>*Chúng tôi đến khách sạn, nhận phòng rồi ra biển.*
- He **paid** the bill and **said** goodbye.<br>*Anh ấy trả tiền rồi chào tạm biệt.*

### 3. Past habits (used to)

Things you **often did before but don't do anymore**. You can use the past simple or **used to + V**.

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + used to + V | I **used to smoke**. |
| Negative | S + didn't use to + V | She **didn't use to like** fish. |
| Question | Did + S + use to + V? | **Did** you **use to live** here? |

- I **used to walk** to school every day.<br>*Hồi trước ngày nào tôi cũng đi bộ đến trường.*
- My dad **used to smoke**, but he stopped five years ago.<br>*Bố tôi từng hút thuốc, nhưng đã bỏ năm năm trước.*
- We **didn't use to have** a car.<br>*Hồi trước nhà tôi không có ô tô.*
- **Did** you **use to play** with dolls when you were small?<br>*Hồi nhỏ bạn có hay chơi búp bê không?*

::: tip TIP
*used to* is only for the **past**. For a habit now, use the present simple with *usually*: I **usually** walk to work. (don't write *I use to walk*)
:::

## Signal words

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| yesterday | hôm qua | I **called** her **yesterday**. |
| last night / week / year | tối qua / tuần trước / năm ngoái | We **moved** house **last year**. |
| … ago | … trước | He **left** ten minutes **ago**. |
| in + year | vào năm | She **was born** **in 1995**. |
| when I was a child / young | khi tôi còn nhỏ / còn trẻ | I **lived** in the countryside **when I was a child**. |
| this morning (when it is over) | sáng nay | I **missed** the bus **this morning**. |
| the day before yesterday | hôm kia | They **arrived** **the day before yesterday**. |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Did you went to school? | Did you **go** to school? | With *did*, the verb goes back to the base form. |
| I didn't saw him. | I didn't **see** him. | *didn't* + base form. |
| She goed home early. | She **went** home early. | *go* is an irregular verb. |
| They was at the party. | They **were** at the party. | *they* goes with *were*. |
| I was go to Da Lat last year. | I **went** to Da Lat last year. | Don't use *was* together with an ordinary verb. |
:::

## In context

> **Hoa:** How **was** your weekend? \
> **Tuan:** Great! I **took** my kids to the zoo on Saturday. \
> **Hoa:** **Did** they **like** it? \
> **Tuan:** Yes, they **loved** the monkeys. We **had** lunch there and **came** home at 4. \
> **Hoa:** I **didn't do** much. I **stayed** home and **cleaned** the house. \
> **Tuan:** That's OK. I **used to spend** every weekend like that too!

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Use it for things that **finished** in the past, often with a clear time (yesterday, last…, … ago).
- Positive sentences use **V2 / V-ed**; negatives and questions use **did + base form**.
- **be** becomes **was** (I, he, she, it) and **were** (you, we, they).
- Old habits that have stopped: **used to + V**.
:::
