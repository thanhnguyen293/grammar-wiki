# Present perfect

Use it to talk about things that **happened but still matter now**: the result is still there, the situation is still going on, or it is an experience up to now. Vietnamese learners often mix this tense up with the past simple, so the comparison below is very important.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + have/has + V3 | She **has finished** her work. |
| Negative | S + have/has + not + V3 | She **hasn't finished** yet. |
| Question | Have/Has + S + V3? | **Has** she **finished**? |
:::

### Forms by subject

| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I / You / We / They | I'**ve eaten**. | I **haven't eaten**. | **Have** you **eaten**? |
| He / She / It | He'**s left**. | He **hasn't left**. | **Has** he **left**? |
| Singular nouns (the bus) | The bus **has arrived**. | The bus **hasn't arrived**. | **Has** the bus **arrived**? |

::: warning IMPORTANT
**have/has** goes with **V3** (the third column in the [irregular verb table](./irregular-verbs)), not V2.

go → went → **gone**: She has **gone** (don't write *has went*).
:::

## How to use

### 1. Something happened and you can see the result now

You don't say when. What matters is **the situation now**.

- I'**ve lost** my keys. I can't open the door.<br>*Tôi làm mất chìa khóa rồi. Tôi không mở cửa được.*
- Someone **has broken** the window.<br>*Ai đó làm vỡ cửa sổ rồi.*
- She'**s cut** her hair. It looks nice.<br>*Cô ấy cắt tóc rồi. Trông đẹp đấy.*
- The price of rice **has gone** up.<br>*Giá gạo tăng rồi.*

### 2. Something started in the past and is still true now (for / since)

- I'**ve lived** in Hanoi for ten years.<br>*Tôi sống ở Hà Nội được mười năm rồi.* (and I still live there)
- She **has worked** here since 2020.<br>*Cô ấy làm ở đây từ năm 2020.*
- We'**ve known** each other since we were kids.<br>*Chúng tôi quen nhau từ hồi còn bé.*
- How long **have** you **had** this car?<br>*Bạn có chiếc xe này bao lâu rồi?*

### 3. Experience: have done / have never done (ever / never)

Ask or talk about experiences in your whole life, without saying when.

- **Have** you **ever been** to Sa Pa?<br>*Bạn đã bao giờ đến Sa Pa chưa?*
- I'**ve never eaten** durian.<br>*Tôi chưa bao giờ ăn sầu riêng.*
- My mother **has visited** Japan twice.<br>*Mẹ tôi đã sang Nhật hai lần.*
- This is the best film I'**ve** ever **seen**.<br>*Đây là bộ phim hay nhất tôi từng xem.*

### 4. Just, already, yet

| Word | Meaning | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| just | vừa mới | between have and V3 | I'**ve just had** lunch. |
| already | đã … rồi | between have and V3 (sooner than expected) | She'**s already left**. |
| yet | chưa / … chưa? | end of the sentence (negatives and questions) | I **haven't finished** **yet**. / **Have** you **finished** **yet**? |

- I'**ve just had** lunch, so I'm not hungry.<br>*Tôi vừa ăn trưa xong nên không đói.*
- Don't call him. He'**s already gone** to bed.<br>*Đừng gọi anh ấy. Anh ấy đi ngủ rồi.*
- **Have** you **done** your homework **yet**?<br>*Con làm bài tập chưa?*
- The parcel **hasn't arrived** **yet**.<br>*Gói hàng vẫn chưa đến.*

## For and since

| Word | Goes with | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **for** | a **length** of time: two days, three years, a long time, ages | được bao lâu | I've worked here **for** three years. |
| **since** | a **starting point** in time: 2019, Monday, last week, 8 o'clock, I was a child | từ khi | I've worked here **since** 2021. |

| for + length of time | since + starting point |
|---|---|
| **for** two hours *(được hai tiếng)* | **since** 9 am *(từ 9 giờ sáng)* |
| **for** five days *(được năm ngày)* | **since** Monday *(từ thứ Hai)* |
| **for** ten years *(được mười năm)* | **since** 2016 *(từ năm 2016)* |
| **for** a long time *(lâu rồi)* | **since** I was a child *(từ khi tôi còn nhỏ)* |

- I haven't seen her **for** a long time.<br>*Lâu lắm rồi tôi không gặp cô ấy.*
- I've had a headache **since** this morning.<br>*Tôi bị đau đầu từ sáng đến giờ.*
- They've been married **for** 25 years.<br>*Họ cưới nhau được 25 năm rồi.*
- He hasn't smoked **since** his son was born.<br>*Từ khi con trai ra đời, anh ấy không hút thuốc nữa.*

::: tip TIP
To ask "how long?", use **How long have you…?**. Answer with **for** (how long) or **since** (from when): How long have you lived here? – **For** five years. / **Since** 2021.
:::

## Compared with the past simple

**A specific finished time** (yesterday, last week, in 2019, … ago) → **past simple**. **No time given**, or the action **still matters now** → **present perfect**.

| Present perfect | Past simple |
|---|---|
| I'**ve lost** my phone.<br>*Tôi làm mất điện thoại rồi.* (still not found) | I **lost** my phone yesterday.<br>*Hôm qua tôi làm mất điện thoại.* (says when) |
| She **has lived** in Hue for 10 years.<br>*Cô ấy sống ở Huế được 10 năm rồi.* (she still lives there) | She **lived** in Hue for 10 years.<br>*Cô ấy từng sống ở Huế 10 năm.* (she doesn't live there now) |
| **Have** you ever **been** to Japan?<br>*Bạn đã từng đến Nhật chưa?* (in your whole life) | **Did** you **go** to Japan last year?<br>*Năm ngoái bạn có đi Nhật không?* (last year) |
| I'**ve seen** that film.<br>*Tôi xem phim đó rồi.* | I **saw** that film last Sunday.<br>*Chủ nhật tuần trước tôi xem phim đó.* |
| He **has** already **eaten**.<br>*Anh ấy ăn rồi.* | He **ate** at 6 o'clock.<br>*Anh ấy ăn lúc 6 giờ.* |

## Been and gone

Both are V3 of *go*, but they mean different things.

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **has/have been** to | went there and **came back** | She **has been** to Da Lat. *Cô ấy đã từng đi Đà Lạt (giờ đã về).* |
| **has/have gone** to | went there and **hasn't come back**, is there now | She **has gone** to Da Lat. *Cô ấy đi Đà Lạt rồi (giờ đang ở đó).* |

- Where's Dad? – He'**s gone** to the market.<br>*Bố đâu rồi? – Bố đi chợ rồi.* (not back yet)
- I'**ve been** to the market. I bought some fish.<br>*Tôi vừa đi chợ về. Tôi mua ít cá.*
- **Have** you ever **been** to Korea?<br>*Bạn đã từng đi Hàn Quốc chưa?*

## Signal words

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| just | vừa mới | I'**ve just arrived**. |
| already | rồi | We'**ve already paid**. |
| yet | chưa | **Has** the bus **come yet**? |
| ever / never | đã từng / chưa từng | I'**ve never tried** sushi. |
| for / since | được / từ khi | She'**s worked** here **since** May. |
| so far / up to now | cho đến nay | We'**ve sold** 50 cakes **so far**. |
| recently / lately | gần đây | **Have** you **seen** any good films **recently**? |
| this week / today (not over yet) | tuần này / hôm nay | I'**ve drunk** three cups of coffee **today**. |
| … times | … lần | He'**s failed** the driving test **twice**. |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I have seen him yesterday. | I **saw** him yesterday. | With a clear time (*yesterday*), use the past simple. |
| I live here since 2020. | I'**ve lived** here since 2020. | Something lasting from the past until now takes the present perfect. |
| She has went home. | She has **gone** home. | After *has* comes V3, not V2. |
| I've worked here since five years. | I've worked here **for** five years. | A length of time goes with *for*. |
| Did you ever eat durian? | **Have** you ever **eaten** durian? | Asking about life experience takes the present perfect. |
:::

## In context

> **Nga:** Hi Long! I **haven't seen** you for ages. Where **have** you **been**? \
> **Long:** I'**ve been** really busy. I'**ve started** a new job. \
> **Nga:** Great! How long **have** you **worked** there? \
> **Long:** Since March. I **started** on the 15th. \
> **Nga:** **Have** you **met** your new boss **yet**? \
> **Long:** Yes, I **have**. She's very kind. She'**s** already **helped** me a lot.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Nga:** Chào Long! Lâu lắm rồi không gặp cậu. Dạo này cậu đi đâu thế?<br>
**Long:** Tớ bận lắm. Tớ mới bắt đầu công việc mới.<br>
**Nga:** Hay quá! Cậu làm ở đó được bao lâu rồi?<br>
**Long:** Từ tháng Ba. Tớ bắt đầu từ ngày 15.<br>
**Nga:** Cậu gặp sếp mới chưa?<br>
**Long:** Gặp rồi. Chị ấy tốt bụng lắm. Chị ấy giúp tớ nhiều rồi.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Formula: **have/has + V3**.
- Use it for results you see now, things lasting until now (**for** + length, **since** + starting point), experience (**ever / never**), and **just / already / yet**.
- With a specific finished time (yesterday, last…, … ago), use the **past simple**.
- **been to**: went and came back; **gone to**: went and hasn't come back.
:::
