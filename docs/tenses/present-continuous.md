# Present continuous

Use it to talk about things **happening right now**, **temporary** situations at this time in your life, and **arranged plans** for the near future. You meet this tense every time someone asks "What are you doing?".

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | S + am/is/are + V-ing | She **is cooking** dinner. |
| Negative | S + am/is/are + not + V-ing | She **isn't cooking** now. |
| Question | Am/Is/Are + S + V-ing? | **Is** she **cooking**? |
:::

### Forms by subject

| Subject | Positive | Negative | Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | I'**m working**. | I'**m not working**. | **Am** I **talking** too fast? |
| He / She / It | He'**s sleeping**. | He **isn't sleeping**. | **Is** he **sleeping**? |
| You / We / They | They'**re playing** football. | They **aren't playing**. | **Are** they **playing**? |
| Singular nouns (my mom) | My mom **is shopping**. | My mom **isn't shopping**. | **Is** your mom **shopping**? |

::: warning IMPORTANT
This tense needs **both parts**: **am/is/are** and **V-ing**. If one is missing, the sentence is wrong.

I **am** work**ing** → don't write *I working* or *I am work*.
:::

How to add -ing (make → making, run → running): see [Adding -s/-es, -ed, -ing](./endings-pronunciation).

## How to use

### 1. Things happening right now

- I'**m having** lunch. Can I call you back?<br>*Tôi đang ăn trưa. Lát tôi gọi lại được không?*
- Look! It'**s raining**.<br>*Nhìn kìa! Trời đang mưa.*
- Be quiet! The baby **is sleeping**.<br>*Khẽ thôi! Em bé đang ngủ.*
- What **are** you **doing**? – I'**m watching** TV.<br>*Bạn đang làm gì đấy? – Tôi đang xem tivi.*

### 2. Temporary things happening around this time

You are not necessarily doing it at the moment of speaking, but it is something for "these days" and it will end.

- I'**m staying** with my aunt this month.<br>*Tháng này tôi ở nhờ nhà dì.*
- My brother **is learning** to drive.<br>*Anh tôi đang học lái xe.*
- She **isn't working** this week. She's on holiday.<br>*Tuần này cô ấy không đi làm. Cô ấy đang nghỉ phép.*
- **Are** you **reading** any good books these days?<br>*Dạo này bạn có đang đọc cuốn sách nào hay không?*

### 3. Arranged plans for the near future

Things you have agreed to, booked tickets for, or put in your diary. The sentence usually has a future time word (tonight, tomorrow, next week).

- I'**m meeting** Lan at 7 tonight.<br>*Tối nay tôi hẹn gặp Lan lúc 7 giờ.*
- We'**re flying** to Phu Quoc next Monday.<br>*Thứ Hai tuần sau chúng tôi bay đi Phú Quốc.*
- My parents **are coming** for dinner on Sunday.<br>*Chủ nhật bố mẹ tôi sẽ sang ăn tối.*
- What **are** you **doing** this weekend?<br>*Cuối tuần này bạn định làm gì?*

### 4. Complaining about annoying habits (with always)

**always** with the continuous is used to complain: "you're always…", "all the time…".

- He'**s always losing** his keys!<br>*Anh ấy suốt ngày làm mất chìa khóa!*
- You'**re always leaving** your socks on the floor.<br>*Lúc nào em cũng vứt tất ra sàn.*
- My neighbor **is always playing** loud music at night.<br>*Nhà hàng xóm tối nào cũng mở nhạc ầm ĩ.*

::: tip TIP
*She always gets up early* (present simple) just describes a habit. *She's always complaining* (continuous) sounds **annoyed**.
:::

## Signal words

| Word / phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| now / right now | bây giờ / ngay bây giờ | I'**m cooking** **now**. |
| at the moment | lúc này | She'**s talking** on the phone **at the moment**. |
| at present / currently | hiện nay | He'**s currently living** in Da Nang. |
| today / this week / this month | hôm nay / tuần này / tháng này | I'**m working** from home **this week**. |
| these days | dạo này | My son **is studying** hard **these days**. |
| Look! / Listen! | Nhìn kìa! / Nghe này! | **Listen!** Someone **is knocking** at the door. |
| Be quiet! / Hurry up! | Trật tự! / Nhanh lên! | **Hurry up!** The bus **is coming**. |

## Verbs not used in the continuous

Some verbs describe a **state** (feelings, thoughts, owning, the senses), not an action. They **don't take -ing**, even when you talk about right now. Use the present simple instead.

| Group | Verbs | ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liking, wanting | like, love, hate, want, need, prefer | I'm wanting a drink. | I **want** a drink. |
| Thinking, knowing | know, understand, believe, remember, forget, mean | Are you understanding? | **Do** you **understand**? |
| Owning | have (= own), own, belong | She's having a new car. | She **has** a new car. |
| Senses | see, hear, smell, taste | I'm hearing a strange noise. | I **hear** a strange noise. |
| Others | seem, cost, contain | This bag is costing 200,000 dong. | This bag **costs** 200,000 dong. |

Correct examples:

- I **know** her phone number.<br>*Tôi biết số điện thoại của cô ấy.*
- This soup **tastes** great.<br>*Món canh này ngon quá.*
- **Do** you **remember** my name?<br>*Bạn có nhớ tên tôi không?*
- This house **belongs** to my uncle.<br>*Ngôi nhà này là của chú tôi.*

::: tip TIP
Some verbs have two meanings. When they mean an **action**, you can still use the continuous:
- I **have** two kids. (own) → I'**m having** breakfast. (eating)<br>*Tôi có hai con. → Tôi đang ăn sáng.*
- I **think** it's a good idea. (believe) → I'**m thinking** about my trip. (considering)<br>*Tôi nghĩ đó là ý hay. → Tôi đang nghĩ về chuyến đi.*
:::

## Compared with the present simple

| Present simple: regular, long-term | Present continuous: in progress, temporary |
|---|---|
| I **drink** coffee every morning.<br>*Sáng nào tôi cũng uống cà phê.* | I'**m drinking** tea now.<br>*Bây giờ tôi đang uống trà.* |
| He **works** in a bank.<br>*Anh ấy làm ở ngân hàng.* (long-term job) | He'**s working** in a café this summer.<br>*Hè này anh ấy làm ở quán cà phê.* (temporary) |
| We **live** in Hanoi.<br>*Chúng tôi sống ở Hà Nội.* | We'**re staying** at a hotel this week.<br>*Tuần này chúng tôi ở khách sạn.* |
| She usually **walks** to school.<br>*Cô bé thường đi bộ đến trường.* | It's raining, so she'**s taking** a taxi today.<br>*Trời mưa nên hôm nay cô bé đi taxi.* |
| The train **leaves** at 8.<br>*Tàu chạy lúc 8 giờ.* (timetable) | I'**m leaving** at 8.<br>*Tôi sẽ đi lúc 8 giờ.* (personal plan) |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I working now. | I **am working** now. | *am/is/are* is missing. |
| She is cook dinner. | She is **cooking** dinner. | After *be* you need V-ing. |
| I am knowing the answer. | I **know** the answer. | *know* is a state verb, so no continuous. |
| Look! It rains. | Look! It'**s raining**. | Something happening in front of you takes the continuous. |
| Is he swiming? | Is he **swimming**? | *swim* doubles the *m* before -ing. |
:::

## In context

> **Mai:** Hi Nam! What **are** you **doing**? \
> **Nam:** I'**m waiting** for the bus. It'**s raining**, so I'**m not riding** my bike today. \
> **Mai:** Where **are** you **going**? \
> **Nam:** To the library. I'**m preparing** for an English test these days. \
> **Mai:** Good luck! By the way, we'**re having** a party on Saturday. Can you come? \
> **Nam:** Sure! Oh, the bus **is coming**. See you!

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Mai:** Chào Nam! Cậu đang làm gì thế?<br>
**Nam:** Tớ đang đợi xe buýt. Trời đang mưa nên hôm nay tớ không đi xe đạp.<br>
**Mai:** Cậu đi đâu đấy?<br>
**Nam:** Đến thư viện. Dạo này tớ đang ôn thi tiếng Anh.<br>
**Mai:** Chúc may mắn! À, thứ Bảy này bọn tớ tổ chức tiệc. Cậu đến được không?<br>
**Nam:** Được chứ! Ôi, xe buýt đến rồi. Gặp lại sau nhé!
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Formula: **am/is/are + V-ing**, with no part missing.
- Use it for things **happening now**, **temporary** situations, **arranged plans**, and **always** when complaining.
- State verbs (know, like, want, have = own…) don't take the continuous.
:::
