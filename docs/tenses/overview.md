# Overview of the 12 tenses

Vietnamese just adds *đang, đã, sẽ, rồi* before the verb; English **changes the form of the verb**. There are 12 tenses, but really they are just **3 times** combined with **4 forms**, so one look at the table below shows the pattern.

## Formula

Read the table by row for **time** (present, past, future) and by column for **form** (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous). Every example uses the same verb *cook* so you can see exactly what changes.

::: info FORMULA
| Time / Form | Simple | Continuous | Perfect | Perfect continuous |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Present** | S + V / V(s/es)<br>I **cook**. | S + am/is/are + V-ing<br>I **am cooking**. | S + have/has + V3<br>I **have cooked**. | S + have/has been + V-ing<br>I **have been cooking**. |
| **Past** | S + V2 / V-ed<br>I **cooked**. | S + was/were + V-ing<br>I **was cooking**. | S + had + V3<br>I **had cooked**. | S + had been + V-ing<br>I **had been cooking**. |
| **Future** | S + will + V<br>I **will cook**. | S + will be + V-ing<br>I **will be cooking**. | S + will have + V3<br>I **will have cooked**. | S + will have been + V-ing<br>I **will have been cooking**. |
:::

### Symbols in the formulas

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| S | subject (the person or thing doing the action) | **My mother** cooks. |
| V | base form of the verb | cook, go, eat |
| V(s/es) | verb with -s/-es (with he/she/it) | She **cooks**. He **goes**. |
| V2 / V-ed | past form of the verb | cooked, went, ate |
| V3 | past participle (column 3 in the irregular verb table) | cooked, gone, eaten |
| V-ing | verb with -ing | cooking, going, eating |

::: tip TIP
For regular verbs, V2 and V3 are the same: both add **-ed** (cook → cooked → cooked). Irregular verbs must be learned by heart (go → went → gone). See the [irregular verb table](./irregular-verbs).
:::

## 12 tenses through one task: cooking dinner

### Present

- I **cook** dinner every evening.<br>*Tối nào tôi cũng nấu cơm.* (present simple)
- Sorry, I can't talk now. I **am cooking** dinner.<br>*Xin lỗi, giờ tôi không nói chuyện được. Tôi đang nấu cơm.* (present continuous)
- I **have** just **cooked** dinner. Let's eat!<br>*Tôi vừa nấu cơm xong. Ăn thôi!* (present perfect)
- I **have been cooking** for two hours, so I'm tired.<br>*Tôi nấu suốt hai tiếng rồi nên mệt quá.* (present perfect continuous)

### Past

- I **cooked** dinner last night.<br>*Tối qua tôi nấu cơm.* (past simple)
- I **was cooking** dinner at 7 pm yesterday.<br>*Lúc 7 giờ tối qua, tôi đang nấu cơm.* (past continuous)
- I **had cooked** dinner before my wife came home.<br>*Tôi đã nấu cơm xong trước khi vợ tôi về.* (past perfect)
- I **had been cooking** for an hour when the guests arrived.<br>*Tôi đã nấu được một tiếng thì khách đến.* (past perfect continuous)

### Future

- I **will cook** dinner tomorrow.<br>*Mai tôi sẽ nấu cơm.* (future simple)
- At 7 pm tomorrow, I **will be cooking** dinner.<br>*Lúc 7 giờ tối mai, tôi sẽ đang nấu cơm.* (future continuous)
- I **will have cooked** dinner by 7 pm.<br>*Trước 7 giờ tối, tôi sẽ nấu cơm xong.* (future perfect)
- By 7 pm, I **will have been cooking** for two hours.<br>*Đến 7 giờ tối là tôi đã nấu được hai tiếng.* (future perfect continuous)

## How to use

Each form has its own meaning, whether it is in the present, past or future.

### 1. Simple: the whole action, short and plain

Use it for habits, facts, finished actions and things you will do. It doesn't care whether the action is in progress or how long it lasts.

- She **drinks** tea every morning.<br>*Sáng nào cô ấy cũng uống trà.*
- We **visited** Da Lat last summer.<br>*Hè năm ngoái chúng tôi đi Đà Lạt.*
- I **will call** you tonight.<br>*Tối nay tôi sẽ gọi cho bạn.*

### 2. Continuous: in progress, not finished

Use it when an action is **in progress** at a certain time. The formula always has **be + V-ing**.

- The kids **are sleeping** now.<br>*Bọn trẻ đang ngủ.*
- At 9 pm yesterday, I **was watching** TV.<br>*Lúc 9 giờ tối qua, tôi đang xem tivi.*
- This time tomorrow, we **will be flying** to Da Nang.<br>*Giờ này ngày mai, chúng tôi sẽ đang bay ra Đà Nẵng.*

### 3. Perfect: finished before a point in time

Use it when an action is **already finished** before a point in time (now, a moment in the past, a moment in the future). The formula always has **have + V3**.

- I **have finished** my homework.<br>*Con làm bài tập xong rồi.* (point in time: now)
- The film **had started** when we arrived.<br>*Lúc chúng tôi đến thì phim đã chiếu rồi.* (point in time: when we arrived)
- By Friday, I **will have finished** the report.<br>*Trước thứ Sáu, tôi sẽ làm xong báo cáo.* (point in time: Friday)

### 4. Perfect continuous: going on without a break up to a point in time

Use it when you want to stress **how long** an action has lasted up to a point in time. The formula always has **have been + V-ing**.

- She **has been working** here for five years.<br>*Cô ấy làm ở đây được năm năm rồi.*
- They **had been waiting** for an hour when the bus came.<br>*Họ đã chờ cả tiếng đồng hồ thì xe buýt mới đến.*
- By June, I **will have been learning** English for a year.<br>*Đến tháng Sáu là tôi học tiếng Anh được một năm.*

::: warning IMPORTANT
Look at the **auxiliary verb** to know the form: **be + V-ing** is continuous, **have + V3** is perfect, **have been + V-ing** is perfect continuous.

Look at the **first word** of the verb phrase to know the time: *am/is/are/have/has* is present, *was/were/had* is past, *will* is future.
:::

## Which 5 tenses to learn first

You don't need to learn all 12 tenses at once. The five tenses below cover most everyday sentences. Get them right first, then move on to the others.

| Tense | Use it for | Example |
|---|---|---|
| [Present simple](./present-simple) | habits, facts, timetables | I **get up** at 6 every day. |
| [Present continuous](./present-continuous) | what you are doing as you speak | I'**m reading** a book now. |
| [Past simple](./past-simple) | finished actions at a clear time | I **met** her yesterday. |
| [Future simple and be going to](./future-simple) | things you will do, plans | I'**m going to buy** a new phone. |
| [Present perfect](./present-perfect) | things that happened and still matter now | I'**ve lost** my keys. |

::: tip TIP
Before you learn the past and perfect tenses, look at [how to add -s/-es, -ed, -ing](./endings-pronunciation) and the [irregular verb table](./irregular-verbs). Wrong verb endings are the most common mistake for beginners.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I cooking dinner now. | I **am cooking** dinner now. | Continuous tenses can't drop *be*. |
| I am cook dinner now. | I **am cooking** dinner now. | After *be* you need V-ing. |
| I have cook dinner. | I **have cooked** dinner. | After *have* comes V3, not the base form. |
| I will cooked tomorrow. | I **will cook** tomorrow. | After *will* always use the base form. |
| Yesterday I cook dinner. | Yesterday I **cooked** dinner. | Vietnamese verbs don't change, but English verbs must change for time. |
:::

## In context

> **Lan:** Something smells good! What **are** you **cooking**? \
> **Minh:** Fish soup. I **cook** it every Sunday. \
> **Lan:** **Have** you **finished**? I'm hungry! \
> **Minh:** Almost. I'**ve been cooking** for an hour. \
> **Lan:** Last week you **burned** the rice because you **were watching** football. \
> **Minh:** Don't worry. I'**ll be** careful this time.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Lan:** Thơm quá! Anh đang nấu gì thế?<br>
**Minh:** Canh cá. Chủ nhật nào anh cũng nấu món này.<br>
**Lan:** Xong chưa anh? Em đói rồi!<br>
**Minh:** Sắp xong. Anh nấu cả tiếng rồi đấy.<br>
**Lan:** Tuần trước anh làm cháy cơm vì mải xem bóng đá.<br>
**Minh:** Đừng lo. Lần này anh sẽ cẩn thận.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Tense name = **time** (present, past, future) + **form** (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous).
- **be + V-ing** is continuous, **have + V3** is perfect, **will** is future.
- Learn these 5 first: present simple, present continuous, past simple, future simple and be going to, present perfect.
:::
