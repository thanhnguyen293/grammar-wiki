# Countable and uncountable nouns

English splits nouns into two groups: **countable** (one apple, two apples) and **uncountable** (water, money, advice). The group decides whether you use **a / an**, a **plural** form, **many** or **much**.

## Quick comparison

| Point | Countable | Uncountable |
|---|---|---|
| What they are | Things you can count one by one: book, egg, friend | Liquids, materials, ideas: water, rice, love |
| a / an | Yes: **a** book, **an** egg | No: ✗ *a water*, use **some** water |
| Plural | Yes: two **books**, three **eggs** | No: ✗ *waters*, use two **glasses of** water |
| Verb | The book **is** new. The books **are** new. | The water **is** cold. (always singular) |
| A lot | **many** books | **much** water |
| A small number / amount | **a few** friends | **a little** money |
| Asking about quantity | **How many** eggs? | **How much** milk? |
| Works with both | **a lot of** books, **some** books | **a lot of** water, **some** water |

::: warning IMPORTANT
A **singular** countable noun cannot stand alone. It needs **a / an / the / my / this…** in front of it.

I have **a** car. / This is **my** car. (not *I have car.*)
:::

## How to use

### 1. Countable nouns: have singular and plural forms

- I have **a sister** and **two brothers**.<br>*Tôi có một chị gái và hai anh trai.*
- There is **an apple** on the table.<br>*Có một quả táo trên bàn.*
- **These chairs are** very comfortable.<br>*Mấy cái ghế này rất êm.*
- She bought **three bananas** at the market.<br>*Cô ấy mua ba quả chuối ở chợ.*

### 2. Uncountable nouns: no a / an, no -s

- I drink a lot of **water** every day.<br>*Mỗi ngày tôi uống nhiều nước.*
- Do you want some **milk** in your coffee?<br>*Bạn có muốn cho thêm sữa vào cà phê không?*
- **Rice is** cheap here.<br>*Ở đây gạo rẻ.*
- I don't have **time** to cook today.<br>*Hôm nay tôi không có thời gian nấu ăn.*

### 3. many / much, a few / a little

- We don't have **many** eggs. Can you buy some?<br>*Nhà mình không còn nhiều trứng. Anh mua thêm được không?*
- She doesn't drink **much** coffee.<br>*Cô ấy không uống nhiều cà phê.*
- I have **a few** friends in Da Nang.<br>*Tôi có vài người bạn ở Đà Nẵng.*
- Add **a little** salt to the soup.<br>*Cho thêm một chút muối vào súp.*

### 4. Asking about quantity: How many / How much

- **How many** children do you have?<br>*Anh có mấy con?*
- **How much** sugar do you want?<br>*Bạn muốn bao nhiêu đường?*
- **How much money** do you need?<br>*Bạn cần bao nhiêu tiền?*

## Countable in Vietnamese, uncountable in English

This is where Vietnamese learners make the most mistakes. The words below are **uncountable** in English, so they take **no a / an** and **no -s**.

| Noun | In Vietnamese | Correct example | To count it, say |
|---|---|---|---|
| information | thông tin | I need some **information** about the hotel. | a piece of information |
| advice | lời khuyên | My mother gave me some good **advice**. | a piece of advice |
| news | tin tức | The **news is** good! | a piece of news |
| furniture | đồ nội thất | We bought some new **furniture**. | a piece of furniture, a table, a chair |
| luggage | hành lý | How much **luggage** do you have? | a bag, a suitcase |
| homework | bài tập về nhà | I have a lot of **homework** tonight. | an exercise |
| money | tiền | I don't have much **money**. | ten dollars, 50,000 dong |
| bread | bánh mì | I buy **bread** every morning. | a loaf of bread, a slice of bread |
| work | công việc | I have a lot of **work** today. | a job, a task |
| traffic | xe cộ, giao thông | There **is** a lot of **traffic** today. | (use a lot of, heavy) |
| weather | thời tiết | We had **terrible weather** last week. | (not counted) |
| equipment | thiết bị | The gym has a lot of new **equipment**. | a piece of equipment, a machine |
| music | âm nhạc | I listen to **music** every evening. | a song |
| hair | tóc (cả mái tóc) | She has long **hair**. | a hair (one single hair) |

::: tip TIP
*news* ends in -s but it is **not plural**: The news **is** bad. (not *The news are bad.*)
:::

## Units for counting uncountable nouns

To say "one glass" or "two loaves", count the **unit**, not the noun. In the plural, only the unit takes -s.

| Unit | Used with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a piece of | information, advice, furniture, cake, paper | Let me give you **a piece of** advice. |
| a glass of | water, milk, juice | Can I have **a glass of** water, please? |
| a cup of | coffee, tea | I drink **two cups of** coffee every day. |
| a bottle of | water, oil, fish sauce | Buy **a bottle of** fish sauce, please. |
| a bowl of | rice, soup, pho | I ate **two bowls of** rice. |
| a kilo of | rice, meat, sugar | She bought **a kilo of** pork. |
| a loaf of / a slice of | bread | I need **a loaf of** bread for breakfast. |
| a bar of | chocolate, soap | He gave me **a bar of** chocolate. |
| a bag of | rice, sugar, flour | We need **a bag of** sugar. |
| a can of | beer, soda | He drank **a can of** beer. |

::: warning IMPORTANT
In the plural, add -s to the **unit**; the noun stays the same.

two **glasses** of water (not *two glasses of waters*)
:::

## Words that are both countable and uncountable

Some words change their meaning depending on how they are used:

| Word | Uncountable | Countable |
|---|---|---|
| time | I don't have **time**. (time in general) | I've been to Hue **three times**. (occasions) |
| paper | I need some **paper**. (the material) | He reads **a paper** every morning. (a newspaper) |
| coffee | I like **coffee**. (coffee in general) | **Two coffees**, please. (two cups of coffee, when ordering) |
| chicken | We had **chicken** for dinner. (the meat) | They keep ten **chickens**. (the birds) |
| room | There's no **room** in the car. (free space) | The hotel has fifty **rooms**. (separate rooms) |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Can you give me an advice? | Can you give me **some advice**? | *advice* is uncountable, so it can't take *an*. |
| I have many homeworks. | I have **a lot of homework**. | *homework* takes no -s and doesn't go with *many*. |
| The news are bad. | The news **is** bad. | *news* is an uncountable noun, so the verb is singular. |
| How many money do you have? | **How much** money do you have? | *money* is uncountable, so use *much*. |
| We bought new furnitures. | We bought new **furniture**. | *furniture* has no plural form. |
:::

## In context

> **Mai:** Are you ready for the trip? How much **luggage** do you have? \
> **Tuấn:** Just **two bags**. But I need some **information** about the hotel. \
> **Mai:** I have **a lot of information** on my phone. And here's **a piece of advice**: bring **some money** in cash. \
> **Tuấn:** Good idea. How **much money** do I need? \
> **Mai:** About two million dong. Oh, and buy **a bottle of water** and **some bread** for the bus. \
> **Tuấn:** OK. Thanks for **the advice**!

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Mai:** Anh chuẩn bị đi chưa? Anh mang bao nhiêu hành lý?<br>
**Tuấn:** Chỉ hai cái túi thôi. Nhưng anh cần chút thông tin về khách sạn.<br>
**Mai:** Em có nhiều thông tin trong điện thoại đây. Còn đây là một lời khuyên: mang theo ít tiền mặt.<br>
**Tuấn:** Ý hay đấy. Anh cần bao nhiêu tiền?<br>
**Mai:** Khoảng hai triệu đồng. À, mua một chai nước và ít bánh mì để ăn trên xe nhé.<br>
**Tuấn:** Ừ. Cảm ơn lời khuyên của em nhé!
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Countable: takes **a / an**, has a **plural**, uses **many**, **a few**, **How many**.
- Uncountable: **no** a / an, **no** -s, a **singular** verb, uses **much**, **a little**, **How much**.
- Remember the "trap" words: **information, advice, news, furniture, luggage, homework, money, bread**.
- To count them, use a unit: **a piece of, a glass of, a cup of, a bottle of**.
:::
