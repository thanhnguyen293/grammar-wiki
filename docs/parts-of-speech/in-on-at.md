# Prepositions in / on / at

**in, on, at** can all be translated as "ở, vào, lúc", so Vietnamese learners mix them up a lot. A simple way to remember: **in** for something large that surrounds you; **on** for surfaces and days; **at** for a single point or an exact time.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Preposition | Time | Place | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| in | Long periods: parts of the day, months, years, seasons | Inside, cities, countries | **in** May, **in** Hanoi |
| on | Days, days of the week, dates | On a surface, floors, streets | **on** Monday, **on** the table |
| at | Clock times, points in time | A point, an exact address | **at** 7 o'clock, **at** the bus stop |
:::

::: tip TIP
Picture a funnel: **in** is the widest (years, months, cities) → **on** is narrower (days, streets) → **at** is the smallest (clock times, house numbers).
:::

## Time

| Preposition | Used with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at | Clock times | The class starts **at 8:30**. |
| at | noon, midnight, night | I go to bed **at midnight**. |
| at | Holidays (the whole holiday period) | We visit our family **at Tet**. |
| on | Days of the week | I play tennis **on Saturdays**. |
| on | Dates | My birthday is **on 15 June**. |
| on | A special day | We eat cake **on my birthday**. |
| in | Parts of the day | I drink coffee **in the morning**. |
| in | Months | It rains a lot **in August**. |
| in | Years, centuries | I was born **in 1990**. |
| in | Seasons | Hanoi is cold **in winter**. |

### 1. at: clock times, points in time

- The shop opens **at 9 o'clock**.<br>*Cửa hàng mở cửa lúc 9 giờ.*
- Let's meet **at noon**.<br>*Hẹn gặp nhau lúc 12 giờ trưa nhé.*
- I can't sleep **at night**.<br>*Tôi không ngủ được vào ban đêm.*
- What do you usually do **at the weekend**?<br>*Cuối tuần bạn thường làm gì?*

### 2. on: days and dates

- We have a meeting **on Monday**.<br>*Chúng ta có cuộc họp vào thứ Hai.*
- My son was born **on 2 September**.<br>*Con trai tôi sinh ngày 2 tháng 9.*
- The shop is closed **on Sundays**.<br>*Cửa hàng đóng cửa vào các ngày Chủ nhật.*
- I got a lot of gifts **on my birthday**.<br>*Tôi nhận được nhiều quà vào ngày sinh nhật.*

### 3. in: parts of the day, months, years, seasons

- I usually go jogging **in the morning**.<br>*Tôi thường chạy bộ vào buổi sáng.*
- We're going to Da Lat **in December**.<br>*Tháng 12 chúng tôi sẽ đi Đà Lạt.*
- She started this job **in 2020**.<br>*Cô ấy bắt đầu công việc này vào năm 2020.*
- Many tourists come to Sa Pa **in summer**.<br>*Nhiều khách du lịch lên Sa Pa vào mùa hè.*

## Time exceptions

| Case | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Part of **a specific day** | on | I have a meeting **on Monday morning**. |
| Part of a specific day | on | We arrived **on the evening of 5 May**. |
| Night | at (not in) | It's quiet here **at night**. |
| Noon, midnight | at | We have lunch **at noon**. |
| The weekend | at (UK) / on (US) | See you **at the weekend**. / See you **on the weekend**. |

Compare:

- I study **in the evening**.<br>*Tôi học vào buổi tối.* (evenings in general)
- I'm busy **on Friday evening**.<br>*Tối thứ Sáu tôi bận.* (the evening of one specific day)

::: warning IMPORTANT
**Don't use** in / on / at before **next, last, this, every, tomorrow, yesterday, today**.

I'll call you **next week**. (not *on next week*)<br>
We met **last Friday**. (not *on last Friday*)
:::

- See you **this evening**!<br>*Hẹn gặp bạn tối nay!*
- She goes swimming **every morning**.<br>*Sáng nào cô ấy cũng đi bơi.*
- I'm going to Hue **tomorrow**.<br>*Ngày mai tôi đi Huế.*

## Place

| Preposition | Used with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| in | Inside a space | The keys are **in my bag**. |
| in | Cities, countries | My sister lives **in Da Nang**. |
| on | On a surface | There's a picture **on the wall**. |
| on | Floors of a building | Our office is **on the third floor**. |
| on | Streets (without a house number) | The bank is **on Le Loi Street**. |
| at | A specific point | I'll wait for you **at the bus stop**. |
| at | Addresses with a house number | She lives **at 25 Hang Bai Street**. |
| at | A place where an activity happens | We met **at a party**. |

### 1. in: inside, cities, countries

- The milk is **in the fridge**.<br>*Sữa ở trong tủ lạnh.*
- The kids are playing **in the garden**.<br>*Bọn trẻ đang chơi trong vườn.*
- I grew up **in Can Tho**.<br>*Tôi lớn lên ở Cần Thơ.*
- Coffee is very popular **in Vietnam**.<br>*Cà phê rất phổ biến ở Việt Nam.*

### 2. on: surfaces, floors, streets

- Your phone is **on the table**.<br>*Điện thoại của bạn ở trên bàn.*
- There's a spider **on the ceiling**!<br>*Có con nhện trên trần nhà!*
- I live **on the fifth floor**.<br>*Tôi sống ở tầng năm.*
- There are many cafés **on this street**.<br>*Trên con phố này có nhiều quán cà phê.*

### 3. at: a point, an address, a place with an activity

- Turn left **at the traffic lights**.<br>*Đến đèn giao thông thì rẽ trái.*
- Someone is **at the door**.<br>*Có ai đó ở ngoài cửa.*
- My office is **at 10 Tran Hung Dao Street**.<br>*Văn phòng tôi ở số 10 phố Trần Hưng Đạo.*
- I saw your brother **at the supermarket**.<br>*Tôi gặp anh trai bạn ở siêu thị.*

### Fixed phrases to learn by heart

| Phrase | In Vietnamese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at home | ở nhà | I'm **at home** now. |
| at work | ở chỗ làm | My husband is still **at work**. |
| at school | ở trường | The kids are **at school** until 4. |
| in bed | trên giường (đang nằm) | He's still **in bed**. |
| in the picture | trong ảnh | Who's that man **in the picture**? |
| on TV | trên tivi | What's **on TV** tonight? |
| on the phone | đang nói điện thoại | Sorry, I was **on the phone**. |
| on the left / right | bên trái / phải | The toilet is **on the left**. |

## Prepositions with transport

| Transport | Preposition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Small vehicles you can only sit in: car, taxi | in | We went to the airport **in a taxi**. |
| Large vehicles you can stand or walk in: bus, train, plane, ship | on | I met my old teacher **on the bus**. |
| Vehicles you sit on top of: bike, motorbike | on | He goes to work **on his motorbike**. |
| How you travel, in general | by (no article) | I go to work **by bus**. |
| Walking | on foot | My kids go to school **on foot**. |

- Get **in** the car. We're late!<br>*Lên xe đi. Mình muộn rồi!*
- Don't eat **on the train**.<br>*Đừng ăn trên tàu.*
- She was **on the plane** for twelve hours.<br>*Cô ấy ngồi trên máy bay suốt mười hai tiếng.*
- How do you get to work? – **By motorbike**.<br>*Bạn đi làm bằng gì? – Bằng xe máy.*

::: tip TIP
If you climb inside and can only sit, not stand → **in** (*get in / get out of* a car). If you step onto it and can stand, or you sit on top of it → **on** (*get on / get off* a bus).
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I was born on 1995. | I was born **in** 1995. | Years take *in*. |
| See you in Monday. | See you **on** Monday. | Days of the week take *on*. |
| The meeting is in 3 pm. | The meeting is **at** 3 pm. | Clock times take *at*. |
| I'll visit you on next week. | I'll visit you **next week**. | No preposition before *next*. |
| She is in the bus. | She is **on** the bus. | A bus is a large vehicle, so use *on*. |
| I go to work by my car. | I go to work **by car**. / I go to work **in my car**. | *by* + transport, with no *my* or *the*. |
:::

## In context

> **Hà:** Are you free **on Saturday**? \
> **Long:** Not **on Saturday morning**. I work **at** the shop until noon. Why? \
> **Hà:** There's a music show **in** the park **at** 7 pm. \
> **Long:** Great! How do we get there? \
> **Hà:** Let's go **by** bus. The bus stop is **on** Le Loi Street. \
> **Long:** OK. I'll meet you **at** the bus stop **at** 6:30. See you **this** Saturday!

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Hà:** Thứ Bảy cậu rảnh không?<br>
**Long:** Sáng thứ Bảy thì không. Mình làm ở cửa hàng đến trưa. Sao thế?<br>
**Hà:** Có buổi biểu diễn ca nhạc ở công viên lúc 7 giờ tối.<br>
**Long:** Tuyệt! Mình đến đó bằng gì?<br>
**Hà:** Đi xe buýt đi. Bến xe buýt ở đường Lê Lợi.<br>
**Long:** Ừ. Mình gặp cậu ở bến xe buýt lúc 6 rưỡi nhé. Hẹn thứ Bảy này!
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- Time: **at** + clock time, **on** + day/date, **in** + part of the day/month/year/season.
- Exceptions: **on** Monday morning, **at** night, **no** preposition before next / last / this / every.
- Place: **in** inside, cities; **on** surfaces, floors, streets; **at** a point, a house number.
- Transport: **in** a car / taxi, **on** a bus / train / plane / bike, **by** bus, **on** foot.
:::
