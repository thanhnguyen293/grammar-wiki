# Articles: a / an / the

**a / an / the** are small words that go before a noun. Vietnamese has no words like these, so Vietnamese learners often leave them out or use too many. The basic rule: **a / an** for something not yet specific, **the** for something both the speaker and the listener know.

## Overview

::: info FORMULA
| Article | Use it when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| a | Singular countable noun that starts with a **consonant sound** | I have **a** dog. |
| an | Singular countable noun that starts with a **vowel sound** | She eats **an** apple every day. |
| the | The listener knows exactly which one, or there is only one | Please close **the** door. |
| (no article) | Talking in general, with plural or uncountable nouns | I like **cats**. **Water** is important. |
:::

## a or an: listen to the sound, don't look at the letter

Choose **a** or **an** by the **first sound you say**, not the first letter you write.

| Word | First sound | Article | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| book, car, house | consonant /b/, /k/, /h/ | a | I want to buy **a house**. |
| apple, egg, orange | vowel | an | I eat **an egg** for breakfast. |
| umbrella | vowel /ʌ/ | an | Take **an umbrella**. It's raining. |
| university | /juː/ (sounds like "you") | a | She studies at **a university** in Hanoi. |
| uniform | /juː/ | a | Students wear **a uniform**. |
| one-way ticket | /w/ (sounds like "won") | a | I need **a one-way ticket** to Hue. |
| hour | silent h, sounds like /aʊ/ | an | I waited for **an hour**. |
| honest | silent h, sounds like /ɒ/ | an | He is **an honest** man. |
| MC | /em/ | an | My sister is **an MC** at weddings. |

::: tip TIP
Say the word out loud. If the first sound is a **vowel** (as in *apple, egg, hour*), use **an**; otherwise use **a**. Careful: *university, uniform* start with the sound /j/ (like a quick "y"), which is a consonant sound, so they take **a**. Words where the **h** is pronounced (*house, hotel*) also take **a**.
:::

## How to use

### 1. a / an: any one, or the first time you mention it

- I have **a** brother and **a** sister.<br>*Tôi có một anh trai và một em gái.*
- Is there **a** bank near here?<br>*Gần đây có ngân hàng nào không?*
- I need **an** umbrella.<br>*Tôi cần một cái ô.*
- We saw **a** dog in the park.<br>*Chúng tôi thấy một con chó trong công viên.*

### 2. a / an: talking about jobs

Vietnamese says *"Cô ấy là bác sĩ"*, with no word for "a". English **must** have a / an.

- She is **a** doctor.<br>*Cô ấy là bác sĩ.*
- My father is **an** engineer.<br>*Bố tôi là kỹ sư.*
- I want to be **a** teacher.<br>*Tôi muốn làm giáo viên.*

### 3. the: something already mentioned, or something both people know

- I bought a shirt and a hat. **The** shirt is blue.<br>*Tôi mua một cái áo và một cái mũ. Cái áo màu xanh.*
- Can you close **the** door, please?<br>*Bạn đóng cửa giúp tôi được không?* (the door in this room)
- Where is **the** remote?<br>*Cái điều khiển đâu rồi?* (the remote in our home)
- **The** food at this restaurant is great.<br>*Đồ ăn ở nhà hàng này ngon lắm.*

### 4. the: something there is only one of

- **The** sun is very hot today.<br>*Hôm nay mặt trời gắt quá.*
- Look at **the** moon!<br>*Nhìn mặt trăng kìa!*
- She is **the** best student in the class.<br>*Cô ấy là học sinh giỏi nhất lớp.*
- He was **the** first person to arrive.<br>*Anh ấy là người đến đầu tiên.*

### 5. No article: talking in general

When you talk about **a whole type of thing** (plural or uncountable), don't use **the**.

- I love **cats**.<br>*Tôi yêu mèo.* (cats in general)
- **Coffee** keeps me awake.<br>*Cà phê làm tôi tỉnh táo.*
- **Money** isn't everything.<br>*Tiền không phải là tất cả.*
- **Children** need a lot of sleep.<br>*Trẻ con cần ngủ nhiều.*

Compare:

- I like **coffee**.<br>*Tôi thích cà phê.* (coffee in general)
- **The coffee** in this shop is very good.<br>*Cà phê ở quán này rất ngon.* (the specific coffee here)

## When not to use the

| Case | Correct example | Don't say |
|---|---|---|
| Meals | We have **dinner** at 7. | ✗ have the dinner |
| School subjects | I'm not good at **math**. | ✗ the math |
| Sports | My son plays **football** after school. | ✗ play the football |
| Languages | She speaks **Japanese**. | ✗ the Japanese |
| go to school / work / bed | I go to **bed** at 11. | ✗ go to the bed |
| at home / go home | I'm at **home** now. | ✗ at the home |
| Names of people, cities, most countries | She lives in **Hanoi**, **Vietnam**. | ✗ the Vietnam |
| by + transport | I go to work **by bus**. | ✗ by the bus |

- My kids go to **school** at 7.<br>*Bọn trẻ nhà tôi đi học lúc 7 giờ.*
- What time do you finish **work**?<br>*Mấy giờ bạn tan làm?*
- We had **lunch** at a small restaurant.<br>*Chúng tôi ăn trưa ở một nhà hàng nhỏ.*

::: tip TIP
- **go to school** = go to study (as a student). **go to the school** = go to that school building for another reason: *My mom went to **the school** to meet my teacher.* (Mẹ tôi đến trường gặp cô giáo.)
- With **musical instruments**, use **the**: *She plays **the** piano.* With **sports**, don't: *She plays tennis.*
:::

## the with names

| Group | Example names | Example sentence |
|---|---|---|
| Countries with States, Kingdom, Republic | the USA, the UK, the Czech Republic | My uncle lives in **the USA**. |
| Countries with plural names | the Philippines, the Netherlands | We went to **the Philippines** last year. |
| Rivers | the Red River, the Mekong | Hanoi is on **the Red River**. |
| Seas and oceans | the Pacific Ocean, the Mediterranean | **The Pacific** is the biggest ocean. |
| Mountain ranges | the Alps, the Himalayas | **The Himalayas** are very high. |
| No the: most country names | Vietnam, Japan, France | I'd love to visit **Japan**. |
| No the: lakes, single mountains | Hoan Kiem Lake, Mount Fansipan | We climbed **Mount Fansipan**. |

::: warning IMPORTANT
**the USA** and **the UK** always take **the**, but **Vietnam**, **America** and **England** don't.

I live in **Vietnam**. My cousin lives in **the UK**.
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She is doctor. | She is **a** doctor. | Jobs need a / an. |
| I waited for a hour. | I waited for **an** hour. | The *h* in *hour* is silent, so the first sound is a vowel. |
| He studies at an university. | He studies at **a** university. | *university* starts with /juː/, a consonant sound. |
| The life is beautiful. | **Life** is beautiful. | Don't use *the* when talking in general. |
| I play the football every Sunday. | I play **football** every Sunday. | Sports take no *the*. |
| My uncle lives in USA. | My uncle lives in **the USA**. | Country names with *States* need *the*. |
:::

## In context

> **Nga:** I have **a** new job! I'm **an** English teacher at **a** university in Da Nang. \
> **Bình:** Congratulations! Is **the** university near **the** sea? \
> **Nga:** Yes, it's ten minutes from **the** beach. I go swimming after **work**. \
> **Bình:** Lucky you! What time do you start? \
> **Nga:** At 8. I have **breakfast** at **home** and go to **work** by motorbike. \
> **Bình:** And what are **the** students like? \
> **Nga:** They're great. They love **music** and **football**, just like me.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Nga:** Mình có việc mới rồi! Mình dạy tiếng Anh ở một trường đại học ở Đà Nẵng.<br>
**Bình:** Chúc mừng cậu! Trường có gần biển không?<br>
**Nga:** Có, cách bãi biển mười phút. Tan làm là mình đi bơi.<br>
**Bình:** Sướng thế! Cậu bắt đầu làm lúc mấy giờ?<br>
**Nga:** Lúc 8 giờ. Mình ăn sáng ở nhà rồi đi xe máy đến chỗ làm.<br>
**Bình:** Thế sinh viên thế nào?<br>
**Nga:** Các bạn ấy tuyệt lắm. Họ mê âm nhạc và bóng đá, giống hệt mình.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **a / an** go with **singular** countable nouns; choose by the **first sound**: *a university*, *an hour*.
- Use **the** when the listener knows exactly which one, or when there is only one: *the door*, *the sun*.
- **Don't** use the when talking in general: *I like coffee.*
- No the with meals, school subjects, sports, *go to school / bed / work*; but use the with **the USA, the UK**, rivers and seas.
:::
