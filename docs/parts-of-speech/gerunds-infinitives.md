# V-ing and to V

When two verbs come together, the second verb is either **V-ing** (*cooking*) or **to V** (*to cook*), depending on the first verb. There is no single rule for all verbs, so the best way to learn is to remember them in groups and with example sentences.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Verb + V-ing | S + V1 + V-ing | I **enjoy cooking**.<br>*Tôi thích nấu ăn.* |
| Verb + to V | S + V1 + to V | I **want to cook** dinner tonight.<br>*Tối nay tôi muốn nấu bữa tối.* |
| Preposition + V-ing | preposition + V-ing | She is good **at cooking**.<br>*Cô ấy nấu ăn giỏi.* |
:::

## How to use

### 1. Verb + V-ing

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| enjoy | like doing | We **enjoy walking** in the park.<br>*Chúng tôi thích đi dạo trong công viên.* |
| finish | complete | I **finished cleaning** the kitchen.<br>*Tôi đã lau dọn xong nhà bếp.* |
| mind | be bothered by | Do you **mind waiting** a few minutes?<br>*Bạn có phiền chờ vài phút không?* |
| avoid | stay away from | I **avoid driving** in the rush hour.<br>*Tôi tránh lái xe vào giờ cao điểm.* |
| keep | continue, not stop | The baby **kept crying** all night.<br>*Em bé cứ khóc suốt đêm.* |
| practice | practise | She **practices speaking** English every day.<br>*Cô ấy luyện nói tiếng Anh mỗi ngày.* |
| miss | feel sad you no longer do | I **miss eating** my mom's food.<br>*Tôi nhớ món mẹ nấu.* |
| suggest | give an idea | He **suggested going** to the beach.<br>*Anh ấy gợi ý đi biển.* |

- My father **enjoys reading** the newspaper in the morning.<br>*Bố tôi thích đọc báo vào buổi sáng.*
- Have you **finished doing** your homework?<br>*Con làm xong bài tập về nhà chưa?*
- Please **keep trying**. You're doing well.<br>*Cứ tiếp tục cố gắng nhé. Bạn đang làm tốt lắm.*
- I don't **mind sharing** a room with my sister.<br>*Tôi không ngại ở chung phòng với em gái.*

### 2. Verb + to V

| Verb | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| want | wish to have or do | I **want to buy** a new bike.<br>*Tôi muốn mua một chiếc xe đạp mới.* |
| need | must have or do | You **need to rest**.<br>*Bạn cần nghỉ ngơi.* |
| decide | make a choice | They **decided to move** to Da Nang.<br>*Họ quyết định chuyển vào Đà Nẵng.* |
| hope | want something to happen | I **hope to see** you soon.<br>*Mong sớm gặp lại bạn.* |
| plan | intend | We **plan to visit** Japan next year.<br>*Năm sau chúng tôi định đi Nhật.* |
| learn | learn how to do | My son is **learning to swim**.<br>*Con trai tôi đang học bơi.* |
| promise | say you will surely do | He **promised to call** me.<br>*Anh ấy hứa sẽ gọi cho tôi.* |
| would like | want (polite) | I'**d like to order** now, please.<br>*Tôi muốn gọi món bây giờ.* |

- She **wants to become** a nurse.<br>*Cô ấy muốn trở thành y tá.*
- We **decided to stay** at home because of the rain.<br>*Chúng tôi quyết định ở nhà vì trời mưa.*
- I **need to buy** some milk on the way home.<br>*Tôi cần mua ít sữa trên đường về.*
- My parents **agreed to help** us with the wedding.<br>*Bố mẹ tôi đồng ý giúp chúng tôi lo đám cưới.*

### 3. Both forms, same meaning

The verbs *like, love, hate, start, begin, continue* can take V-ing or to V, and the meaning is almost the same.

- I **like swimming**. = I **like to swim**.<br>*Tôi thích bơi.*
- It **started raining**. = It **started to rain**.<br>*Trời bắt đầu mưa.*
- She **began learning** English last year. = She **began to learn** English last year.<br>*Cô ấy bắt đầu học tiếng Anh từ năm ngoái.*
- My grandfather **hates waiting**. = My grandfather **hates to wait**.<br>*Ông tôi ghét phải chờ đợi.*

### 4. Both forms, different meanings

With the 5 verbs below, changing V-ing to to V **changes the meaning**. Compare each pair of sentences.

#### remember

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| remember + V-ing | remember that you **already** did it | I **remember locking** the door.<br>*Tôi nhớ là mình đã khóa cửa rồi.* |
| remember + to V | remember that you **need** to do it (not done yet) | **Remember to lock** the door when you go out.<br>*Nhớ khóa cửa khi ra ngoài nhé.* |

#### forget

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| forget + V-ing | forget something you **already** did (usually: will never forget) | I'll never **forget seeing** the sea for the first time.<br>*Tôi sẽ không bao giờ quên lần đầu nhìn thấy biển.* |
| forget + to V | forget to do something you needed to do | I **forgot to buy** milk.<br>*Tôi quên mua sữa.* |

#### stop

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| stop + V-ing | **quit**, no longer do it | He **stopped smoking** last year.<br>*Năm ngoái anh ấy đã bỏ thuốc lá.* |
| stop + to V | stop **in order to** do something else | On the way home, he **stopped to buy** some bread.<br>*Trên đường về, anh ấy dừng lại để mua bánh mì.* |

#### try

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| try + V-ing | **test** something to see if it works | If you can't sleep, **try drinking** warm milk.<br>*Nếu không ngủ được, hãy thử uống sữa ấm.* |
| try + to V | **make an effort** to do (often something hard) | I **tried to open** the window, but it was stuck.<br>*Tôi cố mở cửa sổ nhưng nó bị kẹt.* |

#### regret

| Form | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| regret + V-ing | feel sorry that you **did** something | I **regret not studying** harder at school.<br>*Tôi tiếc vì hồi đi học đã không chăm chỉ hơn.* |
| regret + to V | be sorry that you **have to** tell (bad news) | We **regret to tell** you that your flight is cancelled.<br>*Chúng tôi rất tiếc phải báo rằng chuyến bay của quý khách đã bị hủy.* |

::: tip TIP
With *remember, forget, regret*: **V-ing looks back** (something already done), **to V looks forward** (something not done yet or about to happen).
:::

### 5. V-ing after prepositions

After a preposition (*at, in, of, for, about, without*...), the verb is always in the **V-ing** form.

- She is good **at cooking**.<br>*Cô ấy nấu ăn giỏi.*
- I'm interested **in learning** Japanese.<br>*Tôi thích học tiếng Nhật.*
- Thank you **for helping** me.<br>*Cảm ơn bạn đã giúp tôi.*
- He left **without saying** goodbye.<br>*Anh ấy đi mà không chào tạm biệt.*
- How **about going** to the cinema tonight?<br>*Tối nay đi xem phim nhé?*

::: warning IMPORTANT
In *look forward to*, *be used to*, *get used to*, **to is a preposition**, so it is followed by **V-ing**:

- I'm **looking forward to seeing** you.<br>*Tôi rất mong được gặp bạn.*
- We're **looking forward to visiting** Hoi An.<br>*Chúng tôi rất mong được đến Hội An.*
- I'm **used to getting up** early.<br>*Tôi quen dậy sớm rồi.*
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| I enjoy to cook. | I enjoy **cooking**. | *enjoy* + V-ing. |
| I want going home. | I want **to go** home. | *want* + to V. |
| I look forward to see you. | I look forward to **seeing** you. | *to* here is a preposition. |
| She is good at cook. | She is good at **cooking**. | Use V-ing after a preposition. |
| Remember locking the door when you leave. | **Remember to lock** the door when you leave. | Not done yet → to V. |
:::

## In context

> **Linh:** Do you **enjoy living** in the city? \
> **Hung:** Yes, but I **miss swimming** in the river near my village. \
> **Linh:** Then you should **try going** to the pool near my house. \
> **Hung:** Good idea. I **want to start** exercising again. \
> **Linh:** Great! **Remember to bring** a towel. I'm **looking forward to seeing** you there! \
> **Hung:** Don't worry. I won't **forget to bring** it.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Linh:** Anh có thích sống ở thành phố không?<br>
**Hung:** Có, nhưng anh nhớ những lần bơi ở con sông gần làng.<br>
**Linh:** Vậy anh thử đến bể bơi gần nhà em xem.<br>
**Hung:** Ý hay đấy. Anh muốn bắt đầu tập thể dục lại.<br>
**Linh:** Tuyệt! Nhớ mang khăn tắm nhé. Em mong gặp anh ở đó!<br>
**Hung:** Đừng lo. Anh sẽ không quên mang đâu.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, keep** + V-ing; **want, need, decide, hope, plan** + to V.
- **like, love, start, begin** can take both, with the same meaning.
- **remember, forget, stop, try, regret**: V-ing and to V have **different meanings**.
- After a **preposition**, always use V-ing, including *look forward to*.
:::
