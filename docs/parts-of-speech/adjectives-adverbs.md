# Adjectives and adverbs

**Adjectives** describe people and things (*a **careful** driver*). **Adverbs** describe actions (*drive **carefully***). Vietnamese uses the same word, "cẩn thận", for both, but English changes the form, so this is where Vietnamese learners often get confused.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Adjective before a noun | adjective + noun | a **fast** car |
| Adjective after be / a linking verb | be, look, feel… + adjective | She looks **happy**. |
| Adverb modifying a verb | V (+ object) + adverb | He drives **carefully**. |
| Adverb modifying an adjective | adverb + adjective | **really** cheap |
:::

## Adjectives vs. adverbs

| Point | Adjective | Adverb |
|---|---|---|
| Answers the question | **What** is the person or thing **like**? | **How** is it done? To what degree? |
| Modifies | Nouns | Verbs, adjectives, other adverbs |
| Form | quick, careful, quiet | quick**ly**, careful**ly**, quiet**ly** |
| Example | She is a **quick** learner. | She learns **quickly**. |
| Example | He is a **good** cook. | He cooks **well**. |
| Example | The baby is **quiet**. | The baby sleeps **quietly**. |

## How to use

### 1. Adjectives: describe people and things

- My grandma has a **big** garden.<br>*Bà tôi có một khu vườn rộng.*
- She is a **careful** driver.<br>*Cô ấy là người lái xe cẩn thận.*
- This street is very **noisy**.<br>*Con phố này rất ồn.*
- The test was **easy**.<br>*Bài kiểm tra dễ.*

### 2. Adverbs: describe actions

- She drives **carefully**.<br>*Cô ấy lái xe cẩn thận.*
- Please speak **slowly**. I'm still learning.<br>*Làm ơn nói chậm thôi. Tôi vẫn đang học.*
- The children are playing **happily** in the garden.<br>*Bọn trẻ đang chơi vui vẻ trong vườn.*
- He passed the test **easily**.<br>*Anh ấy qua bài kiểm tra dễ dàng.*

### 3. Adverbs: modify an adjective or another adverb

- The hotel was **really** nice.<br>*Khách sạn thật sự đẹp.*
- This bag is **too** expensive.<br>*Cái túi này đắt quá.*
- She speaks English **very** well.<br>*Cô ấy nói tiếng Anh rất giỏi.*
- I'm **quite** busy this week.<br>*Tuần này tôi khá bận.*

::: tip TIP
For comparatives and superlatives (bigger, the biggest, more carefully…), see the lesson [Comparisons](./comparisons).
:::

## How to make adverbs from adjectives

| Rule | Adjective → Adverb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Add -ly | slow → slow**ly**, careful → careful**ly**, quiet → quiet**ly** | Please close the door **quietly**. |
| Consonant + y → -ily | easy → eas**ily**, happy → happ**ily**, angry → angr**ily** | She answered **angrily**. |
| -le → -ly | gentle → gent**ly**, simple → simp**ly**, terrible → terrib**ly** | Hold the baby **gently**. |
| Irregular | good → **well** | She is a good singer. She sings **well**. |
| No change | fast → **fast** | He runs very **fast**. |
| No change | hard → **hard** | My parents work **hard**. |
| No change | late → **late** | The bus came **late** again. |
| No change | early → **early** | I get up **early** on weekdays. |

::: warning IMPORTANT
**good** is an adjective; **well** is an adverb. To describe how someone does something, use **well**.

She is a **good** dancer. → She dances **well**. (not *She dances good.*)
:::

## Linking verbs + adjectives

Some verbs don't show an action; they show a **state or a feeling**. After them, use an **adjective**, not an adverb.

| Verb | In Vietnamese | Example |
|---|---|---|
| be | thì, là | The room **is clean**. |
| look | trông có vẻ | You **look tired**. |
| feel | cảm thấy | I **feel sick** today. |
| taste | có vị | This soup **tastes good**. |
| sound | nghe có vẻ | That **sounds great**! |
| smell | có mùi | The flowers **smell nice**. |
| seem | dường như | He **seems happy** with his new job. |
| become | trở nên | She **became famous** after the film. |
| get | trở nên | It's **getting cold**. |

- The milk **smells bad**. Don't drink it.<br>*Sữa có mùi hôi rồi. Đừng uống.*
- Your idea **sounds interesting**.<br>*Ý tưởng của bạn nghe hay đấy.*
- My mother **looks young** for her age.<br>*Mẹ tôi trông trẻ so với tuổi.*

::: tip TIP
**look** meaning *seem* goes with an adjective: *She **looks angry**.* (Cô ấy trông có vẻ giận.)<br>
**look at** is the action of *looking*, so it goes with an adverb: *She **looked at** me **angrily**.* (Cô ấy nhìn tôi giận dữ.)
:::

## Common traps

| Word | Part of speech, meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| hard | with a lot of effort (adjective and adverb) | He works **hard**. |
| hardly | almost not | He **hardly** works. (Anh ta hầu như chẳng làm gì.) |
| late | not on time (adjective and adverb) | I arrived **late**. |
| lately | recently | I haven't seen him **lately**. |
| near | close to | She lives **near** the school. |
| nearly | almost | I **nearly** missed the bus. |
| friendly | kind and pleasant (**adjective**) | The staff are very **friendly**. |
| lovely | beautiful, nice (**adjective**) | What a **lovely** day! |
| lonely | sad because you are alone (**adjective**) | He feels **lonely** in the big city. |
| well | healthy (when it is an **adjective**) | I'm not **well** today. |

::: tip TIP
**friendly, lovely, lonely, ugly, silly** end in -ly but are **adjectives**, and they have no adverb form. To describe an action, use **in a friendly way**: *She smiled at me **in a friendly way**.* (Cô ấy cười với tôi rất thân thiện.)
:::

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| She sings beautiful. | She sings **beautifully**. | To describe how she sings (a verb), you need an adverb. |
| He speaks English very good. | He speaks English very **well**. | The adverb of *good* is *well*. |
| The soup tastes deliciously. | The soup tastes **delicious**. | After the linking verb *taste*, use an adjective. |
| He works hardly every day. | He works **hard** every day. | *hardly* means "almost not". |
| She talked to me friendly. | She talked to me **in a friendly way**. | *friendly* is an adjective; it can't modify a verb. |
:::

## In context

> **Lan:** You look **tired**, Minh. Are you OK? \
> **Minh:** I worked **hard** all week, and I **hardly** slept last night. \
> **Lan:** Poor you. Here, try this soup. It tastes **good**. \
> **Minh:** Mmm, it's **delicious**! You cook really **well**. \
> **Lan:** Thanks! Eat it **slowly**. It's very **hot**. \
> **Minh:** I feel **better** already. You're so **kind**.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Lan:** Trông anh mệt quá, Minh. Anh ổn không?<br>
**Minh:** Cả tuần anh làm việc vất vả, tối qua lại gần như không ngủ được.<br>
**Lan:** Tội anh quá. Đây, anh ăn thử bát súp này. Ngon lắm.<br>
**Minh:** Ừm, ngon thật! Em nấu ăn giỏi ghê.<br>
**Lan:** Cảm ơn anh! Ăn từ từ thôi, còn nóng lắm.<br>
**Minh:** Anh thấy đỡ hơn rồi. Em tốt bụng quá.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **Adjectives** describe nouns (*a **careful** driver*); **adverbs** describe verbs (*drive **carefully***).
- To make adverbs: add **-ly**, y → **-ily**; **good → well**; **fast, hard, late, early** don't change.
- After **be, look, feel, taste, sound, smell, seem, become**, use an **adjective**.
- Be careful: **hardly** = almost not, **lately** = recently, **friendly** is an adjective.
:::
