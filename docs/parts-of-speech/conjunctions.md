# Conjunctions

Conjunctions are words that **join** two words, two phrases or two sentences: *and, but, because, although, when*... With conjunctions you can make longer sentences and show how ideas connect: adding, contrast, reason or time.

## Formula

::: info FORMULA
| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Joining two equal sentences | Sentence 1 + , + and/but/so... + sentence 2 | I was tired, **so** I went to bed.<br>*Tôi mệt nên đi ngủ.* |
| Dependent clause after | Main clause + because/when... + dependent clause | I stayed home **because** it was raining.<br>*Tôi ở nhà vì trời mưa.* |
| Dependent clause first | Because/When... + dependent clause + , + main clause | **Because** it was raining, I stayed home.<br>*Vì trời mưa nên tôi ở nhà.* |
:::

## How to use

### 1. The seven FANBOYS conjunctions

FANBOYS is made from the first letters of 7 conjunctions that join two **equal** parts.

| Conjunction | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **F**or | because (formal) | She went to bed early, **for** she was very tired.<br>*Cô ấy đi ngủ sớm, vì cô ấy rất mệt.* |
| **A**nd | adds information | I bought some eggs **and** some bread.<br>*Tôi mua ít trứng và ít bánh mì.* |
| **N**or | and also not | He doesn't eat meat, **nor** does he drink milk.<br>*Anh ấy không ăn thịt, cũng không uống sữa.* |
| **B**ut | shows contrast | The food was cheap **but** delicious.<br>*Đồ ăn rẻ nhưng ngon.* |
| **O**r | gives a choice | Would you like tea **or** coffee?<br>*Bạn uống trà hay cà phê?* |
| **Y**et | but (surprising) | It was raining, **yet** they went to the beach.<br>*Trời mưa thế mà họ vẫn ra biển.* |
| **S**o | shows a result | It was late, **so** we took a taxi.<br>*Muộn rồi nên chúng tôi đi taxi.* |

- My sister likes cats, **but** I prefer dogs.<br>*Chị tôi thích mèo, nhưng tôi thích chó hơn.*
- We can go by bus **or** by train.<br>*Chúng ta có thể đi xe buýt hoặc tàu hỏa.*
- I missed the bus, **so** I was late for work.<br>*Tôi lỡ xe buýt nên đi làm muộn.*
- Minh cooked dinner, **and** I washed the dishes.<br>*Minh nấu bữa tối, còn tôi rửa bát.*

::: tip TIP
In everyday English, the most common ones are **and, but, or, so**. *for, nor, yet* sound more formal and are found more in writing.
:::

### 2. Time: when, while, before, after, until, as soon as

- **When** I was a child, I lived in Hue.<br>*Hồi nhỏ tôi sống ở Huế.*
- I always brush my teeth **before** I go to bed.<br>*Tôi luôn đánh răng trước khi đi ngủ.*
- Please wait here **until** I come back.<br>*Hãy đợi ở đây cho đến khi tôi quay lại.*
- Call me **as soon as** you arrive.<br>*Đến nơi thì gọi ngay cho tôi nhé.*
- She was cooking **while** I was cleaning the house.<br>*Cô ấy nấu ăn trong khi tôi dọn nhà.*

::: tip TIP
After *when, before, after, until, as soon as*, use the **present simple** to talk about the future, not *will*: Call me when you **arrive**. (not *when you will arrive*)
:::

### 3. Reason: because, since, as

- I didn't go to work **because** I was sick.<br>*Tôi không đi làm vì bị ốm.*
- **Since** it's raining, let's stay home.<br>*Vì trời đang mưa, mình ở nhà đi.*
- **As** I was late, I took a taxi.<br>*Vì muộn nên tôi đi taxi.*
- The flight was delayed **because of** the storm.<br>*Chuyến bay bị hoãn vì cơn bão.*

::: tip TIP
**because** + clause (with a subject and a verb): *because it rained*. **because of** + noun: *because of the rain*.
:::

### 4. Contrast: although, though, even though, while

- **Although** it was cold, we went swimming.<br>*Mặc dù trời lạnh, chúng tôi vẫn đi bơi.*
- He passed the exam **even though** he didn't study much.<br>*Anh ấy thi đỗ dù không học nhiều.*
- **Though** the hotel was small, it was very clean.<br>*Khách sạn tuy nhỏ nhưng rất sạch.*
- My husband likes coffee, **while** I prefer tea.<br>*Chồng tôi thích cà phê, còn tôi thích trà hơn.*

### 5. Condition: if, unless, as long as

- **If** it rains, we'll stay at home.<br>*Nếu trời mưa, chúng ta sẽ ở nhà.*
- You'll miss the bus **unless** you hurry.<br>*Nếu không nhanh lên, bạn sẽ lỡ xe buýt.*
- You can borrow my car **as long as** you drive carefully.<br>*Bạn có thể mượn xe tôi miễn là lái cẩn thận.*
- **If** you need help, call me.<br>*Nếu cần giúp thì gọi tôi.*

### 6. Paired conjunctions

| Pair | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| both... and | the two together | **Both** my father **and** my mother are teachers.<br>*Cả bố và mẹ tôi đều là giáo viên.* |
| either... or | one or the other | You can **either** call me **or** send me a message.<br>*Bạn có thể gọi điện hoặc nhắn tin cho tôi.* |
| neither... nor | not this and not that | **Neither** Lan **nor** Mai likes durian.<br>*Cả Lan lẫn Mai đều không thích sầu riêng.* |
| not only... but also | this and also that | She is **not only** smart **but also** very kind.<br>*Cô ấy không những thông minh mà còn rất tốt bụng.* |

- I can speak **both** English **and** French.<br>*Tôi nói được cả tiếng Anh và tiếng Pháp.*
- We can **either** eat out **or** cook at home.<br>*Mình có thể ăn ngoài hoặc nấu ở nhà.*
- This phone is **neither** cheap **nor** good.<br>*Điện thoại này chẳng rẻ mà cũng chẳng tốt.*

## Don't use although with but

::: warning IMPORTANT
Vietnamese says "**Mặc dù**... **nhưng**..." (although... but) and "**Vì**... **nên**..." (because... so). English uses only **one** of the two words, never the whole pair.
:::

- ✗ Although it was late, but he kept working.
- ✓ **Although** it was late, he kept working.<br>*Mặc dù đã muộn, anh ấy vẫn tiếp tục làm việc.*
- ✓ It was late, **but** he kept working.<br>*Đã muộn rồi, nhưng anh ấy vẫn tiếp tục làm việc.*
- ✗ Because I was tired, so I went to bed early.
- ✓ **Because** I was tired, I went to bed early.<br>*Vì mệt nên tôi đi ngủ sớm.*
- ✓ I was tired, **so** I went to bed early.<br>*Tôi mệt nên đi ngủ sớm.*

## Commas with so, but and dependent clauses

| Case | Comma | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *so / but* joins two full sentences | put it **before** *so / but* | It was raining, **so** we stayed home.<br>*Trời mưa nên chúng tôi ở nhà.* |
| *but* joins two short words | not needed | The room is small **but** clean.<br>*Phòng nhỏ nhưng sạch.* |
| A *because / although / when / if* clause comes **first** | put it **after** that clause | **When** I got home, my son was asleep.<br>*Khi tôi về đến nhà, con trai tôi đã ngủ.* |
| A *because / although / when / if* clause comes **second** | usually not needed | My son was asleep **when** I got home.<br>*Con trai tôi đã ngủ khi tôi về đến nhà.* |

## Common mistakes

::: danger COMMON MISTAKES
| ✗ Wrong | ✓ Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Although it rained, but we went out. | **Although** it rained, we went out. | *although* and *but* don't go together. |
| Because I was hungry, so I ate. | **Because** I was hungry, I ate. | *because* and *so* don't go together. |
| I stayed home because of I was sick. | I stayed home **because** I was sick. | *because of* + noun; *because* + clause. |
| It was late so, we left. | It was late, **so** we left. | The comma goes **before** *so*. |
| I'll call you when I will arrive. | I'll call you when I **arrive**. | After *when*, use the present simple for the future. |
:::

## In context

> **Thao:** How was your trip to Da Lat? \
> **Khoa:** Great! **Although** it rained a lot, we visited many places. \
> **Thao:** Did you stay in a hotel **or** a homestay? \
> **Khoa:** A homestay, **because** it was cheaper. It was small **but** very clean. \
> **Thao:** I want to go there **when** I have some free time. \
> **Khoa:** You should! The weather is cool, **and** the food is **both** cheap **and** delicious.

::: details Vietnamese translation
**Thao:** Chuyến đi Đà Lạt của cậu thế nào?<br>
**Khoa:** Tuyệt lắm! Mặc dù mưa nhiều nhưng bọn tớ vẫn đi được nhiều nơi.<br>
**Thao:** Cậu ở khách sạn hay homestay?<br>
**Khoa:** Homestay, vì rẻ hơn. Phòng nhỏ nhưng rất sạch.<br>
**Thao:** Khi nào rảnh tớ cũng muốn đi.<br>
**Khoa:** Nên đi đi! Thời tiết mát mẻ, đồ ăn vừa rẻ vừa ngon.
:::

## Remember

::: warning SUMMARY
- **and, but, or, so** join two equal parts; when they join two sentences, put a comma **before** the conjunction.
- When a *because / although / when / if* clause starts the sentence, put a comma **after** it.
- Use only **one**: *although* **or** *but*; *because* **or** *so*.
- **because** + clause, **because of** + noun.
:::
